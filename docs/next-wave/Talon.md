# Talon implementation and source audit

Talon has target-owned Wounds, separately traveling blade passes, two Q delivery branches and terrain-only vaulting. The kit reads gameplay quantities through `KitContext` at compilation. Combat callbacks use the captured values and actual contacted target; they do not read source records during a duel.

## Combat behavior

Wounds and bleed locks belong to the actual champion or explicitly Champion-class pet that was contacted. A minion, an unclassified summon or a merely Monster-class pet never acquires inferred champion/large-monster eligibility. Separate W and R passes each contribute one stack. Spell shields, untargetability, stasis and Riposte prevent new stacks. A connected ordinary basic refreshes incomplete stacks. At three stacks, that basic consumes the marks and attempts a separately spell-shieldable bleed. Blocking ordinary basic damage still triggers it; blind, dodge and parry preserve the marks and their prior expiry. The bleed snapshots its own AD, schedules sixteen absolute deadlines through tick 60, survives caster death and does not feed new Wounds. Actual pet status and scheduled-target identity remain separate from its owner.

Close Q performs its displacement alongside the actual attack-speed cast clock. Knockdown can stop the movement while the independent cast still completes. The shared cast machinery revalidates close-target range, visibility and targetability before spending its cost or cooldown. A valid completed cast delivers the guaranteed 150% spell packet, resets the basic timer and grants temporary next-basic range. Root and ground allow this close branch. Ranged Q uses the estimated sourced speed, tracks relocated targets with a 100-unit offset, and finishes at the last tracked endpoint when the target exceeds the estimated tracking cutoff. A knockdown denies its arrival hit. The ranged branch is blocked by root/ground and gives no reward merely because a target already died before arrival.

Q is a spell rather than a basic attack: it does not apply ordinary attack on-hits, and its close critical packet does not scale with ordinary basic critical chance. A Q kill grants the sourced healing through normal healing reduction and refunds half the actual remaining shared Q cooldown. Rewards use the resolved Q contact and actual victim, including a health pet. A blocked Q or a kill caused by W grants neither reward. This avoids depending on champion kill notifications, which do not carry the spell's damage attribution.

W launches from Talon's actual cast-end location in the declared direction. The outward flight, endpoint pause and owner-following return keep separate contact lists. Each pass resolves real champion/pet hitboxes, spell shields and projectile walls. Missing outward does not erase the return. Target or owner relocation can change which pass contacts; the returning blade follows the current owner, including after death. A wall removes the flight at its actual crossing and prevents its later return. The return ends without fabricated travel when its source-specified maximum owner distance is exceeded.

E requires a real active terrain interval. It chooses a valid nearby edge and clear far-side landing, enforces both selection and displacement limits, and records cooldown separately for each crossed interval. It neither spawns terrain nor grants an open-ground dash. Stasis pauses the same live vault and resumes its original endpoint; knockdown interrupts it. The reveal checks actual observation and landing proximity without ending an existing R stealth state. Ordinary production scenarios contain no synthetic terrain interval, so E does not gain free duel value from a general wall-availability flag.

R launches opposite axial projectiles, holds each surviving flight at its endpoint and later converges toward the current owner or the actual basic/Q target. Per-target, per-pass guards prevent the two axial representatives from multiplying damage or consuming two spell shields in the same pass. Basic windup completion and Q completion select the attacked target and end stealth; W or manual convergence returns to Talon. Every convergence respects the 0.8-second minimum. The manual recast has its independent one-second gate. Initial R and its instant recast can overlay an allowed dash while retaining an already-running close-Q cast clock. R preserves basic windup. Its post-effect cooldown begins on convergence, when its stealth and speed end. Released projectiles complete after death, with no invented early death-convergence rule.

The normal policy starts with common scenario bookkeeping, preserves completed-trade retreat, and reserves offensive R in hold and trade even when it is actually ready. Genuine reactive defense remains available. Fight/kite behavior may use R, Q and W subject to actual reach, resource and cast eligibility. The source-based budget estimates both blade passes and feasible three-contact bleed cycles, constrained by the bleed lock and basic attack speed. Actual duels resolve misses, defenses, resource exhaustion and terrain; no coefficient is fitted to intended matchup winners.

## Explicit limits

- The arena is one-dimensional. W uses its central axial blade instead of modeling the lateral fan. R's twelve radial blades use two opposite axial representatives, with one permitted hit per target per pass. The source fan angle, side range, blade count and widths remain evidence; no twelvefold damage or invented radial overlap is granted.
- The simulator has no large-monster subtype. Monster class alone does not imply the passive's large-monster eligibility. Lane waves, structures, allied takedowns and external item actives are absent. Ordinary unit-target Q behavior chooses the duel opponent; direct actual-pet contacts still retain their own source guards and rewards.
- Terrain intervals approximate real wall segments; the binary lockout width cannot reconstruct map topology. Walking toward a distant selected wall is left to normal movement, rather than manufacturing a vault. E samples current movement speed at declaration.
- Q's 1400 speed and 800 tracking cutoff are explicitly estimated in the primary. All timing uses 30 Hz deadlines. Close-Q invalid-target cancellation is shared with other unit-target casts; the combat ends when the sole opponent dies, so a later cosmetic close-Q completion cannot create an additional duel event.
- W's two scalar sidestep weights are evaluated independently. R's stealth/convergence clock starts at declaration. Missing lateral acceleration and unsourced server timing details remain assumptions. Already launched R blades retain their original convergence deadline after caster death.

## Validation

`tests/kits/talon.test.ts` passes **59 tests** in `/workspace/counter-browser/batch9-talon-resumed-second.json`. It includes the complete normal six-scenario, 15000-gold matchup against Darius in both orientations, exact reflected builds, finite outcomes and unchanged **1e-12** score/scenario symmetry. Normal traces cast Q, W and R; no synthetic wall permits E.

Focused tests cover all five primary revisions and exact binary attribution; fail-closed missing raw/source attribution; Wound expiry, refresh, guards, blocked basics, blind, independently shielded bleed, sixteen pulses, AD snapshots, cloned pending state and post-death damage; actual Champion-class, minion, unclassified and Monster-class pet identity; close/ranged Q timing, tracking, knockdown, root/ground and invalid targets; champion/pet Q kills and denied rewards; independent W passes, missed passes, walls, moving origins/owners and return limits; actual terrain selection, segment cooldowns, haste, stasis and knockdown; R's separate passes, co-location deduplication, recast gates, minimum lifetime, basic windup, held-cast overlays, outward/return wall destruction, source death, resource exhaustion and reflected co-location facing. The release-level matrix, broader reflection and browser evidence are recorded separately by the batch coordinator.
