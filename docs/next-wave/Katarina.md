## Combat behavior

Voracity begins with no preduel daggers. W creates an airborne dagger at the actual cast location; Q creates a dagger 1.023 seconds after its first contact, 350 units beyond the target's contact position along the throw direction. Each dagger has its own landing and four-second ground-expiry clocks. Airborne daggers can anchor E but cannot spin. Walking across a landed dagger's center picks it up once. Blinks only check their landing position and never sweep the intervening line. Each pickup damages actual enemies within the 340-unit spin radius and reduces the current E cooldown by the level-20 96% share of its hasted total. Spin packets apply on-hits only to champion-class targets, with contacts ordered nearest first. Dodge denies on-hits while preserving the spell damage; blind and attack-block states do not stop either. An actual enemy champion kill refunds fifteen seconds from Q/W/E/R, including a first-hit lethal packet or a posthumous projectile kill. Summon and clone kills grant no champion takedown refund, and no external assists are invented.

Q has a 0.25-second cast followed by a real homing projectile at 1600 units/second. Invalid targets at cast completion cancel without cooldown. The blade selects up to two additional currently visible unhit enemies within 450 units of the first contact, traveling for 0.15 seconds per bounce. Actual summons keep their identities, shields and resistances. Spell shields stop damage on their own target while allowing the bounce and dagger clocks to continue. A dead target does not automatically destroy flight; a naturally expired champion clone does. Projectile interception destroys that flight before its downstream dagger or bounce. Intended dagger points inside real terrain move to the nearest clear point.

W grants ghosting and its rank-five 90% movement bonus, linearly decaying until the dagger lands after 1.25 seconds. E immediately blinks to an actual dagger or valid unit anchor and damages the nearest enemy at the destination before its 0.15-second cast completes. Enemy champions have damage priority. E resets the attack timer unless its damage killed the target. Ordinary anchors have 725 range, daggers 775; airborne daggers and actual allied summons are valid anchors. Ground and root prevent the blink. E has full on-hits; dodge prevents only those on-hits, while blind and attack blocks do not prevent damage or on-hits. Each actual summon retains its own spell shield and damage rules.

R requires a real champion-class target within 500 units and can target invisible champions. The 2.5-second channel launches fifteen separate knives to up to three closest champion-class targets within 550 units. Ordinary summons are excluded. Knives travel at 2400 units/second, retain finite projected arrival/expiry clocks, and resolve physical, magic, 35% on-hit and three-second Grievous Wounds through one guarded contact packet. The physical component is `(0.16 + 0.5 bonus AS) × bonus AD`, the magic component `50 + 0.19 AP`. One spell shield consumes one complete knife packet. Death or interruption stops future launches; previously launched knives persist. Leaving all targets behind does not end the channel. Each launch reveals Katarina for 4.5 seconds. Explicit Q/W/E casts interrupt R. Voluntary movement and champion/pet attack orders may cancel only outside the first and last 0.25 seconds; disabling crowd control still interrupts during those windows. The ordinary behavior holds its channel and first builds Q/E/W opportunities. Trade and hold modes reserve R; the common short-trade clock starts before custom dagger selection, and a finished trade retreats even when a dagger refund or ordinary cooldown makes a spell available again.

## Explicit source and geometry limits

- The pinned primary and versioned game objects do not expose a verified dagger pickup trigger radius. Pickup conservatively requires reaching or crossing the exact dagger center on the arena line. The primary's 340 `effectRadius` and game's `DaggerRadius` describe spin damage, so neither is reused as pickup distance.
- Q describes a slightly random position about 350 units from first contact without a distribution. The model uses exactly 350 on the throw line. Multidirectional bounce paths, lateral dagger opportunities and local map-sight bubbles are absent. Q's next-game-tick template is retained as its normalized 1.023-second landing delay. Timings round cumulatively to 30 Hz ticks.
- E's 150 wrapper `castRadius` is interpreted as the cursor selection/landing offset; the actual E object's 225 `castRadius` is interpreted as its damage search. These are field interpretations, not verified script exports of those semantics. The primary's 775 dagger range takes precedence over the bin's conflicting 800. Wards, structures, neutral map units and ally champions are absent from the duel; actual minion/monster/summon/clone classes remain valid anchors. Post-E automatic attack-command bugs are outside the command API.
- W's decay curve is unspecified; a linear decay is explicit. R's first-knife offset is unspecified; launches occur at cumulative sixth-second boundaries, with the last at 2.5 seconds. Knives read current damage stats at impact. The arena has no item/rune on-attack system or single-use Spellblade inventory. It cannot recreate those item interactions or distinguish ordinary reveal from true sight.
- The reference budget values one spin per Q/W cooldown, ordinary E cadence and one full R per cooldown. It grants no guaranteed kill resets or extra E chains. No matchup score tuning is included.
