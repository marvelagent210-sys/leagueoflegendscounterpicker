## Runtime mechanics

**Moonsilver Blade:** Starts empty, with the wiki's level-20 extension: 37.3529411764706% innate bonus AS, tripled to 112.058823529412% for five seconds after an actual Q/W/E/R cast completes. Only the difference is temporarily added over the permanent innate; repeated casts refresh one buff. Connected, unblocked basic attacks build two five-second stacks, and the next basic consumes them. Its independently guarded 175-radius cleave centers on the actual basic recipient, including real summons, for `270 + 0.5 AP`. Explicit monster targets take 270% of that amount. Blind, dodge, parry and blocked basic damage preserve the stacks under the documented accepted-contact policy. The cleave's own spell shield is separate from physical basic damage. Structures, pre-fight farm and item phantom on-hits are outside the duel.

**Crescent Strike:** Costs 50 mana, casts for 0.25 seconds, and has a six-second cooldown and 900 targeting range. Two destructible finite flights release from Diana's cast-completion position. Each begins at the binary's forward offset 81.77568817138672 and uses its exact 70 missile-width value, local Hermite start/tangent coordinates and sourced 1900/2100 speed. Arc length is integrated over 128 segments; the forward coordinate is projected onto the duel axis. At a 900-unit aim with 100 AP, the projected inner/outer coordinates at tick 9 are approximately 92.537545553 and 86.296077598 after a tick-8 release; first contact with the stationary 65-radius champion is tick 22. Actual champion and pet contacts share one `210 + 0.7 AP` packet and three-second Moonlight per cast. A spell-shielded bolt may still take one separately guarded endpoint explosion; an older Moonlight mark does not falsely suppress that explosion. Walls clip flights at the exact traveled intersection after resolving earlier contacts, and destroyed flights create no endpoint blast. Released Q follows the shared persistent-artifact death policy. Moonlight records standard sight without revealing invisibility or camouflage.

**Pale Cascade:** Costs 60 mana and immediately grants `105 + 0.3 AP + 0.14 bonusHP` shield for up to five seconds. Three actual spheres become available. An actual eligible enemy hitbox entering the projected 200-radius contact envelope consumes them as three separately guarded area packets, each dealing `68 + 0.18 AP` around the contact. A pet can initiate the contact without counting its owner twice. A single spell shield blocks one orb packet; invulnerability/parry guards remain target-local. Projectile destruction consumes spheres as losses and prevents the second shield. If all three detonate, a full shield is reapplied in addition to the surviving first-shield amount, with a refreshed five-second duration. The nine-second, haste-adjusted cooldown starts when the spheres are consumed or expire; caster death ends remaining spheres. Hit-count props and untargetable objects are not invented contact targets.

**Lunar Rush:** Costs 60 mana, has a 14-second cooldown and validates actual target range at 825 center-to-center. It chooses the actual champion or an explicitly aimed eligible summon. It retains a real interruptible dash action and resolves at the earliest subsequent 30 Hz tick. Starting within 400 uses the target's far pathing boundary; longer dashes use the near boundary, with separate pet pathing radius when supplied. Root/ground gating, knockdown, anti-dash interception, intervening-terrain traversal and mid-dash spell overlay use the shared movement engine. Uninterrupted arrival delivers `130 + 0.6 AP`; ending untargetability is an explicit damage exception, while stasis and spell shield still guard the packet. The end hook consumes only this Diana's currently present Moonlight on all actual enemy bodies, even after interruption. Accepted marked arrival or a marked target dying during travel resets the current cooldown to a static 0.25 seconds, rounded to eight ticks. A spell-shielded arrival consumes marks without reset. Dead/removed pet targets preserve their real destination and marked-death reset instead of retargeting the champion.

**Moonfall:** Costs 100 mana, casts for 0.25 seconds and has an 80-second cooldown. The initial independently guarded packet contacts actual hitboxes within 475, pulls by at most 225 over approximately 0.5 seconds, applies a 60% two-second slow and records standard sight. The pull's origin is fixed at cast completion, so crossing the victim cannot reverse an already-declared displacement. The delayed beam follows Diana's current position. Accepted champion contacts and explicitly classified clone bodies count even through CC/displacement immunity; spell-shielded contacts do not. Ordinary summons do not arm or amplify the beam. After one second it delivers `400 + 0.6 AP + (count - 1) × (85 + 0.15 AP)` to actual eligible bodies within 475, with no artificial four-extra-champion cap. The delayed packet independently checks defenses and follows the shared persistent-artifact death policy.

## Source conflicts and explicit limits

* **E travel is unpublished.** The exact primary says "dashes" and "cast time = none"; the active binary gives generic `mCastTime:0.25`, `spellCastTime:0.25` and `missileSpeed:20.0`, with no dash-speed, travel-duration or overshoot-distance parameter. Primary cast time takes precedence. `Infinity` selects the engine's earliest finite one-tick dash phase; it is not a claimed measured speed. Collision-boundary overshoot is a documented geometric policy. This short window can differ materially from the real travel window, while preserving genuine dash counters and end events. No 2500-speed constant is invented.
* **W phase is unpublished.** The binary contains exact angular velocity 5.199999809265137, linear velocity 900 and lifetime five seconds; neither source quantifies initial sphere phase or sphere collision radius. Spheres therefore use actual 200-radius projected contact and three guarded packets without fictitious orbital phases or claimed finite orbital contact times.
* **Q geometry remains a projection.** Exact spline tokens do not document the server's local-coordinate transformation. The implementation interprets X as lateral, Z as forward and the two control vectors as distance-scaled Hermite tangents. One dimension cannot reproduce lateral sidestepping. Neither pinned source supplies Q's explosion radius, so the endpoint explosion conservatively requires the aim point to overlap an actual target hitbox; no broad radius is guessed. Numerical integration resolution is an implementation choice, not source evidence.
* **Shared duel scope:** Standard sight has no fog-of-war area to expose, and it never grants true sight. No allied bodies, preloaded passive stacks, imaginary clones or farm are supplied. Item effects, structures and heal/shield-power purchases are outside the model. Released-artifact persistence and caster-death sphere cleanup are explicit simulator lifecycle policies where these primary templates do not state a death rule.

## Budget and validation

The reference prices ordinary third strikes, normal capped AS uptime, one Q hit, three W spheres per cooldown-plus-state cycle, one E per ordinary cooldown and an isolated-champion R. It grants no free Moonlight resets or clone amplification and budgets only W's guaranteed initial shield. Runtime contacts determine resets, second shields and multi-champion damage. Default behavior retains trade restraint, approaches for W/R, uses E to enter R range, and keeps ordinary QWER usage in the budgeted duel.

The 59 focused tests cover source-reader failure on missing pinned geometry, all slot numbers, passive expiration/guards/pets, finite Q coordinates/contact ticks/offsets/shielded endpoint/walls/death, W contact/expiry/shield remainder/destruction/death, E range/ground/root/boundaries/pathing/interrupt/anti-dash/marked target death/current marks/overlays, R timing/guards/actual clone count/following origin/death, and an ordinary 15,000-gold Darius matchup with reflected builds and all scenario scores. Test command: `npx vitest run tests/kits/diana.test.ts --maxWorkers=1`. Logs are retained outside the repository under `/workspace/counter-browser/batch8-diana-*`.

Exact co-location uses the shared reflected facing rule for Q and E; target tracking is updated immediately before dash movement after same-tick cast completions. Four additional regressions cover reflected E landing and Q flight directions for both sides.
