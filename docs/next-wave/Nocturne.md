## Runtime mechanics

**Umbra Blades** starts ready without fabricated farm or preduel attacks. The actual primary basic packet is modified, preserving critical outcomes, blind, dodge, block, parry, on-hits and real pet identity. Avoided basics leave the passive ready. An accepted empowered basic starts the static timer and independently contacts each eligible secondary body inside the center-radius circle; a parried primary does not cancel those other contacts. Secondary damage is proc damage, so a pet's area-damage reduction does not apply. Explicit minion classification alone halves secondary damage and healing. Hit-count objects receive their ordinary basic interaction without consuming the empowerment. Successful champion/monster basics refund three seconds from the current timer, including the empowered basic itself. Healing uses the ordinary healing pipeline, including grievous wounds, and occurs per accepted body.

**Duskbringer** releases its real piercing missile when the 0.25-second cast begins, while the independent cast lock, resource cost and cooldown retain their completion clocks. Each traveled segment creates a projected trail with its own five-second lifetime. Projectile walls destroy the remaining missile at the actual blocking coordinate; prior trail survives. Real health pets have separate contacts and defenses. A hit champion or an explicitly Champion-class summon gains its own source-owned mark and leaves further trail along actual subsequent movement. No owner position is substituted for a pet's position. A mark exposes the path without granting true sight or legal targeting through stealth.

Trail occupancy renews ghosting, 55 AD and 40% bonus total MS in short 30 Hz pulses. The movement bonus multiplies other movement bonuses before soft caps. Leaving all live trail removes the buffs after the quantized pulse expires. Q's damage excludes this Nocturne's trail AD, including AD from a previous Q, while retaining purchased AD. The whole formed trail does not grant a permanent buff merely because Q was cast. A released projectile continues after caster death under the shared artifact policy.

**Shroud of Darkness** adds its innate 50% AS once. Only consumption of this Nocturne's own W shield grants the additional 50% AS, for five seconds; natural expiry and unrelated spell shields grant nothing. At Paranoia recast the kit records the actual active W shield's start tick. Only that surviving shield is eligible for the 0.25-second flight refresh. A W first cast during flight, or a replacement W after the launch shield is consumed, does not inherit that entitlement. The consumed shield cannot be refreshed back into existence.

**Unspeakable Horror** applies an independently guarded target-local tether. A shield at application denies the whole tether. A later shield cannot stop the already accepted persistent ticks or its aftereffects. Actual center-to-target-edge distance is checked each tick; crossing the 465 leash, target death or target resurrection ends the tether permanently. Four scheduled tick opportunities lead to separate fear and slow attempts. Damage reduction, stasis, invulnerability, CC immunity, tenacity, and ordinary targetability remain under shared combat rules. Pet tethers use the real summon and its own statuses rather than the champion. The passive haste requires a live feared body within 1000, in Nocturne's forward 180-degree half-plane. Exact overlap uses reflected `directionTo` facing.

**Paranoia** first casts globally, including when the enemy is beyond recast range or invisible. It spends mana once, applies target-following nearsight even to a dead or untargetable real enemy champion, and leaves that status through death/resurrection until normal expiry. Spell shields, CC immunity and parry still guard the initial effect. Summoned clone bodies do not receive it. The recast has its own range, visibility, living-target and root/ground checks. Usage or timeout starts the original cooldown.

The second stage creates a real displacement-immune dash. It tracks current target position immediately before each movement step, including same-tick relocations and relocations beyond the obsolete maximum distance. Target visibility loss after declaration does not invent a miss. Arrival independently attempts its damage against the target's current defenses; spell shield, stasis or untargetability can deny it. Target death, caster death or caster resurrection ends the flight without an arrival packet. Q/W/E can overlay flight, retaining its movement action. Displacement immunity ends with flight and does not become permanent general CC immunity.

The behavior runs ordinary trade bookkeeping before optional R2 priority. Hold/trade reserve both ultimate stages, and a completed trade retreats even with a genuinely ready recast. Idle and flee retain their usual decisions. The stat budget includes the extra passive AD share, attainable static-timer refunds, healing, Q trail AD and ordinary authored spell estimates. It does not grant a free second passive hit, guaranteed fear-facing movement, or an already consumed spell-shield proc.

## Explicit limits

- Trail geometry is a one-dimensional projection of the primary 150-radius blobs. Each traveled segment is treated as a short capsule projected on the duel axis. Lateral path shape, fog of war, and the primary's unspecified slow fade after five seconds are not reproduced. Movement and refresh clocks use the existing 30 Hz quantization.
- The sources distinguish large monsters for Q trail tagging, but the shared summon metadata does not record monster size. Generic Monster-class pets are therefore not presumed large; they still receive Q damage and the passive's monster cooldown refund.
- The source's intermittent permanent-ghosting E bug is not asserted as guaranteed behavior. Tether hooks require a living caster; no post-death E continuation is promised by these pinned notes. Already released Q flight follows the shared persistent-artifact policy.
- No lane wave, structures, wards, allied bodies, summoner spells or item actives are fabricated. The primary's secondary-minion item on-hit replication is outside the current item-free combat model. Standard path exposure has no separate fog-of-war area to reveal and never reveals invisibility.
