The arena starts at a fresh spawn, so Corki has four missiles and no prebuilt Big One progress. The source's two charges when first learning R are not used as the level-20 starting state. The wiki's ordinary 150-unit splash takes precedence over a conflicting 200-unit game tooltip value.

Valkyrie's source leaves two unresolved details. It gives neither exact patch spacing nor a server-verified reconciliation between the nominal five-tick total and a one-second lingering debuff. The current geometric approximation places patch centers at 200-unit milestones from the dash origin. The burn follows the explicit patch/lingering durations, so continuous occupancy can produce more than the nominal five ticks. The game field named `MaximumTicks` is a tooltip calculation input; this implementation does not claim it proves an independently enforced server damage cap. This discrepancy is exposed in the kit assumptions and tested explicitly, rather than silently substituting an extra hard cap. Wall crossing clips an endpoint inside a one-dimensional wall and does not assert two-dimensional terrain placement.

The stat reference includes passive true basic damage once through the shared attack packet, nominal five-tick W occupancy, a complete E cycle and an ammo/refund-limited R rate with a one-third Big One share. Q is included once through its explicit hook damage hint. Movement, mana, actual occupancy, resistances and avoidance determine combat results separately.

## Draven

| Component | Implemented maximum-rank behavior |
| --- | --- |
| League of Draven | Configurable starting Adoration, default zero. Successful axe catches and actual enemy-summon kills each add one. Champion kills cash `25 + 2 × stacks` gold and clear the stacks. Lethal notification loses half the stacks, rounded down. Earned gold is recorded; no items are bought during a duel. R executes use the actual current count. |
| Spinning Axe | 45 mana and eight-second cooldown. Q readies at most two held axes and refreshes their 5.8-second window, including a legal refresh when both axes are already held. One is consumed at launch, including when Q is readied during an already declared, preserved windup. The 60 + 115% bonus AD addition does not critically amplify; it is folded into the same physical basic packet as ordinary AD/crit, retaining single-packet flat reduction and life steal. |
| Axe return/catch | A tagged contact independently lands after 1.4 seconds. Stationary landing uses Draven's current position; moving landing projects his current movement for 1.4 seconds. A catch requires his gameplay hitbox at that position, restores one held axe if there is room, adds Adoration and resets W. Parry still bounces; projectile-wall destruction creates no catch. The held limit does not cap independently airborne axes. |
| Blood Rush | 20 mana and 12-second cooldown; 40% bonus attack speed for three seconds, 70% decaying movement speed plus ghosting for 1.5 seconds. A catch permits a legal refresh while the buff is active, replacing its duration without stacking attack speed. The policy refreshes it during combat when the attack-speed duration expires and catch resets make it available. |
| Stand Aside | 70 mana, 12-second cooldown and 0.25-second cast. A piercing 1100-range, 260-width projectile travels at 1400, deals 215 + 50% bonus AD physical damage, applies the sourced 0.5-second airborne interruption and a two-second 40% slow to champions and eligible health-based summons. Summon displacement immunity and airborne immunity retain the interruption guard. The transverse push has zero longitudinal travel in the one-axis arena; its unsourced sideways distance is not invented. |
| Whirling Death | 100 mana, 80-second cooldown and 0.5-second cast. Each pass deals 400 + 150% bonus AD physical damage. A finite global projectile passes health-based summons, reverses on champion collision/range/manual recast, decelerates at 4500 units/s², then accelerates back to 2000 while following Draven's current position. It hits each target once per pass. Each contacted unit multiplies subsequent damage by 0.95 down to a 0.5 floor; reversal resets falloff and target tracking. |
| R counterplay | A spell shield blocks one pass, while geometric contact still initiates reversal. The two passes have separate travel and skillshot avoidance. A projectile wall destroys the axes without producing a fake return; moving beyond the turnaround point can avoid the return. Current Adoration executes are checked only after a connected, unshielded R damage packet. The flight and turnaround continue after Draven dies, then terminate at his position. |

The sourced game projectile has range 25000; the root casting wrapper's 20000 range is not substituted for the actual projectile object. Thirty-Hz velocity integration approximates the exact sub-tick turnaround. The 1.4-second catch timing follows the pinned wiki over the conflicting 1.2-second raw return-missile animation. Empowered outgoing axes use the explicit 1700-speed `DravenSpinningAttack` projectile; the game also retains 1600-speed attack animation wrappers. Neither conflict is hidden as verified script behavior.

Stand Aside's wiki sentence pins only the slow's two-second duration. The parsed airborne mechanic omits its former unsupported two-second inference; the simulator's half-second displacement duration is attributed separately to the exact game object's `KnockbackDuration` field.

The wiki does not pin the precise ground-marker offset, catch radius or movement-speed decay curve. Catch geometry uses a documented one-axis projection and Draven's gameplay radius; movement speed decays linearly. Changing movement, immobilization or displacement can lose a catch, and the policy does not assume every catch succeeds. Stationary sideways marker variance, lateral axe travel, shared-gold minions, turrets, map sight, allies and assists are absent.

The stat reference values a bounded two-axe return cycle and catch-supported W attack-speed uptime, respecting the actual attack-speed cap. It includes both R passes once per ultimate cycle; neither an internal projectile nor the manual reversal is valued as a freely spammable extra spell. The combat simulation independently resolves actual catches, shields, avoidance and mana.

## Verification scope

Focused tests cover exact source revisions and game-file attribution, critical expectation, packet order and flat reduction, life steal, real pets, blind/dodge/parry/block/shield/wall interactions, bomb flight and sight, E's cumulative pulse timing/shred/expiry/facing, W overlap/lingering/persistence, ammo/third-shot/recharge, axe windup/catch/displacement/expiry, legal Q/W refreshes, summon interruption and immunity, movement decay, separate R passes/falloff/manual reversal/execute/post-death termination, branch copying, finite hidden-helper usability, ordinary combat policy and positive finite stat valuation. Global matrix, browser and publication checks are reported separately by the batch delivery.

The fallback interaction profiles were rebuilt from the reviewed current records. Draven E retains its sourced two-second slow but no longer falsely assigns that duration to airborne control; the separate game-backed half-second combat interpretation stays in the simulator kit. Kog'Maw's ooze-spacing note keeps its upstream pending-test qualifier.
