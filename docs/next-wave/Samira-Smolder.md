# Samira and Smolder source and mechanics audit

Both modules compile directly from the pinned 16.20 records at level 20 and maximum ability ranks. Samira starts with no Style. Smolder exposes a changeable `dragonPractice` option, defaulting to 225 stacks collected before the duel; this is a farming assumption, not an automatic award during combat.

## Samira

- **Style:** attacks and distinct Q/W/E contacts alternate identities. Repeated hits refresh the six-second expiry without adding a stack. Both W slashes use W identity, so W → E → W can add three stacks. Spell shields still permit Style; parry, dodge, damage block and minion/unknown summon contact do not grant it. Source-classified champion clones can grant Style, as the pinned passive explicitly permits. At six stacks the movement bonus is 21%, R becomes available, and R completion or interruption clears Style. No free pre-combat S rank is assumed.
- **Sword attacks:** the 200-unit condition is measured edge to edge. These attacks retain ranged classification while resolving without a projectile. The separate noncritical magic packet follows physical damage, uses the resulting missing-health share and grants no life steal. Q blade/explosives, both W slashes and E also carry the sword packet. Actual summon contacts preserve their own health, defenses and critical packet.
- **Immobilized-target attack:** a target must already have eligible crowd control and no spell shield, CC immunity or displacement immunity. A fixed one-tick windup from the game’s 0.01-second cast launches six independent 17%-AD bolts over 0.5 seconds. The first is the basic attack with one on-hit application; later bolts use default damage, critical expectation and life steal without repeating on-hits. The per-target ten-second lockout begins at declaration. One connected eligible bolt can extend an existing knock-up by half a second; root alone does not create a knock-up. The optional approach dash does not count as an ability cast for effects such as Kassadin’s Force Pulse.
- **Q:** maximum-rank damage is 20 + 110% total AD. Its critical multiplier follows the pinned calculation `1 + 0.5 × critChance × (critDamage − 1)`, giving 150% at guaranteed crit and ordinary 200% critical damage. Physical Q damage grants full life steal; its sword magic does not. Gun Q travels at 2600 speed and is intercepted by the first target or projectile wall. Blade Q is nonprojectile. Q during E queues explosives for the actual traveled dash segment and does not terminate the dash.
- **W:** starts immediately, preserving voluntary movement. It destroys hostile projectiles within 325 center units for 0.75 seconds and applies independent first/end slashes to actual nearby targets. A spell shield blocks one slash. R, stasis or death ends the whirl without an invented second hit. Its nonrestrictive 0.1-second infobox cast is documented rather than imposed as a movement lock.
- **E:** requires a living, visible and targetable enemy within 600 center units, travels a fixed 650 units at 1600 speed, and grants 40% attack speed for five seconds. The swept path damages a champion and actual summons once each. Q and R can overlap E. Both R → E and E → R preserve the dash and original R clock; disarm interrupts R while an already committed E dash continues. A real champion takedown resets E.
- **R:** costs six Style and no mana, has a static five-second cooldown, permits movement with an additive 30% penalty, and emits ten separately defended homing shots over a finite 2.277-second channel. Each shot checks 600 center range at emission. A visible enemy, including an actual minion summon, is needed to activate R; subsequent invisibility does not prevent its shots. Root does not interrupt the channel; silence, stun and disarm do. Actual summons receive independent packets and each spell shield blocks only one shot. Only source-classified minions receive the 25% minion packet; champion clones, other and unknown classes retain full damage. Already launched projectiles persist after interruption or death, without producing future shots.

The wiki explicitly extrapolates the level-20 sword bonus to **21 + 11.3235294% total AD**, multiplied by `1 + targetMissingHealthFraction`. The pinned client tooltip calculation instead contains a flat **10% AD** coefficient. The implementation uses the wiki’s explicit level-20 series and discloses this unresolved discrepancy.

The raw R template gives a mix of 0.198/0.231-second intervals without specifying their order. Shot times use absolute cumulative rounding uniformly over the parsed 2.013-second firing span, starting immediately. This is a declared tick-level scheduling approximation. Q shot/blade selection uses the 340-unit declaration check; the source’s end-of-cast form switch when distance changes during the cast remains a limitation. Forward cone geometry, lateral explosives, allied CC, turrets and full two-dimensional attack-dash order reacquisition are not reconstructed by the one-axis arena. E uses the existing terrain-crossing dash policy, which clips a blocked endpoint.

## Smolder

- **Practice and critical scaling:** a real champion ability hit adds one stack. W and E grant at most one champion-hit stack per cast. There is no free ambient farming or ordinary-basic stack gain. The wiki parser loses the passive’s embedded critical-damage numbers; the exact game formulas recover Q magic as `0.25 × stacks × [1 + 1.4 × critChance × (critDamage − 1)]`, W explosion magic as `0.55 × stacks`, and E magic as `0.08 × stacks × [1 + 0.75 × critChance × (critDamage − 1)]`.
- **Q:** uses the current basic-attack windup and current edge attack range, then sends a targeted 1800-speed fireball. Maximum-rank physical damage is `(100 + 130% bonus AD) × [1 + 0.75 × critChance × (critDamage − 1)]`. The physical portion grants 50% life steal; passive magic grants none. Q applies one full on-hit packet. Spell shield, dodge and damage block deny its whole packet and burn. Invalid cast targets cancel without paying the normal completion cost. Cast upgrades use the stack count at completion; impact damage uses current stats.
- **25 stacks:** a real 285-radius collision explosion reaches nearby actual targets. A per-cast identity list prevents the primary from taking a duplicated explosion or later extra-bolt hit.
- **125 stacks:** the sourced arc contains `ceil(2 + 0.008 × stacks)` bolts. The one-axis representation uses one 900-speed forward corridor over 500 units, with half physical/passive-magic damage and a 150-radius terminal explosion. It does not multiply damage by bolt count or hit the primary twice. Exact angular coverage and minion-primary Q orders remain limitations.
- **225 stacks:** each accepted contact adds an independent three-second true burn with one-second pulses, totaling `(0.00025 × bonusAD + 0.00005 × stacks) × targetMaxHP`. New burns stack without refreshing previous clocks. The obsolete unused `Tier3_TrueDamagePercent` client constant is not added to the explicit burn calculation. Only Smolder’s own qualifying damage can execute a burned champion below 6.5% maximum health, including the initial Q packet and burn ticks. Parry and ordinary minimum-health floors remain effective. The 300-damage monster cap does not apply to ordinary minion summons.
- **Q refunds and dead targets:** a champion kill or source-classified minion kill refunds 15 mana at most once for that cast. An actual minion killed by an independently ticking Q burn grants its kill stack and the same cast-local refund; an E kill on a Q-burned target does not receive Q credit. Champion clones, other and unknown pet classes do not receive invented minion kill rewards. The first actual missile is prepared from cast-local metadata before contact, including the first Q of a fight. A target that dies while Q is in flight causes that flight to be discarded and grants the documented stack, without inventing splash or extra bolts at the corpse.
- **W:** the actual first projectile is piercing and uses the pinned movement component: 2000 initial speed, −1600 units/s² acceleration and 400 minimum speed. Champion contact creates a separate 385-radius explosion, even when a shield blocked the glob. The glob has no passive magic; the explosion does. A shield can block one of these packets. Nearby summons receive actual glob and splash contacts. The duel has one enemy champion, so there is no invented chain of additional champion explosions. The wiki’s post-cast basic-attack lockout has no numerical duration and is not assigned a guessed one.
- **E:** a finite 1.25-second flight disables ordinary attacks, grants 75% additive movement speed, ignores unit/terrain collision for voluntary walking, and emits `5 + floor(stacks/100)` independently shieldable spell bolts. Champion targets have priority over summons; otherwise lowest current health percentage wins, with deterministic distance/id ties. Only visible, targetable enemies within 700 center units can be acquired. Other ability casts, immobilization including root/self-stasis, and death end flight. Silence and ground alone do not end an existing flight. Ending inside generated terrain snaps to its nearest legal edge. Already launched bolts survive death.
- **R:** declaration captures its origin and direction. An independent wave starts 600 units behind that origin, waits the chosen spawn delay, travels at 1700 speed and heals only when crossing Smolder’s current position. The 0.75-second cast permits movement after an explicit 0.4-second movement lock. The wave persists after death. The modeled center deals 525 + 150% bonus AD + 150% AP and applies a 40% slow for two seconds; source-classified minion summons take half damage, while champion clones, other and unknown classes retain full damage. Ordinary sight is not true sight.

For E, the wiki specifies **1800** bolt speed while the active game missile’s movement component says **2000** and its cosmetic `missileSpeed` field says **1800**. The implementation retains the wiki’s 1800 policy and records the conflict. Initial E shot phase is not given by the sources; shots use evenly spaced absolute clocks starting immediately.

R’s client `MomSpawnDelay = 1.0` does not prove whether it is relative to cast declaration or cast completion. The chosen policy launches at declaration + one second from the declaration origin, explicitly an approximation. The one-axis aim line models the 125-radius center sweetspot; independent weaker contacts in the 300-radius outer band cannot currently be recovered from lateral sidestep state. Unobstructed map vision, brush, ally interactions, item actives and full two-dimensional terrain/arc geometry remain outside the duel model.
