This kit uses the existing patch 26.20 / Data Dragon 16.20.1 dataset, at level 20 with Q5/W5/E5/R3. It adds actual persistent Dark Sphere bodies, two paid W stages, independently moving E waves and spheres, and a declaration-committed R barrage. It does not create a minion, ally or pre-existing sphere to improve a duel.

## Progression and passive

Seventeen maximum-rank skill points beyond the first give the sourced 85 Splinters. This is automatic skill progression, independent of unknown minion or champion history. The `additionalSplinters` option accepts an explicit integer from 0 to 35, defaults to zero and grants no invented farm. Thus the ordinary kit has the Q40, W60 and E80 upgrades; R100 and the AP120 capstone require additional history or actual in-duel procs.

Two qualifying damaging casts against the same real enemy champion within four seconds can create one pickup, subject to that target's eight-second cooldown. The pickup arrives after 0.6 seconds. At level 20 it adds three Splinters, capped at 120, and restores 239 mana once, limited by missing mana. The exact binary `ManaPerProc` level calculation extends beyond the parsed wiki's level-18 series. A three-stack pickup does not restore three times 239. At the cap, pickups still restore mana without adding stacks. Ordinary attacks and ordinary minion/monster pets give no champion-damage proc. An actually killed minion requires an explicit `largeMinion` classification before it can send the source's one-stack pickup. That real pickup also arrives after 0.6 seconds and restores 239 mana once, including at the stack cap; arrival while Syndra is dead grants neither stacks nor mana. No anonymous minion wave supplies it.

The sources do not specify how every multi-packet ability is grouped into passive damage instances. The implementation explicitly groups W magic/true damage, an E cast's cone/spheres and an entire R barrage into one instance each; separate Q casts remain separate. This conservative cast grouping is a model boundary, including for clone eligibility. Actual positive health or shield damage is required; parry, spell shields and damage-only invulnerability cannot invent a proc. At 120, a single dynamic total-AP multiplier grants 15%, including current bought and other flat AP, without recursive flat-AP growth.

## Dark Sphere

Q costs 60 mana and creates its body at the actual aimed location after 0.6 seconds. Aimed distance is clamped to 800. Its initial 230 + 70% AP area damage uses the primary 210 radius; damage defenses and sidestep branches do not erase the sphere. The body remains for six seconds, has a stable id and position, is untargetable and does not attack. There is no supplied cap on simultaneously active spheres.

The Q40 upgrade gives two charges. The static minimum interval is 1.25 seconds, independent of purchased haste. Sequential seven-second recharge uses purchased haste plus R3's 30 Q-only ability haste. Other spells do not borrow that haste. The unused binary `Upgrade2MaxAmmo:3` has no corresponding pinned primary upgrade and is not enabled. Pending Q damage remains meaningful after caster death.

The primary 210 radius and no cast lock override the binary command's 180 radius / 0.375-second cast metadata. The binary Q missile's fixed 0.6-second clock corroborates the primary appearance delay rather than supplying a normal flying damage projectile.

## Force of Will

W has no free opening ammunition. It chooses an actual eligible ground sphere or actual hostile Minion-class pet within 925 of Syndra and 500 of the aimed point. The primary description says nearest units before spheres while its notes say sphere priority; this implementation explicitly chooses spheres first, then distance and stable id. W-held and E/R-moving spheres are unavailable. Unknown or epic monster identity is not inferred from a body's radius or name; the current arena does not supply classified non-epic Blue/Red jungle monsters. Their special mana/cooldown/burn benefits are not fabricated.

Pickup pays 100 mana, is not an ability activation for cast listeners, and holds the exact body for up to five seconds. A sphere's six-second expiry refreshes at pickup only. A nonsphere is cleansed and placed in source-owned stasis with the same id, HP, expiry and already launched projectiles. Its actual position follows Syndra. New attacks, maturing attacks, custom beams/leaps and voluntary movement are denied; surrounding passive aura effects such as real Tibbers burn remain active. Manual recast is an activation, releases that same body and removes only W's own stasis. Timeout, actual owner death or resurrection also drops it. Lethal-damage cleanup runs synchronously rather than waiting for a later dead-owner tick.

The sourced rank-five W damage is 190 + 65% AP magic. Its upgrade adds true damage equal to that full pre-mitigation formula times `(0.12 + 0.0002 × AP)`: at 100 AP, 255 magic plus 35.7 true before defenses. The mixed damage and 25% slow for 1.5 seconds form one guarded packet, so a spell shield blocks both damage parts and control. Landing grants ordinary sight, not true sight. The primary post-effect nine-second cooldown starts on manual landing or automatic drop; the primary 225 impact radius overrides binary 195, and primary rank-five cooldown nine overrides ambiguous binary stage arrays ending at eight.

**Unquantified timing:** the W command declares missile metadata 1450 while the throw object's movement component says 20. Neither establishes actual gameplay throw flight or arc. This model explicitly places the actual held body at the chosen landing point when the manual recast resolves. It does not claim this instantaneous spatial placement is exact server timing, or fabricate a 47.5-second flight. An endpoint inside impassable terrain is clipped to an edge; lateral throw geometry remains outside the scalar arena. Carrying offset, recall/realm transitions and classified jungle-monster special cases remain limits where those arena systems do not exist.

## Scatter the Weak

E costs 50 mana, casts in 0.25 seconds and has a 15-second ordinary cooldown. Its 200 + 60% AP magic damage and cone displacement use primary ranges: enemies through 700, eligible spheres through 800. The visible wave travels at 2500. Two independent invisible waves at 1600 and 1100 can acquire a late Q sphere, and never gain an additional enemy-damage entitlement. The original primary angles, 56° and upgraded 84°, are retained as evidence; the one-dimensional axis cannot reconstruct lateral cone coverage. Client half-angle and 650/675 targeting metadata do not replace the primary ranges.

The action policy uses the same existing coordinate allowance as actual wave acquisition at the primary 800 boundary, so reflected floating-point coordinates do not select different commands. A sphere exactly coincident with Syndra has no positional left/right side; its push follows the actual wave that acquired it, including a wave traveling in the negative world direction. Spheres on either side otherwise retain their real radial direction.

Every acquired sphere has its own actual 2000-speed, 200-width swept projectile. Push distance is 950, capped to 1200 from Syndra's position when that sphere is struck, plus its 100 hit radius. A sphere can be acquired only once by one E cast. A body is kept alive while it moves even if its original expiry passes, and returns to the same id at the end of flight. Starting inside terrain can extend a push to an open edge or reverse it when the forward exit is unreachable. Actual projectile walls stop the visible/invisible waves and moving spheres.

A target takes E's damage once per cast across cone and sphere contacts. Later spheres can still apply their separate 70-unit push and 1.25-second stun. Stun supplies standard sight only while that accepted stun remains. Damage, parry, spell shields, control immunity, Black Shield and terrain guards use actual recipient state. The E80 70% slow begins after the target's real stun ends, or after the cone's modeled displacement; it is not spent in parallel with the stun. Cleansing or denying a control does not fabricate its duration.

**Unquantified timing:** no reliable body knockback speed/duration is supplied. The implementation uses the existing guarded zero-duration displacement for the sourced distances, constrained by actual terrain. This omits temporal airborne travel and is declared rather than hidden behind a guessed speed. Geometry and late-Q acquisition retain their actual supplied flight clocks.

## Unleashed Power

R pays 100 mana and starts its 80-second cooldown at declaration, before its 0.25-second cast lock ends. The source says its effect begins at cast start and cannot be interrupted even by owner death or resurrection. A persistent scheduler retains that committed entitlement. The cast uses exactly three newly conjured sphere bodies plus up to four actual eligible ground spheres within 3000, excluding W-held and E/R-moving bodies. Pending unappeared Q spheres are not invented as eligible ground ammunition.

Every released orb has a separate real flight, 160 + 20% AP magic damage, and its own spell-shield/parry/projectile-block interaction. One shield blocks one orb. The exact RSpell movement starts at 1, accelerates at 7500 and caps at 10000. Target untargetability invalidates the orb's effects while preserving its body. Target death stops remaining unlaunched orbs and refreshes their real bodies' life; released orbs continue and become ground spheres. Landed spheres keep their identities and live six seconds. No favorable ring of lateral E targets is invented.

At 100 Splinters, only an R orb that actually removes target HP can execute a target strictly below 15% maximum HP after its own positive damage. Exact equality is not below. Damage-only invulnerability, blocked packets, unrelated damage and ordinary low HP do not grant an execute.

**Unquantified timing:** the pinned sources do not supply orb release staggering. The implementation explicitly releases one orb per 30 Hz simulation tick as an approximation, with the real individual acceleration and defensive checks preserved. This is not claimed as exact server cadence. The source's very-shortly-before-R sphere collection wording does not define how an unappeared Q object should be classified; no arbitrary favorable pending-Q collection window is assumed.

## Allocation, policy and validation

The reference budget prices sustained Q recharge with Q-only haste, sphere-supported W cycles and its nonlinear true damage, one E damage entitlement per victim, and a bounded sustainable R inventory. It does not price invented farm, allies, free opening W, guaranteed ray stuns, passive mana beyond actual missing mana, or guaranteed executes. Fight/kite can use R from real inventory or lethal attainable damage; hold/trade reserve fresh R and completed trades retreat. Flee uses E defensively without inventing a free W object.

Focused tests exercise the actual source formulas and temporal/spatial boundaries, two-charge recharge, capped AP/passive pickup, mixed W defenses, same-body carry/drop, real Tibbers aura and Heimer beam suppression, late-Q E acquisition, one E damage/multiple control contacts, actual walls, moving/held R exclusions, per-orb shields, owner death/resurrection, target death/untargetability, strict execute threshold, legal spending and all six strict mirrored full-budget Darius scenarios. The preserved eighth focused run passed its original 59 assertions. After peer review corrected classified large-minion pickups, the actual `peer-back-after-v2.json` run passed all 63 Syndra assertions, including the strict full-budget reflection, alongside 81 Taliyah assertions and 40 shared combat/movement contracts (184/184). Batch-wide source, matrix, retained-suite, browser and private-delivery validation remain root's separate gates.
