## Graves behavior

- Two shells are spent on committed attack launch. A one-tick windup, an empty-clip attack lock, the one-shell idle reload, and E reload/reset are independent clocks.
- Four normal rays span 24 degrees; six critical rays span 30 degrees. Each ray intersects circular bodies on the duel axis and stops at the closest actual target. Critical chance weights those two actual geometries; per-pellet critical damage adds half the current bonus critical damage (150% at an ordinary 200% critical multiplier). Each target receives one basic/on-hit pellet and subsequent default-damage pellets. Blind sends the fan away from its declared target; dodge, parry, block, spell shields and projectile walls retain their distinct categories.
- Q pierces bodies outward, retains its actual ground endpoint, detonates at the terrain contact or its two-second clock, and launches the reverse wave. Endpoint and reverse damage share target-contact history. A projectile wall removes the powder trail. Raised terrain supplies actual wall locations; the environment's `walls` boolean supplies none.
- W starts its canister flight at declaration, then creates an independent four-second smoke field. Its removable nearsight and anchored sight restriction are separate. Sight restriction survives CC immunity, cleanse, source death and target untargetability; exiting the field clears it. Resistance/removal suppresses the nearsight until exit/reentry. Pulses use an absolute .25-second clock to avoid accumulating tick-rounding drift.
- E reloads one shell and resets the attack timer. One True Grit stack is guaranteed; its second stack requires an actual visible targetable champion or explicitly champion-class clone ahead within 1300 while dashing. Stacks cap at eight and last four seconds. Damage refreshes only against champions or explicitly non-minion pets, including monsters. Q/W/R can overlay only an E that began during an actual reload; their cast clocks continue while the dash moves. Each max-rank stack gives 19 armor and 9.5 MR.
- R pierces ordinary pets with spell damage, explodes on real champion or champion-class clone contact even through a spell shield, and applies the separate reduced cone packet to additional targets. The cone starts 150 units behind the shell and reaches 740 along its axis. Root, ground and airborne prevent recoil without preventing the shot. Recoil obeys actual terrain.
- Custom projectile impact forecasts expose committed threats to defensive behavior. Launched pellets, Q's powder clock/return and smoke remain valid after source death. Budget valuation uses a real two-shell/reload cycle and the direct Q/W/R formulas, without preloaded grit or free E reloads.

## Explicit Graves limits and conflicts

1. **Reload is an analytic approximation.** The pinned primary note expressly says that the precise formula is unknown. Empty and one-shell timings are 2.08/1.3 seconds. The published maximum reload-speed tooltip explicitly extrapolates to level 20 as `0.65114 + (0.89119 - 0.65114) / 17 * 19`; its reciprocal is the minimum modeled reload time. The implementation interpolates measured endpoints up to 150% external bonus attack speed and caps there. It does not invent the documented anomalous 150–200% curve. Growth does not consume the external-AS threshold; the level-dependent minimum accounts for the published growth direction. The 2.08-second baseline is the model's zero-external-AS anchor, not a claim of an exact server formula at every level.
2. **Pellet coefficient conflict.** The pinned primary additional-pellet table uses `.33302` of its first pellet; the exact client calculation contains `.3330000042915344`. The primary table governs this kit. Its primary critical footnote gives increased damage equal to 50% of current bonus critical damage, with the wider six-ray geometry; no ordinary critical multiplier is substituted.
3. **One-dimensional geometry.** Fan rays retain angles, finite radii and actual nearest interception, but combatants have no lateral movement. Blind's unspecified random direction is represented by firing away along the duel axis. R's cone intersects that axis; no fictitious off-axis enemies appear.
4. **Unquantified motion.** Buckshot's non-champion knockback distance and duration are absent from the pinned sources, so that displacement is omitted. R recoil uses an immediate terrain-limited 400-unit move because no trustworthy movement speed/duration is published; its finite dash interval and anti-dash interaction remain unmodeled. The binary `GravesAutoAttackRecoil` object contains stale Riven icon/name/ability fields, so its missile speed is deliberately rejected as recoil-speed evidence.
5. **Q parsing/timing.** The parsed description's `0.231` is a `rutngt` rendering artifact. Exact primary raw text specifies `.2` rounded to a game tick, corroborated by the exact binary; the simulation uses the next 30 Hz tick. Q's perpendicular 500-wide endpoint rectangle has no additional transverse targets in this arena.
6. **Smoke source conflict.** The explicit speed tooltip says its canister starts traveling at cast start, while a generic shared note says the spell casts from the end position. The explicit timing tooltip governs. The primary tooltip labels the 250-unit sight radius itself as estimated. Allied shared vision and non-champion vision-dependent actions are outside this duel's vision model.

## Zeri implementation and source limits

Implemented in `kits/champions/Zeri.ts` from the five pinned primary templates: Living Battery 4051663, Burst Fire 4062236, Ultrashock Laser 4060386, Spark Surge 4015351 and Lightning Crash 4016978. Versioned supplementary evidence is `https://raw.communitydragon.org/16.20/game/data/characters/zeri/zeri.bin.json`, version `16.20.8248524+branch.releases-16-20.content.release`, SHA-256 `4f2a9d404865a68f88f70522ae9c1328193c540475c19bcd6760e80eb5e2efe7`. Each excerpt names its exact object. The module reads numeric values and interaction rules through `KitContext`, including Q attack timings, projectile speeds and attack-event grouping, W's cast-time minimum, and E's 3000-unit wall-crossing maximum.

Living Battery replaces a basic attack at committed launch with a nonprojectile magic spell packet. It adds no ordinary physical attack damage, crit or on-hit/on-attack effects. Only the full-charge zap consumes spell shield. Both notify enemy ability-activation listeners once; a canceled windup does not. Charge starts at 100, increases from actual displacement and Q declarations, and is spent only on a real zap launch. Uncharged executes reject a target with an existing shield and use the shared minimum-health/invulnerability rules.

Q emits seven independently moving rounds across a five-degree fan, or three across two degrees during R. Emission spans the current windup and follows Zeri's actual position. Each ray independently intersects circular champion and summoned-unit hitboxes. The first enemy receives the burst's sole basic on-hit event; remaining pellets retain basic damage, while E's later targets have area classification and no on-hit/life steal. Normal rounds stop on the closest real target; E's rounds can continue through it. Blind fires away from the declared target as an explicit approximation for the one-dimensional arena. Dodge, block, parry, untargetability and real projectile walls resolve at impact. Hit-count props take one hit per burst and continue blocking subsequent normal rounds.

Q preserves an existing zap windup, is usable while silenced, and still requires the normal attack/disarm and other cast-control checks. Its static cooldown begins at declaration and follows current attack speed. The module recomputes the 1.5 attack-speed cap and the 60% excess bonus-AS-to-AD conversion after cripples. R's attack speed alone can exceed the cap and never feeds the AD conversion. Q and W missiles expose their real forecast impact clocks to defensive policies.

W uses the primary physical damage description and leveling despite the template's contradictory Magic type flag. Its initial speed is 2500, agreeing with the versioned missile movement component; the legacy top-level speed is not authoritative. Actual terrain contact creates the delayed laser. The primary defines a span from -50 to 1550 relative to the wall, totaling 1600 units; this takes precedence over the client's 1500-unit `WallBeamRange`. The pulse is single-target spell damage and projectile-interceptable. The laser is area damage, cannot be projectile-intercepted, and critically strikes against champions and explicitly classified monsters. It survives Zeri's death once anchored. Terrain without a represented position supplies no invented wall contact.

E resets the attack and Q clocks at declaration. The actual dash travels 300 units at 600 plus current movement speed. A wall within 50 units extends it across that wall's thickness to a clear far endpoint, subject to the pinned maximum. Its five-second empowerment begins when the dash ends, including an interruption. W and R can overlay the dash; Q and attacks cannot. E refunds once per connected Q burst, weighted between 0.5 and 1.5 seconds by its critical chance, or 0.5 seconds for a connected charged zap. Its magic bonus uses the retained critical-scaling formula.

R checks real targetable nova contacts before granting five seconds of Overcharge, including a spellshielded champion contact. The nova can hit invisible enemies as an area effect; chains require visible targets. Later qualifying champion contacts extend the remaining duration by 2.5 seconds up to five and grant temporary Hypercharged stacks. Q uses only its first target. Chains visit up to four distinct actual enemies, excluding the triggering target and hit-count props; E's extra packet and chains cannot recursively extend R. Resurrection clears Hypercharged and prevents regaining it during that existing Overcharge. Ordinary resurrection is not treated as a new game respawn for passive charge. R cooldown starts after Overcharge ends.

The arena has no lateral motion, terrain fog, structures or wards/plants as explicit target classes. These are disclosed boundaries rather than synthetic secondary targets. Unclassified summons are not assumed to be monsters or structures. E wall riding follows the represented axis. The finite reference budget replaces ordinary AD attacks with sustained Q, a charged zap after ten Q charges, regular W and one R cycle; conditional secondary-target chains and repeated E/R bonuses are not free starting damage.
