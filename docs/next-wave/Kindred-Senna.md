# Kindred and Senna — batch 7

These kits use the exact 16.20 wiki revisions and versioned CommunityDragon objects described below. Both begin without invented farm history. Their optional permanent stacks are explicit inputs, and runtime contacts keep champion and summon state separate.

## Kindred

The primary templates are pinned to Mark of the Kindred **4069806**, Dance of Arrows **4007746**, Wolf's Frenzy **4078260**, Mounting Dread **4078261**, and Lamb's Respite **4024177**. The committed record retains the raw range formula, Hunter's Vigor interpolation and E critical-scaling template that the ordinary parser omitted. Every value and rule used by the module is read through `KitContext`.

[Kindred's 16.20 binary](https://raw.communitydragon.org/16.20/game/data/characters/kindred/kindred.bin.json) is version `16.20.8248524+branch.releases-16-20.content.release`, SHA-256 `ae871533c0b6379f188a0ea7805cd1f11eb1f8a026ed85c2b20d0b156cc2c391`. It supplies Q/E missile clocks, E's full-bite critical calculation and the documented R-radius disagreement. Wolf's actual companion record is [KindredWolf's 16.20 binary](https://raw.communitydragon.org/16.20/game/data/characters/kindredwolf/kindredwolf.bin.json), SHA-256 `893932abab7632401551a31e4e593c7a15c4ccbbfc488f75e1c2fa5540a246cd`, with the same patch version. It supplies his base attack speed, reach, walking speed, spell windup and untargetable unit geometry. Exact objects and excerpts are preserved with each ability record; the original bytes and retrieval metadata remain in `/workspace/counter-next-wave/batch7-audit/`.

- `marks=0` and `targetMarked=0` are the defaults. Before-duel marks require an explicit caller option. The hunt selection is a genuine HUD action, does not notify enemy spell-cast hooks, arms after eight seconds and obeys the six-second combat restriction. Only a real hunted champion kill supplies a mark. No jungle camps or allied takedowns are created.
- Q performs a real dash and resets attacks. At most three distinct visible actual targets each receive one arrow; an isolated champion receives one packet. Each arrow has its own homing travel and projectile-wall/spell-shield check. The current basic target is preferred, and pet area reductions apply to that pet. The AS buff scales with permanent marks. Wolf's territory only lowers Q cooldowns that are longer than the sourced two-second static value.
- W has a fixed territory, an initial travel clock, an untargetable Wolf, real target selection, walking and individual spell attacks. Ordinary movement respects terrain. Lamb's last basic target takes priority, followed by the visible champion and nearest summon. Wolf ends on departure or death. His remaining W cooldown applies haste after elapsed active time is subtracted. Only explicitly classified monster summons receive monster damage, the health-component cap and the monster slow.
- Hunter's Vigor starts empty, gains progress from actual movement and declared attacks, and heals on a connected damaging ordinary hit. The level-20 heal uses the pinned interpolation and remains available at full health.
- E keeps its expiring mark and three-hit counter on the contacted unit. Its third accepted basic releases an independently blockable Wolf projectile. The current pinned revision uses continuous critical scaling of the entire bite: `bite × (1 + critChance × 0.5 × (critDamage − 1))`. The former low-health critical execute threshold is absent from this revision and is not recreated. Monster caps apply only to explicitly classified monsters. An invalid target at cast completion costs no mana and retains the normal cooldown.
- R creates a real shared ground zone at declaration, before its cast lock. The generic engine queries current spatial membership for every damage, execute and healing transaction. Both sides receive the 10% maximum-health floor before lethal processing. At the floor, active healing and life steal fail, while regeneration works. The final heal occurs exactly four seconds later, after protection expires, for eligible targetable units still inside; the expiry callback survives the caster's death and never revives a dead unit.

The one-dimensional model uses center membership for R's 535-radius zone; the primary template does not fully specify boundary geometry. The wiki's 535 radius takes precedence over the game's 530 tooltip data, with both preserved as evidence. Q uses the pinned wiki's estimated `500 + movementSpeed` dash speed and 27-unit Vigor movement estimate. Its ordinary dash crosses clear terrain endpoints, but the special 400-unit extended wall endpoint search is not implemented. Wolf uses the companion's base attack speed and proportional cast scaling; player level-growth stats are not added to the temporary unit. No untargetable visual Wolf is treated as a minion or a monster for unrelated targeting rules. R protection and healing of hit-count props remain unmodeled pending prop-specific evidence: the primary R source excludes wards, structural turrets and plants, but does not establish an exception for every hit-count summon. The shared engine therefore conservatively excludes these special health bodies; this is a model limit, not a sourced blanket exemption.

The reference budget prices one isolated Q arrow, mixes Q's ordinary and two-second territory cooldowns by Wolf uptime, caps added basic attack speed, and includes a realizable three-hit E cadence and Wolf's actual attack rate/active cooldown cycle. Runtime target sharing, defenses, movement, expiration and death remain resolved by the duel.

## Senna

The six exact primary revisions are Basic Attack **4044104**, Absolution **4071407**, Piercing Darkness **4042887**, Last Embrace **4009139**, Curse of the Black Mist **3994514**, and Dawning Shadow **4008033**. Supplemental raw excerpts retain the level-18 windup cap and R's explicit standard-sight qualifier. [Senna's 16.20 binary](https://raw.communitydragon.org/16.20/game/data/characters/senna/senna.bin.json) is version `16.20.8248524+branch.releases-16-20.content.release`, SHA-256 `872181c999e995ef3eef416fdcb26162a048ebe448b583574360598f82d44a25`. All 14 curated field fragments were verified against their exact pinned objects.

- Basics use a nonprojectile beam, 0.4 AS ratio, level-18-capped 21.875% windup with 60% AS timing effectiveness, and 160% AD critical damage. Natural level-20 AS growth remains intact. Relic Cannon is a separate noncritical 20% AD packet after positive ordinary damage, with its own life steal.
- Absolution's four-second mark and four-second extraction immunity belong to the actual contacted unit. The second accepted hit extracts 10% current health after ordinary damage and Relic, adds Mist if its return path survives projectile walls, and consumes the mark. Late windups extend a near-expiring mark to 0.75 seconds. Actual champions and summons explicitly classified Champion by their pinned sources share this rule; minion, monster and unknown pets receive no champion extraction. Fractional fallback uses the lesser of the first and second contact weights for proc damage and Mist, then consumes the mark and starts ordinary immunity. This is a bounded scalar approximation without invented residual branches.
- `mist=0` is the default explicit preduel option. Each stack supplies 0.75 AD; each 20-stack band supplies 20 range and 10% crit. Excess purchased-plus-Mist critical chance converts to life steal. No lane farm, allies, quest rewards, gold or corpse pickups are created. The pinned source supplies no extracted-soul travel speed, so its return-path wall check is immediate. Champion corpse pickups occur after the scored duel ends and are omitted.
- Q uses 80% of the continuous attack-windup formula before tick rounding. It acquires a real visible target within current attack range capped at 1100, or a real pet to aim its 1300 beam; explicit endpoints choose the nearest actual unit. The 100-wide shadow and 280-wide light rays damage/slow actual targets, heal Senna once, use ordinary pets' area resistance and trigger one basic interaction against hit-count objects. Sourced Champion summons receive independent Q on-hits, life steal and Absolution. The acquired Champion receives the single-target spell packet and sole on-attack refund; if the acquired unit is not a Champion, the first struck Champion in stable real-champion-then-pet-ID order receives that primary role. Every other Q target retains area damage, including secondary Champion summons with area resistance. Normal ordering is spell damage → attached on-hits → Relic → current-health extraction; a spell shield preserves the on-hit packet before consuming the main damage/slow. Ordinary attack declarations independently refund one real second of Q cooldown.
- W deals a single-target spell packet to the first actual contacted enemy, attaches for one second, follows it, then separately roots enemy centers within 280 for 2.25 seconds before tenacity. The target's death triggers the spread immediately. The attachment survives Senna's death; root-time untargetability, stasis, spell shields and CC immunity remain independent. The nondamaging spread creates no second Absolution hit.
- E has a mobile one-second setup and eight-second aura, 400-center-unit champion detection, and 20% + 5%/100 AP movement speed while camouflaged, detected or broken. Basic windup starts and other spell declarations break camouflage; it rearms after 1.75 seconds. The binary says 1.5 seconds, while the exact primary wiki says 1.75; the latter is retained with the conflict recorded. True sight bypasses camouflage, while invisibility and nearsight keep their own rules. Real pets use their actual owner's detection, so Wolf cannot invent remote sight. Resurrection during setup prevents activation and leaves no phantom aura cooldown hold.
- R retains cast-start origin/direction, reveals Senna during its one-second setup and grants her a three-second shield on release: 200 + 50% AP + 1.5 per current Mist. Its 20000-speed global beam has 320-wide shadow and 2400-wide light waves sharing one destructible flight. Actual champions and sourced Champion summons receive 550 + 115% bonus AD + 70% AP damage; minion, monster and unknown pets receive no damage, sight or extraction. A wall stops both waves without removing the already-granted selfshield. Its three-second enemy reveal explicitly supplies standard sight and never exposes stealth as true sight would.

The one-dimensional geometry cannot distinguish transverse Q/R ray contacts, supplies no allied recipients, and does not simulate path fog or brush sight. Q's dodge, block and blind interactions are marked pending-test by the source: this model lets spell damage/slow ignore blind/block while dodge suppresses champion on-hits and parry suppresses contact bonuses. The source-limited return timing and fractional extraction approximation above remain visible assumptions. Reference hints include direct spells, attack-refunded Q cycles, Relic, bounded half-health extraction, Q healing and R selfshield.
