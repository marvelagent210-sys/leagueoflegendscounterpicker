### Passive: Phenomenal Evil Power, revision 3932550

Source: `parts[Innate].text`: "Veigar generates a stack of Phenomenal Evil for each enemy champion hit by his abilities, and 5 stacks whenever he scores an enemy champion takedown." `parts[Phenomenal Evil].text`: "For each stack, Veigar gains 1 ability power." Notes: "Veigar's abilities do not have to deal damage nor affect their targets to grant a stack." Track integer stacks per world and update a permanent AP stat, using `ctx.buff` or replacing status arrays rather than mutating a cached derived-stat value in place. There is no sourced number of pre-fight stacks at level 20. Expose a nonnegative integer `phenomenalEvilStacks` option, with its chosen default prominently documented; **0 is the least assumptive fresh-duel default, not a realistic assertion about a farmed late-game Veigar**. Do not infer a default of hundreds of stacks from his level. Follow the existing Nasus/Cho'Gath option pattern, but do not optimize free stacks as a build variant.

Use `start` for the initial AP stat so the budget reference sees it: `engine/budget.ts` deliberately strips `hooks` before reference initialization, so an AP grant only in `onStart` is invisible to allocation. Initialize runtime memory from the option in `onStart`; make `cooldownFor` fall back to the same option when the reference unit has no stack memory.

`onAbilityContact` already occurs before parry/spell-shield guards, but after untargetability rejection. It can record passive grants from negated contacts. `onAbilityHit` alone is insufficient. Ensure exactly one grant per qualifying ability contact; do not award additional grants for artificial helper abilities or each repeated cage check. A safe implementation order is a normal connected grant after its packet, with defended contacts handled in the contact hook; pin the same-hit AP update order as an assumption because the passive source does not state it. Avoid applying +1 AP before calculating the same spell's damage by accident. Untargetable targets produce no contact and should receive no stack under the ordinary hit semantics. The source does not establish whether E's non-CC kill-credit marker by itself qualifies as a passive hit; keep that question explicit.

Takedown accounting must deduplicate champion death and account for the existing `fallen`/Sion continuation distinction if supported. The simple duel terminates at ordinary champion death, so the AP gained at that moment usually cannot affect the score; still implement/count it correctly for source fidelity and post-death tests.
