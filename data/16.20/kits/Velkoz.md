## Innate: Organic Deconstruction

Source: [Template:Data Vel'Koz/Organic Deconstruction](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Vel'Koz/Organic_Deconstruction?oldid=3986690) (revision 3986690). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies, Self · Damage type: True · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: unknown · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Vel'Koz's abilities apply a stack of Deconstruction to enemies hit for 7 seconds, refreshing on basic attacks on-hit and subsequent applications and stacking up to 3 times.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

The third stack consumes them all to deal 35 – 180 (based on level) (+ 60% AP) bonus true damage.

Notes:

- The stack duration will not be refreshed if the attack misses due to blind.
- Interaction between duration refresh on basic attacks and parry effects (dodge and block).
