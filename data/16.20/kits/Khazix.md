## Innate: Unseen Threat

Source: [Template:Data Kha'Zix/Unseen Threat](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Kha'Zix/Unseen_Threat?oldid=4064849) (revision 4064849). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Kha'Zix gains Unseen Threat whenever the enemy loses sight of him or he activates Void Assault. Unseen Threat: Kha'Zix empowers his next basic attack against an enemy champion to deal 22 – 141 (based on level) (+ 50% bonus AD) bonus magic damage and slow them by 25% for 2 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** reveal; slow (2 s, 25%)

Notes:

- Any form of vision loss may trigger Unseen Threat, such as Curse of the Black Mist, nearsight and Brushmaker.
- Void Assault grants Unseen Threat even if Kha'Zix never becomes unseen (e.g. affected by true sight).
    - Other stealth such as E does not do this.
- Both the attack's damage and bonus spell damage are grouped under the same cast instance.
    - Because of this, a single Unseen Threat attack does not trigger two Electrocute stacks.

## R: Void Assault

Source: [Template:Data Kha'Zix/Void Assault](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Kha'Zix/Void_Assault?oldid=3969560) (revision 3969560). Ranks: 3.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Cooldown | 100 / 85 / 70 |
| Cost | 100 mana |
| Cast time | none |
| Cooldown starts | on-cast |

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Each rank in Void Assault allows Kha'Zix to evolve one of his abilities, granting it additional effects. Evolving an ability causes him to enter a 2-second cast time. Kha'Zix cannot evolve while he is affected by cast-inhibiting crowd control or a lockout that disables abilities.

Active: Kha'Zix becomes invisible for 1.25 seconds, during which he gains 40% bonus movement speed.

- **Mechanics:** invisible (1.25 s)

After 2 seconds of leaving invisibility, and for the next 12 seconds, Void Assault can be recast at no additional cost.

- **Mechanics:** invisible (12 s)

Recast: Kha'Zix mimics the first cast's effects.

Notes:

- Each cast counts as an ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- Kha'Zix receives the evolution even if he dies while in cast time.
- Every time Void Assault is ranked, a secondary menu will pop up for Kha'Zix to select an ability to evolve, this can only be done once per ability.
    - The only way for Kha'Zix to evolve all of his abilities is to gain the fourth evolution point by being victorious in The Hunt is On! (by scoring a takedown on Rengar).
- Using a basic attack breaks stealth at the end of the attack windup.
