## Q: Poison Trail

Source: [Template:Data Singed/Poison Trail](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Singed/Poison_Trail?oldid=4006737) (revision 4006737). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Toggle: Singed continually creates a toxic cloud in his wake that lingers for 3.25 seconds. The cloud inflicts poison to enemies within. Poison Trail is toggled off automatically if Singed becomes unable to pay its mana cost.

- **Mechanics:** damage over time

poison: The target takes magic damage every 0.25 seconds over 2 seconds as well as upon being hit if not currently affected. Subsequent inflictions refresh the duration.

- **Magic Damage per Tick:** 5 / 7.5 / 10 / 12.5 / 15 (+ 10.625% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 15 (+ 10.625% AP)
- **Magic Damage per Second:** 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 (+ 42.5% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 60 (+ 42.5% AP)
- **Minimum Magic Damage:** 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 / 120 (+ 85% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 120 (+ 85% AP)
- **Mechanics:** damage over time

Singed earns the kill credit of enemy minions that are poisoned and would die to the damage of allied minions within the time before the next damage instance.

- **Mechanics:** damage over time

Notes:

- Toggled abilities do not count as ability activations for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- When Poison Trail's debuff is applied instead of refreshed, the target is dealt one additional instance of the debuff at the same time as the application. This is done by delaying the actual debuff by 0.1 seconds.
    - If the target is already affected by the debuff, its duration is refreshed to the maximum (2 seconds).
- While active after spawning, a cloud checks for valid targets who remain in or re-enter a cloud every 0.25 seconds.
    - Valid targets already afflicted with the poison will have their debuff's duration refreshed every 0.25 seconds if applicable.
- If Singed has moved fewer than 90 units since spawning the last poison cloud, it will spawn 35 units in front of him. They also only spawn every 1 second in this case.
    - When Singed is moving faster than that, the poison clouds spawn more frequently and on top of himself.

- **Slow:** 50 / 55 / 60 / 65 / 70% → at max rank, level 20: 70%
- **Mechanics:** ground; slow (70%)

Notes:

- The goo missile is a projectile. It has a fixed travel time but is independent from when the zone is established. It is destructible by effects such as Wind Wall and will destroy item spell shields if the goo missile is thrown past an enemy champion.
- Mega Adhesive's slow and ground debuffs are each marked as non-dispellable, so they are not removed by most cleanses. Each is however allowed to be removed by cleanses that also grant immunity to the debuff type, such as Ragnarok.

- **Mechanics:** airborne

- **Root Duration:** 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 2 s
- **Mechanics:** root (2 s)

Notes:

- During Fling cast time (the windup not during the actual fling) the enemy's flash is already locked, making it a non reactable spell.
- Fling can throw enemies over walls (circumstances permitting).
- Slow-immune enemies will not be rooted when flung into Mega Adhesive.
- Fling is special cased to not fling Warwick while Singed is suppressed by Infinite Duress after the cast time.
- If the target becomes untargetable, dies, or is too far away or no longer in sight during the cast time, this ability will cancel but does not go on cooldown nor pay its cost (if applicable).

### Assumptions

- Poison Trail is on from the start of every fight.
- Mega Adhesive's slow and ground are renewed every 0.25 s while the enemy is inside.
- Singed throws Mega Adhesive where Fling will land the enemy, then flings it in; otherwise he saves Mega Adhesive while Fling is ready.
