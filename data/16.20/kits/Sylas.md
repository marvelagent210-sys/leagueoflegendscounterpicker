## Innate: Petricite Burst

Source: [Template:Data Sylas/Petricite Burst](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Sylas/Petricite_Burst?oldid=4061737) (revision 4061737). Ranks: 1.

| Effect radius | 300 |

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Whenever Sylas casts an ability, he generates a stack of Unshackled for 4 seconds, refreshing on subsequent casts and stacking up to 3 times.

Unshackled: Sylas next basic attack gains 125% bonus attack speed and is empowered to have an uncancellable windup and consume a stack to whirl his chains around him, dealing 130% AD (+ 30% AP) magic damage to the primary target and 40% AD (+ 20% AP) magic damage to nearby enemies.

- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup

Unshackled deals 115% damage to monsters, and executes minions that are secondary targets and would be left below 25 health.

Petricite Burst can critically strike for 175% damage only against the primary target.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Notes:

- If Unshackled has less than 0.5 seconds remaining and Sylas begins an attack, its duration will refresh to 1 second to prevent it from expiring during the windup.
- Spellblade damage does not get converted to magic damage, and will deal its damage only to the primary target.
- Applies basic damage to the primary target and area damage to secondary targets.
- Unshackled stacks are granted at the end of the abilities' cast time.
- Petricite Burst's damage cannot critically strike against secondary targets.
- Petricite Burst only applies on-hit effects to the primary target.
- Petricite Burst attacks benefit from life steal.
- Petricite Burst can be dodged, blocked, or missed while Sylas is blinded as the primary target but not as a secondary target.
- Petricite Burst's damage incorrectly critically strikes for 175% damage rather than 200%.
