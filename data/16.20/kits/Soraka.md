### Assumptions

- Salvation only sees actual living allied Champion-class bodies below40%maximumHP within2500center-distance and in Soraka's facing direction. No ally is fabricated in a solo duel. A coincident body supplies no direction. The90%bonus remains subject to ordinary movement speed soft caps.
- Starcall uses primary225+35%AP damage,800range and265center-radius. Its post-cast landing delay interpolates the pinned50-to800range series from0.25to1second. A committed fixed-time projectile projects the unknown sky trajectory onto the declared ground axis for destruction/interception; the binary1100speed and210radius do not replace primary timing/geometry. A real champion contact, including a spell-shielded one, creates at most one1400speed return per star. Parry, untargetability and a destroyed initial projectile grant no return. Returning dust follows Soraka's actual center; interception/destruction denies self Rejuvenation. Already launched stars can finish after death but cannot rejuvenate dead or resurrecting Soraka. Ground sight has no supplied vision radius and is not converted to true sight.
- Rejuvenation lasts2.5seconds, heals12times at0.2second intervals and uses the retained raw note's approximate four15%,four5.5%andfour4.5%distribution, preserving exactly the primary total120+30%AP; the equal Heal-per-Tick table conflicts with that distribution. Its30%movement bonus decays linearly. Heal amounts snapshot current SorakaAP separately when each recipient buff is granted, while recipient modifiers are read at each pulse; an alternative server AP snapshot moment is unpublished. Actual ally buffs continue after Soraka's death through independent recipient clocks; refresh replaces an older clock. Primary notes allow allied Rejuvenation immediately at champion contact before the return arrives: that early sharing window lasts one sourced buff duration and is refreshed on self receipt, an explicit boundary where the server's separate sharing flag duration is unpublished.
- Astral Infusion targets only an actual eligible ally or Champion-class decoy within550, with the sourced175forgiveness retained as evidence rather than extending declaration range. Mana and10%maximumHP are paid once on declaration, the latter reduced100%at maximum W rank while self Rejuvenation has actually arrived; it clamps health at1and accepts a cast at exactly5%HP despite insufficient health for the nominal cost. The ally heal and shared Rejuvenation occur before the0.25second cast lock. Self is never substituted for an absent ally, and all healing modifiers belong to the recipient.
- Equinox uses primary925cast range rather than binary875and the retained260center-radius for silence and damage/root checks; primary says the true damage/root radius is slightly smaller but supplies no exact boundary. Initial and1.5second eruption damage/root affect actual enemy Champion-class bodies; continuous non-dispellable silence can affect other real enemy bodies, ignores spell shields and remains subject to true CC immunity/Black Shield/parry.
