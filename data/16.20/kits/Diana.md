## Innate: Moonsilver Blade

Source: [Template:Data Diana/Moonsilver Blade](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Diana/Moonsilver_Blade?oldid=4047026) (revision 4047026). Ranks: 1.

| Effect radius | 175 |

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Diana gains 15 – 35 (based on level)% bonus attack speed. After casting an ability, this bonus is tripled to 45 – 105 (based on level)% for 5 seconds.

Innate - Moonsilver Blade: Diana's basic attacks on-hit generate a stack of Moonsilver Blade for 5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent attacks and stacking up to 2 times. At 2 stacks, Diana empowers her next basic attack to consume the stacks on-hit to additionally cleave nearby enemies, dealing them 20 – 220 (based on level) (+ 50% AP) magic damage. Moonsilver Blade deals 270% damage against monsters.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit; on-hit

Notes:

- Moonsilver Blade will affect structures.
- Each attack has a distinct animation and she will glow when the empowered attack is ready.
- When the stacks are consumed on-hit (e.g Guinsoo's Rageblade, Dusk and Dawn), additional damage is only applied to the on-hit target, without triggering the cleave on nearby enemies. [https://www.reddit.com/r/DianaMains/comments/u7hlez/guinsoos_rageblade_interaction_with_dianas/]
- Sometimes, the empowered basic attack will not consume the stacks.
    - In this case, the attack will also not deal its damage.

## W: Pale Cascade

Active: Diana grants herself a shield for up to 5 seconds and creates three spheres that orbit her counterclockwise for the same duration, each detonating upon contact with an enemy to deal magic damage to nearby enemies.

- **Shield Strength:** 45 / 60 / 75 / 90 / 105 (+ 30% AP) (+ 14% bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 105 (+ 30% AP) (+ 14% bonusHealth)
- **Magic Damage per Orb:** 20 / 32 / 44 / 56 / 68 (+ 18% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 68 (+ 18% AP)
- **Total Magic Damage:** 60 / 96 / 132 / 168 / 204 (+ 54% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 204 (+ 54% AP)
- **Mechanics:** shield (5 s)

If all three spheres detonate, Pale Cascade's shield is reapplied, stacking with its original shield and refreshing the duration.

- **Maximum Shield Strength:** 90 / 120 / 150 / 180 / 210 (+ 60% AP) (+ 28% bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 210 (+ 60% AP) (+ 28% bonusHealth)

Notes:

- On the second application of Pale Cascade's shield, Shield Power will apply to both the new shield amount and the shield amount remaining from the first shield, which has already benefited from shield power.
    - Because of this, shield power effectively applies to the second shield twice, but with reduced efficiency, for up to an increase of 50% of shield power (maximum benefit if no damage was mitigated by the first shield). With 10% shield power, the second shield's total amount will be increased by up to an additional 5%, for a total of 15.5% bonus shield.
    - The formula for the total amount of shield Diana will receive from both shield applications is:
      - Total Shield = (((Shield Amount × (1 + Shield Power)) - Damage Blocked by Shield) + Shield Amount) × (1 + Shield Power)

Active: Diana dashes to the target enemy's location, and upon completion, she deals them magic damage and consumes Moonlight from all enemies. If the target is within 400 range, Diana will dash through their location.

- **Magic Damage:** 50 / 70 / 90 / 110 / 130 (+ 60% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 130 (+ 60% AP)
- **Mechanics:** dash

- If Lunar Rush is blocked by spell shield the Moonlight debuff is still consumed but Lunar Rush's cooldown is not reset.
