Active: Viktor deploys a gravity field at the target location for 4.5 seconds. After a 0.75-second delay, it activates to slow enemies within for 1 second, refreshing every 0.25 seconds while they remain inside.

- **Slow:** 33 / 36 / 39 / 42 / 45% → at max rank, level 20: 45%
- **Mechanics:** slow (1 s, 45%)

The field applies a stack of Heavy Gravity to enemies within every 0.25 seconds, stacking up to 5 times. The fifth stack consumes them all to knock down and stun the target for 1.5 seconds. The knockdown and stun can be triggered only once per cast per enemy.

- **Mechanics:** knockdown (1.5 s); stun (1.5 s)

Augment: Magnetize — Viktor's other abilities, excluding Arcane Storm persistent damage, slow enemies hit by 20% for 1 second.

- **Mechanics:** slow (1 s, 20%)

Notes:

- Gravity Field applies a single debuff for both the slow and the stack.
    - Targets can be applied further stacks after having their stacks consumed for the first time.
      - Stacks are always consumed upon the fifth stack, but only the first instance of stacks being consumed can trigger the crowd control effects.
    - The debuff lasts for 1 second.
- Gravity Field will remain active if Viktor dies.
- Gravity Field's animation can be seen by both teams through fog of war.
- If an enemy enters the area of effect while untargetable, they may generate stacks even after becoming stunned once, causing them to get stunned up-to 3 times by a single Gravity Field.

## R: Arcane Storm

Targeting: Location, Unit · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Viktor conjures an arcane singularity at the target location, dealing magic damage to enemies within the area and disrupting their channeled abilities.

- **Magic Damage:** 100 / 175 / 250 (+ 50% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 250 (+ 50% AP)
- **Mechanics:** disrupt; channel

The singularity then remains active as a moving storm for 6.5 seconds, striking a bolt of electricity onto enemies within it every second to deal magic damage, as well as granting sight of the area within its radius and the surrounding area. The singularity will target the nearest champion hit by its initial damage and will follow them. The singularity moves faster based on its proximity to Viktor.

- **Magic Damage Per Tick:** 65 / 105 / 145 (+ 35% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 145 (+ 35% AP)
- **Total Magic Damage:** 490 / 805 / 1120 (+ 260% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 1120 (+ 260% AP)
- **Mechanics:** reveal

Arcane Storm can be recast at any time while the singularity is active.

Recast: Viktor directs the singularity to the target location, or to follow the target enemy champion or himself for its remaining duration.

Augment: Perfect Storm — The singularity moves 25% faster. Additionally, whenever an enemy champion is taken down after being damaged by Arcane Storm, the storm's size is increased by 40% and its duration is extended by 3 seconds. The storm's radius can be increased up to 6 times and the duration can be extended only up to the original duration.

Arcane Storm's recast can be used while affected by cast-inhibiting crowd control.

- **Perfect Storm Radius:** 325 / 455 / 585 / 715 / 845 / 975 / 1105 (based on number of takedowns)

- **Storm Sight Radius:** 700 or Storm radius

- **Storm Speed:** 200 / 208.333 / 216.667 / 225 / 233.333 / 241.667 / 250 / 258.333 / 266.667 / 275 / 283.333 / 291.667 / 300 (based on Distance)

- **Perfect Storm Speed:** 250 / 260.417 / 270.833 / 281.25 / 291.667 / 302.083 / 312.5 / 322.917 / 333.333 / 343.75 / 354.167 / 364.583 / 375 (based on Distance)

Notes:

- Viktor can command the singularity regardless of how far away he is from it.
- The singularity will remain active even if Viktor dies and will automatically follow the nearest enemy champion in range at the time of his death, if it wasn't locked on already.
    - The singularity will only be issued to automatically follow a nearby champion once; it will not lock on to a new target if its current target becomes invalid.
    - Any commands it was issued prior to his death will not be interrupted.
- Spell shield will only block the initial damage.
- If Perfect Storm's singularity scores the killing blow on an enemy champion with the initial damage, it will spawn with the increased size.
    - If multiple champions are dealt the killing blow by the initial hit, the singularity will spawn with the increased size respective to the number of champions killed.
- The base duration allows for 7 strikes (including the initial one).
- The storm will follow its target even if they are not visible, effectively giving away their position.
- Arcane Storm is not obstructed by terrain.
- The disrupt is 'wrapped' into a status effect that causes affected champions to display the "Silenced" UI on their health bar for 0.25 seconds, without actually applying the disabling effects of the silence crowd control type (functions as an indicator of the target becoming disrupted). This however ensures that the disrupt is also prevented by effects that would negate silences (such as crowd control immunity).
- After becoming augmented, an alternate icon is used for the recast of Arcane Storm.
- The champion that Arcane Storm is following will have an overhead indicator.
