## Innate: Runic Blade

Source: [Template:Data Riven/Runic Blade](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Riven/Runic_Blade?oldid=3989876) (revision 3989876). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: physical · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Riven's ability casts generate a stack of Charge for 6 seconds, refreshing on subsequent casts and stacking up to 3 times. Riven's basic attacks are empowered to each consume a stack to deal bonus physical damage equal to 30 – 45 (based on level)% AD, reduced to 50% against structures. Against monsters, it also deals 50 additional bonus physical damage.

The bonus damage is affected by critical strike modifiers and applies life steal at 100% effectiveness.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike; life steal; life steal

Notes:

- Runic Blade's duration also refreshes when Riven consumes a stack.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against wards.
- The number of empowered attacks available is represented by a counter under her health bar, only visible to the player.
- The bonus damage to monsters also benefits from critical strike modifiers. The full formula is shown below.
    - (1.0 AD × (1 + AD ratio) + monster damage) × (100% × critical damage)

## Q: Broken Wings

Targeting: Auto, Unit · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Active: Riven can activate Broken Wings three times before the ability goes on cooldown, with a 0.3125-second static cooldown between casts. If Riven does not recast the ability within 4 seconds of the previous cast, it goes on cooldown.

Riven slashes with her sword for each of the three casts, dealing physical damage to enemies struck within an area, resetting her basic attack timer, and ordering her to basic attack the target of Broken Wings if there are any.

- **Physical Damage:** 45 / 75 / 105 / 135 / 165 (+ 60 / 65 / 70 / 75 / 80% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 165 (+ 80% bonusAD)
- **Total Physical Damage:** 135 / 225 / 315 / 405 / 495 (+ 180 / 195 / 210 / 225 / 240% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 495 (+ 240% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** attack reset

First Cast: Riven dashes 225 units in the direction she is currently facing, or up to 225 units towards the target enemy, striking enemies in the target area 100 units away. This cast cannot cross terrain.

- **Mechanics:** dash

Second Cast: Riven mimics the first cast's effects.

Third Cast: Riven mimics the first cast's effects in a larger area while also knocking back enemies hit 75 units over 0.5 seconds. This cast can cross terrain.

- **Mechanics:** airborne (0.5 s)

Notes:

- This ability always employs quick cast.
- Each cast counts as a single ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- Moving after using Broken Wings allows Riven to do other actions faster.
- The Third Cast prevents Riven from using basic attacks and abilities for longer than the other 2 casts.
- The Third Cast may have an extended dash range when crossing terrain.
- The Third Cast's knockup debuff is not removed early if the forced movement is interrupted.
- Any forced movement affecting the target being knocked up is interrupted when the knockup debuff is removed.
