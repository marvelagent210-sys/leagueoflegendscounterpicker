Innate: Xayah's ability casts generate 3 stacks of Clean Cuts, lasting for 8 seconds, refreshing on subsequent casts, and stacking up to 5 times. Xayah's basic attacks are empowered to each consume a stack on-attack to instead shoot a Feather that deals the triggering attack's damage to the primary target and 35 / 45 / 55 (based on level)% AD physical damage to other enemies hit. The secondary target damage can critically strike for 200% damage if the triggering attack does.

- **Mechanics:** on-attack; critical strike

Innate - Lover's Leap: If either Xayah or Rakan is channeling Recall, the other may move nearby and channel their own to join alongside them. Both reach base at the time of which the initiator's Recall completes.

Notes:

- Feathers will stop upon being intercepted by projectile-blocking effects.
    - : Missile popping before and after striking the primary target.
- After striking their target, the attack will proceed in a line in the same direction it was already flying.
    - If the target is more than 1000 units from Xayah when the empowered attack hits them, the attack will not keep flying in a line and drop the Feather at their location immediately.
    - Unlike similar effects, the homing and line trajectories use the same missile.
- All her Feathers on the ground will be destroyed when Xayah dies, and while she is dead, missiles that are still in flight will not spawn new ones, either.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against structures nor wards.
    - The duration will be refreshed when starting the attack windup against them.
- The number of Clean Cuts stacks available is represented by a feather counter in her ammo bar, visible to the player only.

### Assumptions

- Clean Cuts stores scalar per-world stacks, starts empty, grants 3 per completed Q/W/E/R cast, caps at 5 and expires 8 seconds after the last grant. A stack is consumed at windup declaration even if that attack later misses or is cancelled. The primary remains a homing ordinary attack at 4000 speed; after actual contact, the same logical feather continues along the current axis to 1000 from Xayah's impact-time position, without hitting that primary twice. Projectile-wall destruction prevents a new planted feather. If the primary has moved beyond 1000, it plants at the primary immediately. Basic blind/dodge/parry still deny primary damage while an already launched physical feather can continue.
- Planted feathers last 6 seconds, have individual ground positions and expire exclusively at their deadline. All planted feathers are removed at actual lethal death, and outbound projectiles cannot plant new feathers while Xayah is dead. A previously launched E return may still deal damage. No imaginary Rakan, recall companion, ward, structure or item interaction is created.
- Double Daggers has max(0.1, 0.25 − 0.07 × bonus AS) cast time from the exact raw-template formula, with ordinary 30 Hz rounding. Two 3500 speed piercing daggers use the version-pinned root DataValues; the separate 400 speed missile objects appear inconsistent, so the runtime choice is disclosed. Their relative 0.25 second spacing is the difference between the two pinned animation-delay values; first launch is at cast completion. Both use the 100 width body on the duel axis; lateral origin offsets and the unsized initial formation-area contact are not invented. Each dagger independently falls to 50% damage after its first actual enemy contact, and a spell shield blocks only that dagger.
- Deadly Plumage gives 55% AS for 4 seconds. Successful attacks declared during it launch a separate 3000 speed 25%-of-basic-physical strike and grant 30% MS for 1.5 seconds on champion contact; its raw basic damage inherits the triggering attack's averaged critical factor without applying on-hits or critting a second time. W cast during an already flying attack adds the strike but not movement speed. The supplemental missile is launched from Xayah on the primary's successful contact; exact undocumented script launch geometry is an approximation. Blind, dodge, parry and damage block conservatively prevent the supplemental strike; the wiki leaves dodge/block interactions unresolved.
- Bladecaller requires an actual unexpired planted feather, costs 20, waits the pinned 0.25 second return delay and returns each feather at 4000 speed toward Xayah's current position. Feathers already in flight at E declaration are tagged and recall only after landing, while subsequently launched feathers are excluded. Each actual return checks independent projectile walls, spell shields, parry and spatial overlap. Accepted contacts receive per-target/per-E falloff 100/95/90…floor 10%, with 1 + 0.5 × critChance × (critDamage − 1) exact pinned scaling, and the third accepted feather roots 1.25 seconds. Only pets explicitly classified as minions by their pinned pet records receive the additional 50% minion multiplier; unknown summons and champion clones are not guessed to be minions. A spell-shielded feather is conservatively excluded from the three accepted-hit counter because the available note specifies only a single blocked damage instance. Champion sidesteps are expected shares per return; the combination of every fan feather is not independently branch-enumerated.
- Featherstorm keeps its 1.5 second untargetable/ghosted/no-attacks/no-other-casts mobile phase and fires after 1 second. The pinned client RUntargetable value is 1.25, a documented conflict resolved in favor of the explicit wiki 1.5. Five feathers are equally spaced across the sourced 30 degree cone, an explicit geometry approximation, with 1000 effective outward length and 4000 speed. Actual 2D fan positions are retained for recall, while champion movement remains on the duel axis. Each target receives at most one R damage packet; this prevents the five projectiles becoming five times the listed damage. The unsourced rear launch offset behind Xayah is not invented; the wiki 1060/1000 effective-reach distinction remains a limitation.
- The budget includes only capped W attack-speed uptime and its 25% strike, two Q damage packets per cycle, and a finite E feather supply from Q, capped Clean Cuts attacks and only one central R feather. Its E reference applies the real diminishing-return sum and critical factor, excludes fake preplanted feathers, and does not value guaranteed five-feather fan overlap or allied Rakan damage.
