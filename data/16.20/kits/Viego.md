## Innate: Sovereign's Domination

Source: [Template:Data Viego/Sovereign's Domination](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Viego/Sovereign's_Domination?oldid=4063480) (revision 4063480). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: – · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: false · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Whenever Viego scores an enemy champion takedown while alive and within 3 seconds of damaging them, a Mist Wraith spawns from that champion's corpse for 8 seconds. Viego can basic attack a Mist Wraith to consume it: causing him to blink to its location after winding up for 1 second, during which he is untargetable (except to turrets) and unable to act. He then gains Possession and heals himself for 2% (+ 2.5% per 100 bonus AD) (+ 2% per 100 AP) (+ 0.4% per 100 bonus health) (+ 5% per 100% bonus attack speed) of the target's maximum health.

- **Mechanics:** blink; untargetable (1 s); lockout; heal

Possession: For 10 seconds, Viego assumes the enemy champion's form and gains their basic attacks, basic abilities, items (excluding trinket items), and base stats (based on Viego's level). He also gains a free cast of Heartbreaker, which is initially placed on a 1.5-second cooldown, and 10% bonus movement speed while facing enemy champions within 2500 units.

Viego cannot use the possessed champion's item actives or consumable items, and is unable to use the shop during Possession. Casting Heartbreaker or attacking a Mist Wraith ends Possession immediately.

Viego will preserve his current health percentage between transformations.

Notes:

- Viego will copy the possessed champion's ability ranks.
    - Viego cannot level up their abilities with his available skill points.
- Mist Wraiths will expire when Viego dies, and they will not expire while he is winding up on them.
- Viego gains the bonus movement speed during possession even while moving towards nearby enemy champions that are untargetable.
- Finishing the possessing attack against a Mist Wraith kills it, rewarding Viego an extra 3 as he becomes the target champion.
- The wind up's untargetability does not destroy in-flight projectiles from turrets.
- The possessed champion's abilities do not cost any mana or energy even if that specific champion normally uses either as a casting resource.
    - Alternative resources, such as Fury, Flow, or Health, will still be utilized.
    - When possessing Akali, Viego is given 10,000 energy. This causes his secondary resource bar to render incorrectly.
- The current cooldowns of Viego's abilities will not be reset between transformations.
    - Those of the possessed champion's abilities will be reset between transformations.
    - This does not apply to the following long-cooldown abilities. Viego specifically tracks their proper cooldowns which can be seen via a buff in his hotbar for each when applicable.
      - Rebirth
      - Cell Division
      - Viego will not track Cultivation of War cooldown.
- The passive cooldowns of items are shared between Viego's and the possessed champion's.
    - This does not apply to Lifeline items.
- Once possession ends, Viego returns to his normal state and certain effects gained from the possessed champion's abilities will be immediately removed.
    - Some buffs will be kept and some buffs will be lost.
      - Viego will almost always keep shields and spell shields.
      - Passive effects will be lost by default, as Viego no longer has their abilities.
    - Channeled and charged abilities maintained will be interrupted, and without granting any cancelation refunds to the respective slot (if applicable).
    - Toggled abilities will be automatically toggled off.
- When taking possession of Kled, Viego's current health percentage will become Kled's health percentage, while Skaarl health percentage will be the same as when he remounts.
    - Once possession ends, Viego only keeps Kled health percentage as his current.
- Viego retains his own summoner spells, trinket item, and runes during possession.
- Mist Wraiths will still spawn while Viego is in a zombie state.
- Sovereign's Domination will not summon a Mist Wraith from clones.
- The possession will not expire while Viego is in a zombie state.
    - While in a zombie state, Heartbreaker is disabled.
    - If Viego enters a zombie state while winding up on a Mist Wraith, the possession will still last its normal duration.
- The possession will not end prematurely if Viego enters resurrection.
    - The possession will end if it expires while Viego is under Cultivation of War resurrection; he will lose all its effects instantly and be revived without restoring the complete amount of health.
- The possession will acquire the basic abilities of shapeshifting champions (e.g. Spider Form / Human Form, Transform Mercury Cannon / Transform Mercury Hammer, and Aspect of the Cougar) based on which form they died as.
- Mist Wraiths are considered minions and can only be targeted by Viego.
- If Viego possesses an enemy champion who owns Dark Seal or Mejai's Soulstealer, he will gain their Glory stacks during the possession.
- As Cull tracks its progress for each player even if they do not possess the item at the time, Viego may discharge stacks for the benefit of 1 each per minion kill, and if Viego killed 100 minions possessing Cull, no matter whether bought himself or acquired via a possessed champion's inventory, he receives the 350 payout and can no longer discharge stacks.
    - This entire interaction does not interact with the state of the Cull stacks of the enemy.
    - The stacks shown on the item in the inventory and scoreboard may not update properly, however.
- If Viego possesses an enemy champion who owns Tear of the Goddess, the stacks it has will be transferred to his Tear of the Goddess if he owns one.
- If Viego possesses an enemy Bel'Veth, his attack speed cap will be modified to 90 permanently.
- If Viego posesses an enemy Evelynn, he will never activate the healing from Demon Shade.
- If Viego possesses an enemy KDA Evelynn, he will still hear Demon Shade music upon discarding the possession if he does it while active.
- If Viego targets a Mist Wraith while already untargetable he will become targetable once again during the cast time.
- If Viego possesses an enemy on Howling Abyss, he will be able to use the shop upon returning to the fountain and discarding the possession.
- If Viego posesses an enemy Naafiri, he will keep her Packmates upon discarding the possession.
- If Viego possesses an enemy Fiora and the possession expires while performing Riposte, Spectral Maw will be placed on Riposte's cooldown.
- If Viego possesses an enemy Rek'Sai, Tunnels he creates will persist after discarding the possession. These Tunnels cannot be destroyed by standing on their entrance, and do not expire as time passes.

## Q: Blade of the Ruined King

Source: [Template:Data Viego/Blade of the Ruined King](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Viego/Blade_of_the_Ruined_King?oldid=4067448) (revision 4067448). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Direction · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: false · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Viego's basic attacks deal bonus physical damage on-hit. This damage can critically strike for 170% damage, has a minimum threshold and is capped at 80 against monsters.

- **Bonus Physical Damage:** 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6% of target's current health → at max rank, level 20: 6% of targetCurrentHealth
- **Critical Bonus Physical Damage:** 3.4 / 5.1 / 6.8 / 8.5 / 10.2% of target's current health → at max rank, level 20: 10.2% of targetCurrentHealth
- **Minimum Bonus Damage:** 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 → at max rank, level 20: 30
- **Mechanics:** on-hit; critical strike

Additionally, his damaging abilities apply a mark to enemies hit for 4 seconds. Viego's next basic attack against a marked target is empowered to consume the mark on-hit to strike a second time, dealing 20% AD (+ 15% AP) physical damage. He also heals himself for a portion of the post-mitigation damage dealt by the second strike: 100% against minions, 125% against monsters, and 150% against other targets.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit; heal

The second strike applies on-hit effects and can critically strike for 200% damage.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit; critical strike

Active: Viego stabs in a line in the target direction that deals physical damage to enemies hit, increased by critical strike damage (based on critical strike chance).

- **Physical Damage:** 25 / 40 / 55 / 70 / 85 (+ 70% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 85 (+ 70% AD)

Blade of the Ruined King's passive remains under Possession, and while transformed, the second strike is instead converted to bonus damage on-hit which no longer applies on-hit effects but can still critically strike (based on the Possession's stats, abilities and/or item effects).

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Notes:

- Blade of the Ruined King deals:
    - Area damage for the thrust.
    - Basic damage for the second strike.
    - Proc damage for the on-hit bonus damage.
- Ability damage that applies on-hit effects (e.g., R damage against the primary target) cannot apply the mark but can consume it, as a basic attack would.
- The second strike rolls critical strikes independently from the primary attack.
- This ability's cast time respects the attack speed floor and cap.
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.
- Viego will not perform the second strike if the first attack was dodged, blocked, and missed.
- If the second strike hits a dead unit, Viego will still heal based on the post-mitigation damage that he would deal with it.
- The attacks do not deal the bonus damage against structures.
- The mark does not cause Viego to attack twice. Effects that trigger on-attack or interact with the attack windup will only trigger once and thus do not interact with Blade of the Ruined King. (e.g. Lethal Tempo, Hail of Blades).

While inside the mist, Viego gains bonus attack speed. If he does not attack or cast abilities, he also gains bonus movement speed and camouflage.

    - The stealth regain timer is refreshed from basic attacks at the start of their attack windup.

## R: Heartbreaker

Source: [Template:Data Viego/Heartbreaker](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Viego/Heartbreaker?oldid=4001566) (revision 4001566). Ranks: 3.

Targeting: Location · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Viego gains displacement immunity over the cast time, then blinks to the target location. Upon arrival, he strikes the most wounded enemy champion nearby, dealing physical damage, slowing them by 99% for 0.25 seconds, and applying on-hit effects. Other nearby enemies are knocked back up to 400-units away based on proximity, though not through terrain.

- **Physical Damage:** 12 / 16 / 20% (+ 5% per 100 bonus AD) of target's missing health → at max rank, level 20: 20% of targetMissingHealth (+ 5% per100:bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** displacement-immune; blink; slow (0.25 s, 99%); on-hit; airborne

All targets hit are dealt 120% AD physical damage, increased by critical strike damage (based on critical strike chance).

If a Possession is discarded, Heartbreaker will be placed on a 1.5-second cooldown if it is not already on cooldown.

Notes:

- Heartbreaker will cast at the edge of its range if the target location is further beyond.
- Heartbreaker will cast at maximum range from the end of Spectral Maw dash if it is cast during the dash.
- The knockback speed is 1000 units per second. The airborne is removed as soon as the displacement ends.
    - The maximum airborne duration is therefore 0.4 seconds. The minimum is 0.1 seconds.
- To the struck champion deals basic damage, but also triggers spell effects by also being tagged as spell damage; to all other enemies, deals area damage.
- Viego will enter a 0.25-second cast time if he strikes an enemy champion.
    - After this cast time, he will automatically receive an attack command against that enemy. Previous movement inputs from the player are ignored.
- Heartbreaker's strike to the champion can be dodged and blocked, but not missed.
- Viego will be ordered to basic attack the primary target afterwards.
