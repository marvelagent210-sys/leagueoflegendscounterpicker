Targeting: Auto · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: false · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Rammus rolls into an accelerating ball, channeling for up to 6 seconds to gain 25 – 39.1667 (based on level)% bonus total movement speed per second over the duration, up to a maximum of 150 – 235 (based on level)%. Powerball can be recast after 1 second within the duration, and does so automatically afterwards or upon casting Defensive Ball Curl.

- **Mechanics:** channel (6 s)

Rammus stops rolling upon colliding with an enemy, dealing magic damage to all nearby enemies and knocking them back 125 units, though not through terrain. Enemies hit are then stunned and revealed for 0.4 seconds, as well as slowed for 1 second.

- **Magic Damage:** 80 / 120 / 160 / 200 / 240 (+ 100% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 240 (+ 100% AP)
- **Slow:** 40 / 50 / 60 / 70 / 80% → at max rank, level 20: 80%
- **Mechanics:** airborne; stun (0.4 s); reveal (0.4 s); slow (1 s, 80%)

Recast: Rammus ends Powerball.

Notes:

- The initial cast and the manual recast count as ability activations for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
    - If the ability is not manually recasted, the secondary effect will trigger without being considered as an ability activation.
- The bonus movement speed stacks multiplicatively with other sources of movement speed boosts.
- Powerball will still collide with enemies if Rammus is ghosted.
- For the purpose of moving closer to an enemy when right clicking them, Powerball also reduces Rammus attack range to 0 for the duration.
    - Regardless of this, during Powerball Rammus will also always attempt to path towards his target's center instead.
- Rammus will ignore unit collision for the purpose of pathfinding during Powerball.
    - Rammus acts as if he were ghosted for the duration of Powerball.
- Powerball is not a movement channel, and so will not be interrupted by root and ground.
- Displacement immunity will also resist the application of the stun.

### Assumptions

- Powerball is a mobile channel, paths straight toward the only enemy in one dimension, and accelerates continuously using the wiki's level-20 40.8333% bonus total movement speed per second, capped at 245%. The game-file MinimumMoveSpeed extension differs slightly (40.7588% at level 20); the pinned wiki progression is used.
- Powerball's 125-unit knockback takes 0.25 seconds because the source does not specify travel duration; its recorded estimated 0.4-second stun begins with the collision. Displacement immunity prevents this stun as well as the knockback, but leaves damage and slow intact.
- Soaring Slam uses the source's base 800 range and minimum 900 dash speed. The source also lists movement-speed-dependent range and 2000 maximum speed without a complete dash-speed formula; those extensions are not inferred. The three aftershocks are evenly spaced across 3.5 seconds and refresh only the slow, as the pinned source specifies.
- Soaring Slam's impact and aftershocks resolve at its landing location. All three aftershocks are created on landing even if the impact misses or is spell-shielded; an interrupted leap creates no aftershocks.
- Frenzying Taunt affects an enemy champion, so its monster-only damage is excluded. The pinned reworked kit has no attack-speed steroid on E, no magic on-hit passive, and no Defensive Ball Curl self-slow or percentage damage reduction.
- The behavior uses Powerball to engage and casts Soaring Slam during Powerball when available, then Defensive Ball Curl and Frenzying Taunt at close range. It preserves Defensive Ball Curl while fighting at attack range instead of immediately cancelling it for another Powerball.
- The stat budget estimates Defensive Ball Curl at its 7/(7+7) uptime for resistance multiplication and reflected basic-attack damage. Its flat resistance gains and damage returned by the opponent's on-hit spells are resolved in the duel; those terms are not separately estimated by the stat budget.
