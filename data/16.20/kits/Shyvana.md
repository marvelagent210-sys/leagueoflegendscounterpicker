Targeting: Auto · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Physical Magic · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: unknown · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Shyvana's basic attacks on-hit will reduce Emberstrike's current cooldown by 0.5 seconds, and deal bonus magic damage equal to 1% (+ 1.1% per 100 bonus AD) of the target's maximum health. Against non-champions, this effect deals a minimum of 10 and is capped at 200.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

Active: Emberstrike can be cast twice in Human Form and thrice in Dragon Form. Each cast empowers Shyvana's next basic attack within 6 seconds to have an uncancellable windup and trigger an additional exclusive effect. After 1.5 seconds of using an Emberstrike attack, or immediately following a basic attack, the ability can be recast within and up to the next 4 seconds each time, before it incurs its cooldown. Shyvana gains 50 bonus range on all of her basic attacks while Emberstrike is active. The attacks deal 10 bonus physical damage against monsters.

- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup

First Cast: Shyvana slashes her claws, blasting a cone in the target's direction that deals modified physical damage to enemies hit. The damage to the primary target is affected by critical strike modifiers.

- **Area Physical Damage:** 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 (+ 110% AD) (+ 30% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 30 (+ 110% AD) (+ 30% AP)
- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Second Cast: Shyvana slams her tail, dealing the same damage to the target and surrounding enemies.

Dragon Form Bonus — Third Cast: Shyvana takes a bite out of the target, dealing modified true damage. This damage cannot critically strike.

- **True Damage:** 15 / 22.5 / 30 / 37.5 / 45 (+ 165% AD) (+ 45% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 45 (+ 165% AD) (+ 45% AP)

All casts reset Shyvana's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- Applies spell damage to the primary target (attack target) and area damage to secondary targets. The passive on-hit damage applies proc damage.
- Spell shield will not block any damage nor be consumed by the strikes.
- The true damage is always equal to 150% of the pre-mitigation physical damage.
- Blind will negate the damage to the primary target, but not to any secondary targets.
- Interaction with parrying effects (dodge, block).
- Only the last cast of Emberstrike puts Shyvana's basic attack on cooldown.
    - In Human Form, only Q2 will put it on cooldown; in Dragon Form, only Q3 will.
    - This results in the same functionality as Shield of Daybreak and Thundering Smash, effectively being a triple/quintuple attack reset.
