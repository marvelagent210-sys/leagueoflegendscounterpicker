## Innate: Mark of the Storm

Source: [Template:Data Kennen/Mark of the Storm](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Kennen/Mark_of_the_Storm?oldid=3932481) (revision 3932481). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Kennen's abilities apply a stack of Mark of the Storm to enemies hit for 6 seconds, refreshing on subsequent applications and stacking up to 3 times.

The third stack against a target consumes them all to stun them for 1.25 seconds and restore 25 energy. The stun duration is reduced to 0.5 seconds if this occurs on the same target again within 6 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** stun (1.25 s)

Slicing Maelstrom can apply only up to 3 stacks on a target.

Notes:

- Kennen will still restore energy if the target dies from the third stack of Mark of the Storm.
- After Slicing Maelstrom applies its third stack, the target gains a 6-second stack immunity against Slicing Maelstrom that refreshes on subsequent hits.

## W: Electrical Surge

Targeting: Proximity · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: false · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Kennen's basic attacks on-attack generate a stack of Electrical Surge, stacking up to 4 times. At 4 stacks, his next basic attack on-attack consumes all stacks to both deal bonus magic damage and apply a stack of Mark of the Storm on-hit. This damage can critically strike for 140% damage. Kennen gains maximum stacks of Electrical Surge upon respawning.

- **Bonus Magic Damage:** 35 / 45 / 55 / 65 / 75 (+ 80 / 90 / 100 / 110 / 120% bonus AD) (+ 35% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 75 (+ 120% bonusAD) (+ 35% AP)
- **Mechanics:** on-attack; on-attack; on-hit; critical strike

Active: Kennen sends out a surge of electricity that deals magic damage to all nearby enemies afflicted by Mark of the Storm or within Slicing Maelstrom.

- **Magic Damage:** 70 / 95 / 120 / 145 / 170 (+ 80% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 170 (+ 80% AP)

An enemy within 775 units with Mark of the Storm or inside Slicing Maelstrom is required to cast this ability. The target does not have to be visible to be targeted by this ability.

- **Mechanics:** reveal

Notes:

- The empowered basic attack applies bonus spell damage, while the electrical surge deals area damage.
- Electrical Surge's enhanced attack will be consumed and the bonus damage will not be applied if Kennen is blinded.
    - Electrical Surge's enhanced attack cannot be dodged or blocked.
- Uniquely to Electrical Surge, on-hit effects triggered by the enhanced attack's cast instance (e.g. Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury, Dusk and Dawn Spellblade) will apply the bonus damage and Mark of the Storm to targets hit.
    - This is not the case with any other effects applied on-hit.
    - This is intentional.
- The empowered attack will trigger but not be consumed nor apply its effects against structures and wards.
