## Innate: An Acquired Taste

Source: [Template:Data Tahm Kench/An Acquired Taste](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Tahm_Kench/An_Acquired_Taste?oldid=4008458) (revision 4008458). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Tahm Kench's basic attacks on-hit and Tongue Lash are empowered to deal 5 – 60 (based on level) (+ 1.25% AP per 100 bonus health) (+ 4% of his bonus health) bonus magic damage and apply a stack of An Acquired Taste against enemy champions for 5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits and stacking up to 3 times. Stacks expire one by one every 0.67 seconds when the duration ends.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

Tongue Lash and Devour gain additional effects against enemies with 3 stacks, consuming them all.

Notes:

- The passive bonus damage applies to turrets.
- When An Acquired Taste is applied by Tongue Lash, the damage is dealt as an additional instance of damage (as opposed to bonus damage when applied by basic attacks).
    - This causes effects like Bone Plating and Bloodletter's Curse Vile Decay to be applied twice.

## R: Devour

Source: [Template:Data Tahm Kench/Devour](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Tahm_Kench/Devour?oldid=4063964) (revision 4063964). Ranks: 3.

| Field | Value |

| Cooldown | 120 / 100 / 80 |
| Cost | 100 mana |

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Self, Enemies, Allies · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Tahm Kench abducts the target champion, pulling them to him over the cast time and then swallowing them to carry them in his belly for up to 3 seconds. While inside, the target is attached to Tahm Kench and is rendered untargetable and displacement immune. Devour can only be cast on enemies with 3 stacks of An Acquired Taste.

- **Mechanics:** attach; untargetable; displacement-immune

If the target is an ally, they become unable to act and are granted a shield, which decays in strength by 50 every 0.25 seconds after they are Regurgitated. Tahm Kench will also gain 60% bonus movement speed for 3 seconds and is unable to cast movement spells besides Abyssal Dive while an ally is inside of his belly.

- **Shield Strength:** 650 / 800 / 950 (+ 100% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 950 (+ 100% AP)
- **Mechanics:** lockout; shield

After 1 second, Tahm Kench can cast Regurgitate while a target is inside of his belly, and automatically does so after the duration. The swallowed ally can also input a movement command to force Tahm Kench to cast Regurgitate in a target direction of their choice, unless they are immobilized.

An Acquired Taste Bonus: The target is suppressed during Devour's cast time and while attached. Tahm Kench will also be grounded and slowed by 40% under those same conditions.

- **Mechanics:** suppression; ground; slow (40%)

If Tahm Kench is performing Abyssal Dive, Regurgitate will not automatically cast until after he finishes Abyssal Dive effects.

Notes:

- Devour's initial pull is a non-airborne displacement that displaces the target toward Tahm Kench over the ability's cast time.
    - This is also the same displacement that is used for when Devour is cast with Tongue Lash, though with a greater pull distance as well as faster pull speed to account for Devour's short cast time.
    - The target will blink to Tahm Kench's location at the end of this displacement, including if it is interrupted.
- The untargetability and displacement immunity is granted to the target during Devour's cast time as well.
- Against enemies, Devour's pull from abducting the target depends on the application of the suppression; if the suppression is not applied, neither is the pull. Similarly, if the suppression is removed, the displacement from the pull plus the untargetability and displacement immunity are instantly stopped.
    - The attachment also depends upon the occurrence of the pull; if the target is not pulled, they are not attached to Tahm Kench and the swallow fails, cancelling the ability entirely.
    - If the target resists the suppression by being immune to crowd control, displacement immune, or having a spell shield, Tahm Kench will not pull the target towards him.
    - If the suppression was removed while the target is attached to Tahm Kench (in other words, inside of his belly), the target will detach themselves from him instantly without Tahm Kench performing Regurgitate.
- Devour's suppression against enemies is removed if Tahm Kench dies, enters resurrection, or transitions in or out of the Death Realm.
- Experimental Hexplate Overdrive, Fiendhunter Bolts Opening Barrage, and Zeke's Convergence Frostfire Tempest trigger after Devour's suppression ends or after Regurgitate displacement, if Regurgitate is cast.
- Enemies will be dealt Regurgitate damage regardless of Tahm Kench casting the ability if Devour's suppression ends by any means other than dismounting (including during Devour's cast time).
- If the target enters resurrection, or they are a Mordekaiser that transitions into a different dimension from Tahm Kench, he will Regurgitate them automatically.
- Devour cannot be used on allies that are channeling or using an ability that preloads UnstoppableForceMarker.
- The self-slow is affected by slow resist.
- Tahm Kench cannot use gates during Devour.
- Allies can see if this spell is ready next to Tahm Kench's health bar.
- The swallowed target is not considered to be vanished. They are instead hidden below the visible map plane, to maintain ongoing effects such as Curse of the Black Mist.
- During Devour's attachment and Regurgitate displacement, the swallowed target's camera is locked and centered on their champion.
    - This does not affect Tahm Kench.
- Devour's self ground can be cleansed by R.
