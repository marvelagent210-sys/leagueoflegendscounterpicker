## E: Black Shield

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Self, Allies · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Morgana grants a shield to the target allied champion or herself for 5 seconds, which absorbs incoming magic damage and grants crowd control immunity while it holds.

- **Magic Shield Strength:** 100 / 155 / 210 / 265 / 320 (+ 70% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 320 (+ 70% AP)
- **Mechanics:** shield (5 s); cc-immune (5 s)

Notes:

- Black Shield will not resist self nor allied crowd control (including e.g. an allied Tempered Fate).
- Black Shield will not resist nearsight.
- Black Shield negates crowd control effects before any magic damage is absorbed; even if the shield is broken by an enemy dealing enough damage, its associated disables will not apply. Shield-destroying effects bypass this however, since they destroy the shield before applying their effects.
- Although not considered a crowd control effect, Black Shield is special-cased to block Test of Spirit spirit pull, but does not prevent R tether, R, W, R.
- Black Shield does not prevent Chum the Waters from attaching.
- Black Shield takes priority over other sources of crowd control immunity and those that grant immunity to specific types of crowd control (displacement immunity and slow immunity).
- Spell shield will take priority over Black Shield.
- Black Shield will always take priority over regular shields. If used in conjunction with Lifeline, the most recently-applied one will have priority however.
- Black Shield has a forgiveness radius of 175 units.

## R: Soul Shackles

Source: [Template:Data Morgana/Soul Shackles](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Morgana/Soul_Shackles?oldid=4020315) (revision 4020315). Ranks: 3.

Targeting: Proximity · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: false · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Morgana latches chains of energy onto nearby enemy champions over the cast time, dealing magic damage and forming a tether between herself and each target for 3 seconds, during which she gains bonus movement speed and the targets are revealed and slowed by 20%.

- **Magic Damage:** 200 / 275 / 350 (+ 80% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 350 (+ 80% AP)
- **Total Magic Damage:** 400 / 550 / 700 (+ 160% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 700 (+ 160% AP)
- **Bonus Movement Speed:** 20 / 40 / 60% → at max rank, level 20: 60%
- **Mechanics:** tether (3 s); true sight; slow (20%)

If a target does not break their tether by the end of its duration, they are dealt the same magic damage again and become stunned for a duration, during which they are revealed.

- **Stun Duration:** 1.5 / 1.75 / 2 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 2 s
- **Mechanics:** stun (2 s); true sight

An enemy champion within 575 units is required to cast this ability. The target does not have to be visible to be tethered by this ability.

- **Mechanics:** reveal

Notes:

- Spell shield will block the tether's application and initial damage but not the aftereffects of one already applied.
- Morgana will turn to face southeast upon casting Soul Shackles.
- Soul Shackles checks for nearby valid targets around a location that is Morgana's exact location at the start of the cast time.
    - In other words, the latch-on radius is from a fixed location around Soul Shackles' cast point in which targets are acquired; targets around Morgana's location at the end of the cast time that are not within this radius will not be targeted, since her location after the cast starts is not considered.
      - Enemies that were outside of the latch-on radius at the time of cast that enter it afterwards, during the cast time, will be targeted.
- A circle indicator for Soul Shackles' maximum tether range is visible to Morgana and her tethered targets only.

### Assumptions

- Black Shield is self-cast in the champion-only duel. Soul Siphon heals from champion damage; minion and monster targets are absent.
- Dark Binding's first-enemy collision resolves against the opposing champion; intervening minions and targetable pets are not modeled as projectile blockers.
- Soul Shackles' cast requirement, latch and tether radii use champion centers on the simulator's line. Its reveal exposes invisible enemies while the tether or completion stun lasts; nearsight still limits vision.
- Tormented Shadow's missing-health damage bonus is linearly interpolated from its committed 0–100% source series. Its field deals ten pulses including the on-cast pulse, and overlapping pools from this Morgana do not stack.
