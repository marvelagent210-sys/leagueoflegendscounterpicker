### Assumptions

- Every basic ability is rank5, while Soulflare/Defiance/Renewal bonus tables explicitly use Mantra rank4. The four-rank empowered records are read at rank4 despite their parsed five-rank parent classifications. Mantra starts ready but inactive, lasts eight seconds, is instant/free and is available during cast-inhibiting control. Separate basic/empowered variants share their underlying Q/W/E cooldowns. A successful completed empowered cast consumes its declaration-selected Mantra entitlement; already declared variants retain their selected effect if the eight-second window expires during the cast. Free target invalidation retains a still-live entitlement; disposition on free cancellation is not explicit in the primary and remains an assumption.
- Inner Flame uses the primary950center flight range (the same field lists890edge range),120full width and1700speed; Soulflare uses160full width. Real first-body collision, including actual damageable pets, creates one280radius explosion at the missile's contact coordinate. The flight itself does not separately spend a spell shield or deal a duplicate packet. Regular Q fizzles at maximum range; empowered Q explodes there. Cast origin is the actual completion position and direction is fixed at declaration. The primary live values override client metadata: Q root castRange25000/missileSpeed902 are targeting metadata, while its actual missile object corroborates950flight; E and R primary instant casts override each client spellCastTime0.25. Scalar line-hit probability gates the intended champion's impact/splash together; a real pet detonation uses actual axial area contacts. A Soulflare aimed beyond flight range can explode within its sourced radius. Lateral splash positioning and independent sidesteps cannot be reconstructed in1D and are not invented.
- Soulflare creates its1.5second50%slowing field at the actual impacted body's center (or at maximum-range explosion coordinate), separately from initial explosion center. Occupancy is refreshed each simulation tick without inventing damage pulses; the one rupture is separately delayed, spatial and spell-shield-immune. Its location and per-cast scale remain branch-local; released missiles/fields persist after owner death. An expected-value champion-only impact carries that conditional scale to rupture, while an actual pet contact generates its real full field. Continuous lateral dodging of the field is outside the axial geometry.
- Resolve initially damages and reveals the actual chosen enemy body, then watches its own two-second tether every tick. It breaks on distance beyond the primary825center tether radius, caster death/resurrection, target death/resurrection or disappearance; generic untargetability after attachment does not itself erase an existing tether, while stasis denies completion. An intact tether deals its live-AP second packet, roots for2seconds (3.25with Renewal) and reveals only while its own root remains. Completion bypasses newly gained spell shields; new parry still negates it. Initial spell shields/parry deny attachment, but Renewal's completed-cast self heal is independent. Source does not specify AP snapshot or caster-death tether behavior; these remain explicit interpretations.
- W can manually choose a real hostile damageable pet with its explicit pet variant; no minion wave or pet is fabricated. Completion revalidates the declared body: death/untargetability/vision loss is free, range-only cancellation pays the shared W cooldown and mana. Paid Renewal range cancellation still grants the primary-specified initial heal and consumes Mantra. Healing reads current missing HP at each event and uses17%plus0.01%per AP; an attached target's actual death grants the second heal once, without waiting for the tether timer or producing damage/root on a corpse. Runes are not simulated, so the quoted Aery/Conqueror/Electrocute bugs add no phantom damage or stacks.
- Inspire/Defiance can shield self or an explicitly chosen real allied Champion-class pet; other classes and invented teammates receive nothing. Primary shield280+60%AP is supplemented by165+45%AP for empowered primary, while each actual eligible body within700of that primary receives only165+45%AP and15%speed. The primary keeps40%speed, all shields last2.5seconds and speed lasts2seconds; a new own Inspire replaces its own earlier shield/speed rather than stacking duplicate stages. Instant ally declaration uses800range with the sourced175forgiveness at completion. Ordinary solo AI uses self shields only; missing ally benefits remain zero.
- Gathering Fire reduces actual remaining R cooldown by four seconds once per accepted damaging ability hit against each real champion or explicit Champion-class clone, including each Q explosion/rupture and each W endpoint. Ordinary attacks, field-only slows, casts, nonchampion pets, misses, spell shields and parry give no refund. Damage-only invulnerability does not fabricate hit avoidance. The reference allocation prices full-contact Q, both W endpoints and self E shielding, with RQ as the sustained Mantra branch and a fixed-point passive-refund cadence; it prices no ally support or Renewal healing. Real fights require target contact, mana, tether survival and actual missing health. AI reserves new R casts in hold/trade, respects completed-trade retreat before optional spells, and chooses Renewal when wounded or Soulflare otherwise.
