## Innate: Cultivation of War

Source: [Template:Data Zaahen/Cultivation of War](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Zaahen/Cultivation_of_War?oldid=4060321) (revision 4060321). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Zaahen generates a stack of Determination for each enemy champion damaged by his basic attacks and abilities, lasting for 5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent triggers, and stacking up to 12 times. After the duration, the stacks expire by 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 (based on Seconds) every 0.5 seconds.

Determination: For each stack, Zaahen gains bonus attack damage equal to 1.5 – 2.8 (based on level)% AD. At maximum stacks, Zaahen becomes filled with Determination, causing him to double the bonus to 36 – 67.2 (based on level)% AD. Periodically, if Zaahen would take lethal damage while at maximum stacks of Determination, he refreshes its duration and enters resurrection for 4 seconds: becoming invulnerable, untargetable, and unable to act. Additionally, he restores 30 / 45 / 60 / 75 (based on level)% of his maximum health over the duration. Afterwards, he revives himself and refreshes the duration of Determination.

- **Mechanics:** revive (4 s); invulnerable (4 s); untargetable (4 s); lockout

- **Static Cooldown:** 300 / 240 / 180 / 120 (based on level)

Notes:

- The bonus attack damage granted by Determination stacks will update dynamically with Zaahen's total attack damage.
- Determination stacks are generated per damage instance per champion damaged with Zaahen's attacks and abilities.
    - Q initial cast empowered attack can generate a stack for each strike.
    - Both passes of W can generate a stack.
    - Both E swing and R shockwave generate a stack for every champion hit.
    - Triggerseed generates a stack for every champion hit when cast on Zaahen.
- During the resurrection's health restoration, Zaahen's current health is set to a percentage of his maximum health equal to the cumulative amount of health restored on that restoration tick, for every single tick.
    - In other words, his current health will always be overridden by the percentage of health he is supposed to have restored on every restoration tick, to prevent him from healing or restoring health by other sources.
- Upon the resurrection being triggered, all summoner spells that are not already on cooldown are placed on a 5-second cooldown.
- The untargetability from the resurrection does not destroy in-flight projectiles.
- Cultivation of War's resurrection takes priority over Guardian Angel Rebirth, but grants priority to Chronoshift.
- The resurrection does not activate if Zaahen is killed by the Nexus Obelisk.
- Invulnerability will prevent stacks from being generated, as no damage is dealt.
- Spell shield will not prevent stacks from being generated, even if no damage is dealt.
- Cultivation of War stacks additively with Infernal Might and Overlord's Bloodmail Retribution and multiplicatively with mid lane quest's bonus AD multiplier.
- Determination stacks are displayed in a secondary resource bar under Zaahen's health bar.
    - The bar is white at <4 stacks, yellow at &ge;4 stacks and orange at &ge;9 stacks.
      - At maximum stacks, if the revive is available, the bar will be red and will remain red after Zaahen revives until he loses stacks again.
- There's a short delay between the stacks falling off and the removal of the revive. If Zaahen triggers it during this time, it will fail to go on cooldown.

## Q: The Darkin Glaive

Source: [Template:Data Zaahen/The Darkin Glaive](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Zaahen/The_Darkin_Glaive?oldid=4060370) (revision 4060370). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Zaahen empowers his next basic attack within 4 seconds to have an uncancellable windup and gain 25 bonus range. On-attack, he strikes the target twice. If the target was a champion, minion, or monster, he also heals himself. The strikes deal modified physical damage and the heal is reduced to 50% against minions and monsters. Each strike applies on-hit effects and can critically strike for 50% AD bonus physical damage.

- **Total Physical Damage:** 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 75 (+ 100% AD) (+ 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 75 (+ 100% AD) (+ 40% bonusAD)
- **Physical Damage per Hit:** 7.5 / 15 / 22.5 / 30 / 37.5 (+ 50% AD) (+ 10 / 12.5 / 15 / 17.5 / 20% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 37.5 (+ 50% AD) (+ 20% bonusAD)
- **Champion Healing:** 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 / 9% of his maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 9% of maxHealth
- **Non-Champion Healing:** 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 / 4 / 4.5% of his maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 4.5% of maxHealth
- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; on-attack; heal; on-hit; critical strike

After 1.5 seconds of using the empowered attack, The Darkin Glaive can be recast within the next 4 seconds at no additional cost, modifying it with a new effect.

Recast: Zaahen empowers his next basic attack within 4 seconds to have an uncancellable windup, gain 25 bonus range, deal bonus physical damage and knock up the target for 0.75 seconds.

- **Bonus Physical Damage:** 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 150 (+ 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 150 (+ 40% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; airborne (0.75 s)

The Darkin Glaive deals 200% damage against monsters.

Both casts of The Darkin Glaive reset Zaahen's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- The Darkin Glaive's damage is tagged as basic damage and spell damage, which allows it to trigger spell effects.
- Spell shield will only block the knock up from the recast.
- Since the healing is granted on-attack, parrying effects will not prevent Zaahen from gaining the heal. Otherwise, parrying effects will negate all other aspects of The Darkin Glaive's attacks while consuming them.
- The ability will not heal against wards, plants, or structures.
- Upon being put in resurrection and if The Darkin Glaive is active, it is placed on cooldown immediately.
- The first cast's double-strike attack:
    - Rolls critical strike once for both strikes.
    - Does not cause Zaahen to attack twice.
      - Effects that trigger on-attack or interact with the attack windup will only trigger once and thus do not interact with The Darkin Glaive. (e.g. Lethal Tempo, Hail of Blades).
    - Will consume Bone Plating once.
      - If the first strike triggers the rune, the second one will get blocked by it.
      - If the first strike is blocked by the rune, the second one will not.
- The 1.5-second delay is not "fuzzy" (it will never take up to another 0.25 seconds) and so will never correctly sync up with Sheen like Precision Protocol.
