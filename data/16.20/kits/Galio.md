## Innate: Colossal Smash

Source: [Template:Data Galio/Colossal Smash](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Galio/Colossal_Smash?oldid=4038336) (revision 4038336). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Periodically, Galio's next basic attack is empowered to have an uncancellable windup, gain 40% bonus attack speed and deal 15 – 115 (based on level) (+ 100% AD) (+ 40% AP) (+ 60% bonus magic resistance) modified magic damage to the target and all enemies near them.

- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup

Whenever Galio hits at least one enemy champion or epic monster with an ability, Colossal Smash's current cooldown is reduced by 3 seconds. This may occur only once per cast.

Colossal Smash's damage based on its AD ratio can critically strike for 200% damage to all targets hit.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Notes:

- Colossal Smash deals basic damage to the primary target but also triggers spell effects by also being tagged as spell damage, while dealing proc damage also tagged as area of effect to secondary targets.
- Spellblade does not get converted to magic damage and it will deal its damage only to the primary target.
- Colossal Smash applies on-hit effects to its primary target twice.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against wards and plants.
- Galio gains attack speed even on non-empowered attacks as long as the ability is not on cooldown.
- Colossal Smash cannot be triggered against allies.
- Even if the ability hit is spell shielded Colossal Smash's cooldown will still be reduced.
