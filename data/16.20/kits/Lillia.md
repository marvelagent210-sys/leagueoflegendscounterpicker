## Innate: Dream-Laden Bough

Source: [Template:Data Lillia/Dream-Laden Bough](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Lillia/Dream-Laden_Bough?oldid=4038402) (revision 4038402). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Lillia's abilities apply Dream Dust to enemies hit, which deals 5% (+ 1.25% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health total magic damage over 3 seconds, capped at 70 – 180 (based on level) against monsters.

Lillia heals herself for 39 (+ 15% AP) against large monsters and 6 – 90 (based on level) (+ 30% AP) against champions over the duration of Dream Dust, reduced to 15% effectiveness for each target afflicted beyond the first. She may only heal against 1 monster at a time.

- **Mechanics:** heal

Notes:

- Dream-Laden Bough deals persistent damage, additionally tagged as indirect damage, and applies spell effects.
- Lilting Lullaby application does not apply Dream Dust, but its triggering damage does.
- Dream Dust is applied even if the target takes no damage from an ability, e.g. by being shielded or invulnerable, but not when the ability hit is prevented in the first place (e.g. spell shield).

Active: Lillia casts a magical lullaby over all enemy champions affected by Dream Dust, which arrives after 0.3 seconds and renders them drowsy for 1.5 seconds, slowing them by 10%. After the duration, they fall asleep for a few seconds.

- **Sleep Duration:** 2.5 / 2.75 / 3 → at max rank, level 20: 3
- **Mechanics:** drowsy (1.5 s); slow (10%)

The next instance of non-persistent damage that a sleeping target takes from champions, large monsters or turrets consumes the debuff to deal additional magic damage.

- **Magic Damage:** 100 / 150 / 200 (+ 40% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 200 (+ 40% AP)
- **Mechanics:** damage over time; sleep (3 s)

An enemy champion affected by Dream Dust is required to cast this ability. Lillia can move during Lilting Lullaby's cast time.

Notes:

- Spell shield will not prevent falling asleep nor block the wake-up damage.
    - The lullaby's drowsy application will be blocked.
- The additional magic damage dealt is accredited to Lillia and benefits from her magic penetration.
- Lilting Lullaby's missile will not hit a target that becomes untargetable during the cast time or while it is travelling, or is in another realm.
- Lilting Lullaby's application does not trigger aggro from turrets, only its triggering damage does.
    - Dream Dust's persistent damage will still cause Lillia to trigger turret aggro, unless she casts Lilting Lullaby right before it runs out.
- Dream Dust will mark targets if Lilting Lullaby is learned.
