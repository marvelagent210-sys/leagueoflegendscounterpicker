Innate - Final Service: Whenever Yorick uses Last Rites to score the killing blow on any enemy or damage an enemy champion or large monster, he raises a grave at their location. Additionally, a grave is passively raised for every 8th / 7th / 6th / 5th / 4th / 3rd / 2nd (based on level) enemy that dies otherwise near him. See Pets for more details about Mist Walkers.

Notes:

- Damaging a large monster or champion with Last Rites will only spawn 1 grave, even if it kills them.
