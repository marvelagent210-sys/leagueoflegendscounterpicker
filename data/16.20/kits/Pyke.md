## R: Death from Below

Source: [Template:Data Pyke/Death from Below](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Pyke/Death_from_Below?oldid=4007995) (revision 4007995). Ranks: 3.

Targeting: Location · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical True · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Pyke marks the target location with the shape of an X before striking it, executing enemy champions within the area that are below 250 / 290 / 330 / 370 / 400 / 430 / 450 / 470 / 490 / 510 / 530 / 540 / 550 (based on level) (+ 80% bonus AD) (+ 1.5 per 1 Lethality) health. Other enemies hit and enemy champions above the threshold are instead dealt 50% of the amount as physical damage.

- **Mechanics:** execute

If Death from Below hits an enemy champion or at least one is killed inside the X by the execution or an ally, Pyke will blink to the center of the X. For the latter case Pyke can also recast the ability within 20 seconds at no cost.

- **Mechanics:** blink

Each successful execution grants one Your Cut to the last assisting ally, instead of its natural assist gold. Otherwise, Pyke is granted a Your Cut for each enemy champion killed inside the X by an ally during Death from Below's cast time.

Notes:

- Applies raw damage to executed targets and area damage to targets above the threshold.
- Death from Below's execute against an enemy champion will aggro nearby enemy minions.
- Death from Below cannot execute clones. They are always dealt 50% physical damage.
- If the target is in a zombie state, does not enter the death state, or is killed by the ability while above the execution threshold, Death from Below does not grant Your Cut nor the recast.
- Spell shield does not prevent Pyke from blinking.
- Death from Below cannot be interrupted; Pyke will always appear at the target location if enemy champions are caught inside the X even if he is moved (e.g. by being displaced) during the cast time.
    - The only exception is if he is being suppressed, in which case he will not blink.
- Axiom Arcanist does not refund the cooldown while Pyke is able to recast.
    - Axiom Arcanist's tooltip counts it refunding Death from Below even when it doesn't.
- Death from Below will not go on cooldown if Pyke dies during its cast.
    - Pyke will still generate Your Cut from enemies that die inside the X before Pyke dies.
