- **Mechanics:** heal

Perseverance is lost for 8 seconds if Garen takes damage from champions, epic monsters, or turrets, or if he is hit by an enemy ability or affected by an enemy summoner spell, refreshing on subsequent damage and hits taken from them.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: false

Active: Garen cleanses himself of all slows and gains 35% bonus movement speed for a duration.

- **Movement Speed Duration:** 1.4 / 1.95 / 2.5 / 3.05 / 3.6 → at max rank, level 20: 3.6
- **Mechanics:** cleanse

Additionally, Garen empowers his next basic attack within 4.5 seconds to have an uncancellable windup, lunge at the target, deal bonus physical damage, and silence them for 1.5 seconds.

- **Bonus Physical Damage:** 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 150 (+ 50% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 150 (+ 50% AD)
- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; dash; silence (1.5 s)

Decisive Strike resets Garen's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- The enhanced attack will apply other on-hit effects and can critically strike as normal (the bonus damage does not).
    - The bonus damage will also apply life steal.
    - The bonus damage is applied to structures.
- Decisive Strike locks Garen out of using basic attacks for a short period of time. This is shortened with attack speed.
- The enhanced attack will still complete and hit the target even if they become untargetable during the attack's windup.
- Decisive Strike will not prevent the reapplication of slows (e.g. the persistent slow from Pillar of Ice will be immediately reapplied after removal if Garen is still within its area of effect).
- Spell shield will only negate the silence.
- Decisive Strike's lunge can pass very thin terrain and will otherwise not be able to. In any case, Garen will still hit his target.[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0qi54mu_gxg]

## W: Courage

Source: [Template:Data Garen/Courage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Garen/Courage?oldid=4064595) (revision 4064595). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Whenever Garen kills an enemy, he generates a stack of Courage, stacking up to 150 times.

Courage: For each stack, Garen gains 0.2 bonus armor and 0.2 bonus magic resistance, up to a maximum of 30 bonus resistances each.

Active: Garen reduces incoming damage for 4 seconds. For the first 0.75 seconds, Garen additionally grants himself a shield and 60% tenacity.

- **Damage Reduction:** 25 / 29 / 33 / 37 / 41% → at max rank, level 20: 41%
- **Shield Strength:** 65 / 85 / 105 / 125 / 145 (+ 18% bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 145 (+ 18% bonusHealth)
- **Mechanics:** shield; tenacity

Notes:

- Courage's bonus resistances can be obtained by any of Garen's damage sources (basic attacks, abilities, summoner spells, runes, item actives).
    - This does not include last hits transferred to Garen artificially.
- The kills on these enemy unit types count towards Courage stacks:
    - Champions (Only kills, assists don't grant stacks)
    - All minions and monsters
    - Champion summoned units (e.g. Noxious Trap, Powder Keg)
    - All pets and clones.
      - The bonus is equal for all these units; large units do not grant a larger bonus.
- The kills on these enemy unit types do not count towards Courage stacks:
    - Wards.
    - Turrets (including Sun Disk) and other structures
    - Jungle plants.
    - Units destroyed by 'trampling' such as seeds, goo or tunnels.
    - Tentacles and Dark Procession
- Courage will only grant bonus resistances from units killed by Garen himself. Eye of the Herald does not count.
- The Tenacity increase stacks additively with the tenacity reduction from Brittle, but multiplicatively with other sources.

Active: Garen rapidly spins his sword around himself 7 (+ 1 per 25% bonus attack speed) times over 3 seconds, becoming unable to declare basic attacks but gaining ghosting and dealing physical damage to nearby enemies periodically. Judgment can be recast after 1 second while active, and does so automatically after it ends.

- **Physical Damage Per Spin:** 4 / 7 / 10 / 13 / 16 (+ 40 / 43 / 46 / 49 / 52% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 16 (+ 52% AD)
- **Critical Strike Damage Per Spin:** 5.2 / 9.1 / 13 / 16.9 / 20.8 (+ 52 / 55.9 / 59.8 / 63.7 / 67.6% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 20.8 (+ 67.6% AD)
- **Mechanics:** lockout; ghosted

Enemy champions hit 6 times by Judgment are inflicted with 25% armor reduction for 6 seconds, with the duration refreshing upon the 7th hit and every 6th hit thereafter.

Judgment deals 25% increased damage against the nearest enemy hit.

- **Increased Damage Per Spin:** 5 / 8.75 / 12.5 / 16.25 / 20 (+ 50 / 53.75 / 57.5 / 61.25 / 65% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 20 (+ 65% AD)
- **Increased Critical Strike Damage Per Spin:** 6.5 / 11.375 / 16.25 / 21.125 / 26 (+ 65 / 69.875 / 74.75 / 79.625 / 84.5% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 26 (+ 84.5% AD)

Recast: Garen ends Judgment.

Judgment can critically strike for 130% damage.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Notes:

- At level 20, Garen will spin a minimum of 9 times.
- Deactivating the ability manually does not count as an ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- Each spin triggers a stack of Conqueror.
- For the purpose of moving closer to an enemy when right clicking them, Judgment also reduces Garen's attack range to 100 for the duration.
- Judgment only scales with bonus attack speed from items and growth (per level).
- Judgment cancels Garen's last movement command upon ending.
- Each spin deals damage simultaneously to all enemies within its range when the spin is completed.
- The spin animation is capped at 200% bonus attack speed, but the amount of ticks will keep on scaling.
- The armor reduction stacks multiplicatively with other percentage armor penetration effects.
