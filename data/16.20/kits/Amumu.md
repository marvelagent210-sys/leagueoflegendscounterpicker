## Innate: Cursed Touch

Source: [Template:Data Amumu/Cursed Touch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Amumu/Cursed_Touch?oldid=4046475) (revision 4046475). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: True · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Amumu's basic attacks and Curse of the Sad Mummy are empowered to mark enemies with Curse for 3 seconds, refreshing on subsequent applications and Despair per-tick damage.

Cursed targets receive 10% bonus true damage from all incoming pre-mitigation magic damage.

Notes:

- The additional damage inherits tags from the source, and attaches proc damage to it.
- If the triggering basic attack from Amumu applies magic damage on-hit, Curse will be applied before the magic damage.
- The Curse application from Curse of the Sad Mummy happens after the magic damage is dealt by it, therefore amplifying the damage only if the target was also already marked before the hit.
- Neutral units / enemies affected by the Curse can receive bonus damage from the opposing team of Amumu / neutral units, but the damage will be credited to Amumu.
- The application of Curse on Curse of the Sad Mummy is negated if the ability is blocked by a spell shield.

Targeting: Direction · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: true · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Active: Amumu throws a bandage in the target direction that deals magic damage to the first enemy hit, stunning them for 1 second and pulling him to them, during which they are revealed.

- **Magic Damage:** 70 / 95 / 120 / 145 / 170 (+ 85% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 170 (+ 85% AP)
- **Mechanics:** stun (1 s); dash; reveal (1 s)

Amumu periodically stocks a Bandage Toss charge, up to a maximum of 2.

Despair can be cast during the dash.

Notes:

- Amumu will track the target if they change locations.
    - He will dash to the target's previous location if the target is 2000 or more units away or moves beyond 2000 units.
- Bandage Toss will always be directed towards the target's location even if Amumu moves while it is in flight.
- Immobilizing effects will not prevent Amumu from commencing the dash.
- Bandage Toss does not fizzle on Amumu's death, and can still stun and damage enemies.
- Amumu is still able to dash even if Bandage Toss is blocked by spell shield.
- Amumu will be ordered to basic attack the target upon completion of the dash.
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.

### Assumptions

- Despair starts toggled on, drains 8 mana per second continuously, and turns off when mana is exhausted; it is not automatically toggled back on. Its first damage pulse is 0.5 seconds into the fight. Only existing Curse marks are refreshed by its damage.
- Bandage Toss pulls to the enemy's moving position until it moves at least 2000 units away, then finishes toward its last tracked position. The duel has one enemy and no lane minions intercepting the bandage.
- The simulator ends a duel on champion death; a Bandage Toss still in flight at that point cannot affect the already-ended duel.
- The stat budget values Despair as continuously in range and Cursed Touch as active on Amumu's magic damage. Tantrum's attack-driven cooldown refunds and flat mitigation are resolved in the duel, rather than estimated by the stat budget.
