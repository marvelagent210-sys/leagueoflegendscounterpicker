## Innate: Cursed Touch

Source: [Template:Data Amumu/Cursed Touch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Amumu/Cursed_Touch?oldid=4046475) (revision 4046475). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: True · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Amumu's basic attacks and Curse of the Sad Mummy are empowered to mark enemies with Curse for 3 seconds, refreshing on subsequent applications and Despair per-tick damage.

Cursed targets receive 10% bonus true damage from all incoming pre-mitigation magic damage.

Notes:

- The additional damage inherits tags from the source, and attaches proc damage to it.
- If the triggering basic attack from Amumu applies magic damage on-hit, Curse will be applied before the magic damage.
- The Curse application from Curse of the Sad Mummy happens after the magic damage is dealt by it, therefore amplifying the damage only if the target was also already marked before the hit.
- Neutral units / enemies affected by the Curse can receive bonus damage from the opposing team of Amumu / neutral units, but the damage will be credited to Amumu.
- The application of Curse on Curse of the Sad Mummy is negated if the ability is blocked by a spell shield.
