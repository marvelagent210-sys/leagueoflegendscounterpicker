## R: Subjugate

Source: [Template:Data Trundle/Subjugate](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Trundle/Subjugate?oldid=4015247) (revision 4015247). Ranks: 3.

| Field | Value |

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Trundle drains the life force out of the target enemy champion, dealing magic damage and healing himself for the same amount. He also reduces 40% of their current armor and magic resistance, while gaining the same amount reduced as bonus armor and bonus magic resistance, respectively, for himself. Additionally, he increases in size by 18% while reducing the target's size by 9.9%.

- **Total Magic Damage:** 20 / 25 / 30% (+ 2% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 30% of targetMaxHealth (+ 2% per100:AP)
- **Total Healing:** 20 / 25 / 30% (+ 2% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 30% of targetMaxHealth (+ 2% per100:AP)
- **Mechanics:** heal; size change

Half of the drain's total damage, healing, and resistances reduction is applied on-cast, while the other half is applied every second over the next 4 seconds. The size modifiers last for 8 seconds and are applied over the first 2.25 seconds of the duration, both increasing Trundle's size by 2% and reducing the target's size by 1.1% every 0.25 seconds.

- **Initial Magic Damage:** 10 / 12.5 / 15% (+ 1% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 15% of targetMaxHealth (+ 1% per100:AP)
- **Magic Damage Per Second:** 2.5 / 3.125 / 3.75% (+ 0.25% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 3.75% of targetMaxHealth (+ 0.25% per100:AP)
- **Initial Healing:** 10 / 12.5 / 15% (+ 1% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 15% of targetMaxHealth (+ 1% per100:AP)
- **Healing Per Second:** 2.5 / 3.125 / 3.75% (+ 0.25% per 100 AP) of the target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 3.75% of targetMaxHealth (+ 0.25% per100:AP)

The resistances reduction on the target will linger for 4 seconds after the drain has ended.

Notes:

- Subjugate deals persistent damage, additionally tagged as indirect damage, and applies spell effects.
- Subjugate will snapshot the values of the target's current maximum health, total armor, and total magic resistance as soon as it completes its cast. The drain debuff will calculate the remaining half of the damage, healing, and resistances reduction based on these snapshotted values, even if the target's health or resistances change. The snapshot lasts until the debuff expires.
- The target's resistances will be further reduced by 5% per drain tick, up to 20% by the end of the drain (totaling to 40% reduction from the initial 20% reduction on-cast).
- Trundle will gain the reduced resistances of the target for himself for as long as they remain reduced, including while they are lingering after the drain.
- The resistances gained is based on the current reduced resistances of the target; the total amount that would be reduced is not granted to Trundle all at once. In other words, he will initially gain 20% of the target's resistances, then the remaining 20% over the 4 seconds, gaining additional bonus resistances equal to the resistances reduced per drain tick.
- Trundle gains 11.7 extra range on his basic attacks (relative to his and enemy center) as a consequence of his increased size.
    - So do his enemies.
    - He gains 3.78 to 6.255 range against the target he subjugated, depending on their base size, and so does the target against him.
- The debuff on the target also persists through death.
- Trundle will lose the buff if the target loses the debuff, for example if it enters resurrection.
- If the target becomes untargetable, dies, or is too far away or no longer in sight during the cast time, this ability will cancel but does not go on cooldown nor pay its cost (if applicable).
