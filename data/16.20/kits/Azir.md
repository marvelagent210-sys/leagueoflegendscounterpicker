Innate: Whenever a turret outside the enemy team's base is destroyed, a marker is placed above its ruins which can be targeted by Azir.

Innate - Disc of the Sun: Azir can select a marker to construct the Sun Disc above the ruins after a 0.5-second cast time. The Sun Disc will activate after being assembled over 5 seconds. Targeting: Location · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: false · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Azir summons a Sand Soldier at the target location that is untargetable, lasts 10 seconds, and grants sight of its surroundings. The Sand Soldier expires twice as fast while within range of an enemy turret, and does so instantly if Azir is too far away. Azir periodically stocks a Sand Soldier, up to a maximum of 2.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable; reveal

When Azir declares a basic attack against an enemy in a soldier's attack range, the Sand Soldier attacks in his stead, thrusting their spear in the target's direction to deal magic damage to enemies struck in a line. Against the primary target, the Sand Soldier's attack applies both on-hit and on-attack effects, the damage of which is reduced to 50% effectiveness. Targets hit beyond the closest take 20 / 28 / 36 / 44 / 52 / 60 / 68 / 76 / 84 / 92 / 100 (based on level)% damage. Subsequent Sand Soldier attacks against the same target deal 25% damage without applying on-hit and on-attack effects.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit; on-attack; on-hit; on-attack

Sand Soldiers cannot attack structures, wards, nor traps.

- **Mechanics:** trap

See Pets for more details about Sand Soldiers.

Notes:

- Soldiers' attacks deal area damage; against the main target, the damage is additionally tagged as basic damage.
    - If a soldier's attack hits multiple champions, it will deal damage to them in order of their appearance in the pre-game Loading Screen, the order of which may matter for some effects that trigger just once for the first target hit.
- Azir must rank Arise! as his first ability, and will do so automatically if he reaches level 2 before ranking an ability.
- Azir can only command his soldiers to attack if he is able to declare basic attack commands himself, meaning crowd control effects that disable attacking, such as disarm, do not allow him to order his soldiers to attack in his stead.
- Nearsighting effects will only allow Azir to perform attacks from soldiers within his own sight radius.
- Commanding a soldier to attack will trigger on-attack effects, but will not apply any of these effects to the target hit.
    - The attacks do not apply life steal.
    - Energized will stack but not be consumed.
    - Sundered Sky does not apply.
      - However it does apply with Runaan's Hurricane for secondary targets if the main target is a champion.
- Damage dealt by Hail of Blades will not be affected by the on-attack damage modifier.
- The first soldier attack while Azir is affected by Hail of Blades may fail to trigger its true damage.
- Damage to enemy champions from Sand Soldier stabs will draw minion aggro even if the enemy champion was not the primary target.
- Sand Soldiers can damage Powder Kegs, Tentacles, and Sentinels, but only if they are the main target of the attack.
- Sand Soldiers will attack the closest enemy to Azir when using attack move (or A + RMB / Shift + RMB) or auto-attack.
- If an enemy has vision of a Sand Soldier, they will also gain vision of Azir even if he is out of sight.
- While Navori Flickerblade is equipped, if Azir has 1 Arise! charge ready, the recharge timer will appear to restart when triggering Transcendence; the recharge timer is incurred normally.

- **Width (charge):** 620 / 720 / 820 → at max rank, level 20: 820

Notes:

- Monsters are also knocked back.
- The soldier units are spawned only 175 units behind Azir, but do not control the knock back directly. Each comes with a missile that travels the same path and conveys the damage and knockback effect.
