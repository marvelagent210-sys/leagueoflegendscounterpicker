## Innate: Stone Skin

Source: [Template:Data Wukong/Stone Skin](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Wukong/Stone_Skin?oldid=3986748) (revision 3986748). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Wukong gains 6 – 10 (based on level) bonus armor and regenerates an additional 0.35% of his maximum health every 5 seconds. Whenever Wukong or his clone damage an enemy champion or monster with his basic attacks or abilities, he generates a stack of Strength of Stone for 5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits and stacking up to 5 times. Stacks expire one by one every second when the duration ends.

- **Mechanics:** heal

Strength of Stone: For each stack, Stone Skin's effects are increased by 100%, up to a maximum of 36 – 60 (based on level) bonus armor and total additional regeneration of 2.1% maximum health every 5 seconds.

Notes:

- Stacks of Strength of Stone are also granted on-hit, but will specifically not be if the target is invulnerable.

## W: Warrior Trickster

Source: [Template:Data Wukong/Warrior Trickster](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Wukong/Warrior_Trickster?oldid=4016976) (revision 4016976). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Location · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Active: Wukong enters invisibility for 1 second and dashes in the target direction, leaving behind a clone of himself at his casting position for 4 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** invisible (1 s); dash; summon

The clone is untargetable to allies and can basic attack autonomously, prioritizing the last enemy Wukong damaged. It can also gain the effects of Crushing Blow and Nimbus Strike bonus attack speed, and casts Cyclone whenever Wukong does.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable

The clone deals reduced damage.

- **Clone Outgoing Damage:** 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 / 60% → at max rank, level 20: 60%

See Pets for more details about Wukong's clone. Wukong can cast any of his abilities during the dash.

Notes:

- Casting Warrior Trickster during an ability's effects will transfer them to the clone.
- Turret shots instantly kill the clone.
- The clone is immune to stasis and Blast Cone.
- The clone can still be targeted by allied auto-targeted effects (e.g. Heal, Celestial Blessing).
- Using a basic attack breaks the stealth at the end of the attack windup.

Active: Wukong spins his staff around for up to 2 seconds, becoming ghosted and gaining 20% bonus movement speed. The staff deals physical damage every 0.25 seconds to enemies hit, and can knock them up once for 0.6 seconds. Cyclone will not knock up enemies who were already hit by either Wukong or his clone within the same cast.

- **Physical Damage Per Tick:** 1 / 1.5 / 2% of target's maximum health (+ 34.375% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 2% of targetMaxHealth (+ 34.375% AD)
- **Total Physical Damage:** 8 / 12 / 16% of target's maximum health (+ 275% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 16% of targetMaxHealth (+ 275% AD)
- **Mechanics:** ghosted; airborne (0.6 s); airborne

Cyclone can be recast after 0.5 seconds within the duration, and does so automatically afterwards or by casting another ability.

Recast: Wukong ends Cyclone. After 1 second, he can cast Cyclone a second time within 8 seconds of the first cast.

- **Maximum Total Physical Damage:** 16 / 24 / 32% of target's maximum health (+ 550% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 32% of targetMaxHealth (+ 550% AD)

Nimbus Strike bonus attack speed duration is continuously refreshed while spinning.

Cyclone's damage is capped at 200 / 400 / 600 (based on level) per second against monsters.

Notes:

- Cyclone is special cased to grant stacks of Conqueror for each tick of damage.

- Cyclone's remaining duration always stays the same even after the clone replicated the ability.
- During Cyclone, Wukong and his clone's attack ranges are reduced to 50 .
- Spell shield will block one instance of damage.
    - Blocking the knock up will still grant immunity to it from either of Cyclone's casts.
