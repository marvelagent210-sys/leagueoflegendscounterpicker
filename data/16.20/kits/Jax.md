## Innate: Relentless Assault

Source: [Template:Data Jax/Relentless Assault](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Jax/Relentless_Assault?oldid=4038387) (revision 4038387). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies, Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Jax's basic attacks generate a stack of Relentless Assault on-attack for 2.5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent attacks and stacking up to 8 times. Stacks expire one by one every 0.25 seconds when the duration ends.

- **Mechanics:** on-attack

Relentless Assault: For each stack, Jax gains 5 / 6.5 / 8 / 9.5 / 11 / 12.5 / 14 (based on level)% bonus attack speed, up to a maximum of 40 / 52 / 64 / 76 / 88 / 100 / 112 (based on level)%.

Grandmaster at Angling: While out-of-combat with champions and idle in the river for 10 seconds, Jax will occasionally catch a fish, granting him 1 and 1 ability power for 5 seconds. He catches a fish at an average rate of one every 15 seconds. He also has a 5% chance to catch a rare fish that grants 10 and 10 ability power for 5 seconds.

Notes:

- Jax will start fishing while idle in the river if he is in-combat with only monsters (e.g. Epic monsters).
- Jax catching a fish is a random event and will play a special animation.
- The total number of fish caught during the game is displayed when fishing as Grandmaster at Angling passive.
- Relentless Assault uses its old icon in the Death Recap.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Jax enters Evasion for 2 seconds: a defensive stance that causes him to dodge all incoming non-turret basic attacks and take 25% reduced damage from all area of effect abilities sourced from champions. Counter Strike can be recast after 1 second, and does so automatically after the duration.

- **Mechanics:** dodge (2 s)

Recast: Jax deals magic damage to nearby enemies, with the total damage increased by 20% for each attack dodged, up to a 100% increase, and stuns them for 1 second.

- **Minimum Magic Damage:** 40 / 70 / 100 / 130 / 160 (+ 70% AP) (+ 4% of target's maximum health) → at max rank, level 20: 160 (+ 70% AP) (+ 4% targetMaxHealth)
- **Maximum Magic Damage:** 80 / 140 / 200 / 260 / 320 (+ 140% AP) (+ 8% of target's maximum health) → at max rank, level 20: 320 (+ 140% AP) (+ 8% targetMaxHealth)
- **Mechanics:** stun (1 s)

Notes:

- The initial cast and the manual recast count as ability activations for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- Counter Strike will also dodge abilities that can trigger on-hit effects (Parrrley, Mystic Shot) which will count towards Counter Strike's increased damage. There are exceptions of abilities that Counter Strike will not dodge but will dodge the damage from on-hit effects that they trigger (Alpha Strike, Piercing Darkness).
- The damage based on the target's maximum health is capped at 9000 against monsters. This is a placeholder value and impossible to reach because it requires a monster to have over 128571 health.

## R: Grandmaster-at-Arms

Source: [Template:Data Jax/Grandmaster-at-Arms](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Jax/Grandmaster-at-Arms?oldid=3973573) (revision 3973573). Ranks: 3.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Enemies, Self · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Jax's basic attacks generate a stack of Grandmaster-at-Arms on-hit for 2.5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits and stacking up to 2 times. At 2 stacks, his next basic attack on-hit is empowered to have an uncancellable windup and consume all stacks to deal additional magic damage, reduced to 50% against structures. While Grandmaster-at-Arms is active, the empowered attack triggers at 1 stack instead.

- **Additional Magic Damage:** 75 / 130 / 185 (+ 60% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 185 (+ 60% AP)
- **Mechanics:** on-hit; on-hit; uncancellable windup

Active: Jax swings his lantern around, dealing magic damage to nearby enemies. If this hits a champion, he gains bonus armor, increased for each champion hit beyond the first, and bonus magic resistance equal to 60% of that amount as well as 10% increased size for 8 seconds.

- **Magic Damage:** 100 / 175 / 250 (+ 100% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 250 (+ 100% AP)
- **Bonus Armor:** 45 / 60 / 75 (+ 40% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 75 (+ 40% bonusAD)
- **Bonus Magic Resistance:** 27 / 36 / 45 (+ 24% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 45 (+ 24% bonusAD)
- **Bonus Armor per Champion Hit:** 20 / 25 / 30 (+ 10% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 30 (+ 10% bonusAD)
- **Bonus Magic Resistance per Champion Hit:** 12 / 15 / 18 (+ 6% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 18 (+ 6% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** size change

Jax can move during Grandmaster-at-Arms' cast time.

Notes:

- The on-hit deals proc damage. The swing deals area damage.
- The bonus resistances are represented by a glowing aura around Jax that grows with each enemy champion hit beyond the first.
- The additional magic damage can be dodged and blocked but it cannot miss.
- Spell shield will only block the additional damage from the third attack if it is empowered.
- The empowered attack will trigger but not be consumed nor apply its effects against wards.
    - Stacks will still be generated when attacking them.
- The empowered attack is applied in a separate damage instance from Jax's basic attacks.
    - This causes effects like Bone Plating to be applied twice.
- * This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.
