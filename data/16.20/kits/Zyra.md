## Innate: Garden of Thorns

Source: [Template:Data Zyra/Garden of Thorns](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Zyra/Garden_of_Thorns?oldid=3986877) (revision 3986877). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Periodically, Zyra spawns one or two Seeds nearby that last for 30 seconds, granting sight over the surrounding area for 1 second.

- **Mechanics:** reveal (1 s)

After 1 second, enemy champions can destroy Seeds by moving on top of them.

Zyra can only have a total of 8 Seeds planted at a time, preserving Seeds spawned by Rampant Growth over Seeds spawned by Garden of Thorns.

Notes:

- : The pattern/probability of Garden of Thorns spawning one seed or two.
- Zyra tries to plant Seeds in brush and not on top of walls.
- Zyra tries not to plant Seeds too close together and at different angles.
- Zyra does not plant any Seeds if she is concealed within a brush (unless there is an enemy ward in it, in which case it takes her a little longer to plant the Seed) or when there is no room for additional ones.
- Zyra does not plant any Seeds if she is within 1400 units of the coordinate points (400, 400) or (14000, 14000), which are roughly at the center of the fountains on Summoner's Rift.
    - These same coordinates may not hold as fountain positions on other maps (e.g. on the Arena map, the Magma Chamber area overlaps with the second exclusion radius) and may prompt the implementation of this behaviour to be changed in the future.
- Zyra tries to plant Seeds close to an enemy near her.
- : Zyra tries to plant Seeds under enemy structures only if she is in basic attack range of them.
- : Zyra tries to plant Seeds on the other side of the wall she is facing and/or standing right next to.
- : Zyra tries to plant Seeds in the direction she is facing and/or moving.
- Zyra tries to plant Seeds near jungle path entrances.
- : Zyra tries to plant Seeds behind herself when she is walking through the river and there are no jungle path entrances near her.
- Zyra tries to plant Seeds between herself and the enemy champion she has vision of (or is heading towards her) in a 45º angle to the left or to the right depending on where on lane she is standing.
- Zyra tries to plant Seeds against the inner walls of epic monster pits (Dragon, Rift Herald, Baron Nashor)
- Zyra has a hidden passive that grants her 10% increased size for 33 seconds after having Sunlight applied to her.

Active: Zyra plants a Seed at the target location that remains for 60 seconds and grants sight over the surrounding area, though decreasing in radius after 1 second. After 1.5 seconds, enemy champions can destroy the Seed by stepping on it, which reveals them for 2 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** reveal; true sight (2 s)

- Seeds are untargetable.
- Rampant Growth cannot be learnt at level 1.

- **Magic Damage:** 200 / 300 / 400 (+ 70% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 400 (+ 70% AP)

After 2 seconds, the thicket snaps upward to knock up enemies within for 1 second.

- **Mechanics:** airborne (1 s)
