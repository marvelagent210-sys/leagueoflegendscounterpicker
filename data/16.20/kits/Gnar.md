## Innate: Rage Gene

Source: [Template:Data Gnar/Rage Gene](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Gnar/Rage_Gene?oldid=4062252) (revision 4062252). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate - Rage Gene: Mini Gnar generates 4 / 7 / 11 (based on level) Rage over 2 seconds upon dealing or receiving damage, up to 100. Against non-champions, basic attacks on-hit and Boomerang Throw instantly generate 0.5 / 0.875 / 1.375 (based on level) Rage. Against champions, Boomerang Throw instantly generates 1 / 1.75 / 2.75 (based on level) Rage, and basic attacks on-hit instantly generate 2 / 3.5 / 5.5 (based on level). Boomerang Throw can only generate Rage once per cast, and grants Rage based on the first enemy hit. Gnar's Rage decays after being out of combat for 13 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

At maximum Rage, Gnar's abilities switch to Mega Gnar abilities, for 4 seconds and until transforming back to his Mini Gnar form, with the exception of Hop, which switches after the transformation. Casting any of them during this time also causes Gnar to transform into Mega Gnar instantly, else he will transform automatically after the 4 seconds have elapsed.

Gnar's Mega form lasts for 15 seconds, with his Rage bar depleting as an indication of remaining time. Rage cannot be generated while in this form. Afterwards, Gnar returns to Mini Gnar, retaining his current percentage health and becoming too tired to generate Rage for 15 seconds.

Gnar gains different stat bonuses based on whether he is in Mini or Mega form. Both of Gnar's forms share their ability cooldowns.

Notes:

- The Rage generated upon dealing or receiving damage occurs over 2 seconds with 4 ticks every 0.5 seconds. It also does not stack but instead refreshes with subsequent applications (of dealing or receiving damage).
- In-game, the value on the Rage bar always displays rounded down to the nearest integer.
- Mega Gnar gained health is not affected by healing modifiers such as Grievous Wounds and those from items and runes.
- When returning to Mini Gnar, Gnar will only retain his current health percentage, not current health total.
- Gnar's abilities share cooldowns across forms.
- The Rage bar is white when under 60 range, yellow when equal to or above 60 rage, and red at 100 rage.
- If Gnar transforms into Mega Gnar while on enemy's sight and later goes back to Mini while on the fog of war, enemies will see Mega Gnar icon both in the scoreboard and minimap, even if they regain vision of Gnar. This will stay this way until Gnar's Mega form ends while on enemy's sight.

- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the start of the cast time.

### Assumptions

- Rage at the start of a fight (it builds on minions in lane): 50.
- At full Rage Gnar becomes Mega Gnar at once, rather than within the 4 s the wiki allows.
- Boomerang Throw flies at the average of its speeds (1950 out, 1330 back) and only its outgoing pass hits; Gnar always catches it on its way back, from the enemy or, missed, from its full range.
- The boulder drops against the enemy's near side; Mega Gnar steps onto it when it lies within 300 units of him and he isn't fleeing.
- Hyper's 80% movement speed decays evenly over 3 s.
- Hop and Crunch land 0.6 s after they leave the ground, however far they go (the game files' TravelTime; the wiki gives no speed). Hop is kept for getting away: its bounce off the enemy is left out.
- GNAR!'s knockback flies for the 0.4 s before its slow (the wiki gives no flight time).
