Active: Bel'Veth dashes in the designated direction, though not through terrain, dealing physical damage to enemies she passes through. Against the first target, Void Surge can critically strike for 200% damage and applies on-hit effects and life steal.

Void Surge deals 70 bonus physical damage to monsters.

Void Surge can be cast only within a cardinal direction that is off cooldown, and incurs a cooldown between casts. Each cardinal direction has a unique cooldown that is reduced equivalent to 0.2 ability haste per 1% bonus attack speed. These cooldowns reset upon respawning.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

- **Per-Direction Cooldown:** 16 / 15 / 14 / 13 / 12

Notes:

- Void Surge applies basic damage, additionally tagged as spell damage to the first (closest) enemy hit and area damage to secondary enemies:
    - Void Surge will apply spell effects to all targets hit.
    - Spell vamp will grant healing from the damage dealt to all targets, but healing from damage to secondary targets is reduced to 33% effectiveness.
    - Life steal will heal based on the damage dealt to the first target hit.

- While in True Form, Void Surge's dash distance is extended up to 225 units when targeted across terrain.
- Void Surge can be dodged and blocked only as the first enemy hit.

## R: Endless Banquet

Source: [Template:Data Bel'Veth/Endless Banquet](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Bel'Veth/Endless_Banquet?oldid=4053392) (revision 4053392). Ranks: 3.

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: True · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: special

Passive: Bel'Veth's basic attacks on-hit apply a mark to the target for 5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits. Every attack on-hit against the marked target deals bonus true damage and generates a stack of Endless Banquet that increases this damage by the same value. This effect stacks infinitely, but is capped at 8 stacks against epic monsters.

- **Bonus True Damage:** 2 / 4 / 6 (+ 3% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 6 (+ 3% bonusAD)
- **Maximum Monster Damage:** 16 / 32 / 48 (+ 24% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 48 (+ 24% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** on-hit; on-hit

Passive: When Bel'Veth scores a takedown against an enemy champion or epic monster while alive, a Void Coral is spawned from their corpse for 15 seconds. Baron Nashor, the pit Rift Herald, and Voidgrubs (once per spawn group) spawn an Enhanced Void Coral.

Active: Bel'Veth dashes to the target Void Coral to consume it over the cast time, slowing nearby enemies by 25 / 42 / 69 / 96 (based on seconds elapsed)% for the duration. She then creates an explosion at the location to deal true damage to enemies within, capped at 1500 versus monsters, and assumes her True Form for 45 / 90 / infinite (based on Lavender stacks) seconds. Bel'Veth consumes all existing Void Corals at once, generating a stack of Lavender for each one consumed.

- **True Damage:** 150 / 200 / 250 (+ 150% AP) (+ 20% of target's missing health) → at max rank, level 20: 250 (+ 150% AP) (+ 20% targetMissingHealth)
- **Mechanics:** dash; slow (depends on seconds elapsed%)

True Form: Bel'Veth evolves into a monster, gaining bonus health, bonus attack range, increased size, as well as increased total attack speed. Void Surge can dash through terrain. Consuming a Void Coral refreshes the duration of True Form and heals Bel'Veth. Enhanced Void Coral Bonus: True Form is empowered to causes Void Remora to spawn from allied and enemy minions that die nearby.

- **Bonus Health:** 100 / 250 / 400 (+ 150% bonus AD) (+ 150% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 400 (+ 150% bonusAD) (+ 150% AP)
- **Bonus Attack Range:** 25 / 75 / 125 → at max rank, level 20: 125
- **Increased Size:** 10 / 15 / 20% → at max rank, level 20: 20%
- **Increased Total Attack Speed:** 6 / 13 / 20% → at max rank, level 20: 20%
- **Heal:** 100 / 250 / 400 (+ 150% bonus AD) (+ 150% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 400 (+ 150% bonusAD) (+ 150% AP)
- **Mechanics:** dash; heal

A nearby Void Coral is required to cast this ability. The passive bonus true damage is affected by the on-hit modifier of Royal Maelstrom. See Pets for details about Void Remora and Void Corals.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

Notes:

- Applies proc damage on the bonus true damage and deals area damage on the explosion.
- Endless Banquet's takedown effect will not trigger against monsters if they were slain by an enemy.
- Endless Banquet's true damage based on the target's missing health is calculated before the base damage is dealt.
- True Form's duration is displayed in Bel'Veth's resource bar, in seconds.
    - The resource bar's color is white. It is purple when Bel'Veth has the enhanced Void Coral bonus.
- Bel'Veth's True Form at 80 Lavender stacks will last indefinitely, but is still lost upon death.
- Bel'Veth will consume all existing Void Corals at once, therefore she is able to acquire the enhanced effects of True Form even if she does not physically consume an enhanced Void Coral.
- The Void Coral that Bel'Veth consumes herself will not expire in the process of her doing so.
- Bel'Veth will consume all other Void Corals upon consumption regardless of range.
- Ranking up Endless Banquet will update the bonuses dynamically; if Bel'Veth has True Form upon the level-up, she will receive the upgraded stats.
- Bel'Veth gains the bonus health and heal from Endless Banquet at the start of the cast time.
- Bel'Veth will be able to consume the target Void Coral even if her dash is interrupted.
    - She dashes at the start of the cast time.
- A Void Coral will not spawn when scoring a takedown against a summoned Rift Herald.
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the start of the cast time.
- The following table refers for interactions while Bel'Veth is in cast time:
    - Movement summoner spells can be buffered to cast after the cast time completes if permitted.
    - She is locked out of movement and attack actions for 0.25 seconds after the cast time completes and no buffering of them is permitted for the entirety of her lockout.

- Lavender stacks by level 20 (1 per large minion or large monster, 2 per champion or epic monster takedown): 40.
- True Form at the start of the fight (1 = yes, 0 = no: it needs a Void Coral, which a duel has none of): 0.
