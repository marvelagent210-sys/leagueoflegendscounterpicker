## Innate: Void Shift

Source: [Template:Data Malzahar/Void Shift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Malzahar/Void_Shift?oldid=4035856) (revision 4035856). Ranks: 1.

| Field | Value |

| Static cooldown | 30 / 24 / 18 / 12 (based on level) |

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Periodically, Malzahar gains Void Shift until he takes non-minion damage or negates a crowd control effect, to which it then lingers for 0.25 seconds before expiring. Void Shift: Malzahar gains crowd control immunity and 90% damage reduction.

- **Mechanics:** cc-immune

Void Shift's cooldown refreshes whenever Malzahar takes non-minion damage or is affected by a crowd control effect, and resets upon respawning.

Notes:

- Void Shift will not resist allied crowd control nor will it be deactivated by them.
- Void Shift will not mitigate true damage but the buff will still be consumed.
    - It does not deactivate against the Nexus Obelisk.
- Shields will take priority over Void Shift, but the buff is always consumed, even if the shield fully mitigates the damage.
    - However, if Malzahar has a spell shield and is hit by an effect that isn't negated by the spell shield, then if the shield fully mitigates the damage, Void Shift will not be consumed.
- Spell shields take priority over Void Shift for abilities that contain a crowd control effect.
- Void Shift will specifically not deactivate if Malzahar eats Honeyfruit or is within a hostile Frozen Heart passive aura.
- Void Shift is special cased to prevent Chum the Waters, and Death Sentence from attaching to Malzahar.

Additionally, a Null Zone is opened beneath the target's location at the time of cast that lasts 5 seconds, dealing magic damage every 0.5 seconds to enemies within, capped at 120 per tick against minions and monsters.

- **Total Magic Damage:** 10 / 15 / 20% (+ 2.5% per 100 AP) of target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 20% of targetMaxHealth (+ 2.5% per100:AP)

Notes:

- Applies persistent damage for the tether, and persistent area damage additionally tagged as indirect to enemies within the Null Zone.

- As long as the target is within the tether radius, they will take the full tether damage over 2.5 seconds even if they are not tethered due to the tether being broken early into the channel.
    - This occurs regardless of how the tether was broken.
    - If the target exits tether range they will stop taking damage, but can be damaged again by the remaining ticks if they re-enter the tether range within the duration.
- Removing the suppression will also remove the reveal.
- Malzahar places himself onto the ground and interrupts displacements affecting him upon starting the channel.
- If a target is currently being affected by Malefic Visions, casting Nether Grasp on them will grant 2 stacks toward effects such as Electrocute.
    - This is, correctly, not the case if Nether Grasp is cast before Malefic Visions.
