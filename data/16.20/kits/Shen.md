## Innate: Ki Barrier

Source: [Template:Data Shen/Ki Barrier](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Shen/Ki_Barrier?oldid=3985839) (revision 3985839). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: After completing an ability's effects, Shen grants himself a shield for 47 – 120 (based on level) (+ 13% bonus health) for 2.5 seconds. If Shen affects at least one other champion with an ability, Ki Barrier's cooldown is reduced by 4 – 8 (based on level) seconds.

- **Mechanics:** shield (2.5 s)

Shen manifests a Spirit Blade that he can control with Twilight Assault and Spirit's Refuge.

Notes:

- The Spirit Blade spawns with Shen, teleports closer to him if he gets too far away, and instantly blinks to him if he uses any global blink (Teleport, Recall, or Stand United). It will also dissappear if Shen dies and reappear upon respawning.
- Enemies see an arrow pointing from Shen to the location of his Spirit Blade even if it is not visible.
- Twilight Assault will grant the shield if the Spirit Blade reaches Shen. If it is stopped, no shield is granted. It will grant the cooldown reduction upon hitting an enemy champion.
- Spirit's Refuge will grant the shield and/or the cooldown reduction after the protective zone disappears. It does not need to block attacks to trigger the cooldown reduction, just touch an ally champion at any point of the duration.
- Shadow Dash will grant the shield when the dash ends. It will grant the cooldown reduction upon hitting an enemy champion.
    - If the dash is interrupted, the shield will instead be received the moment the crowd control source is applied, before damage calculation (if any).
- Stand United will give the shield and/or the cooldown reduction upon completing the channel.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

If the Spirit Blade hits at least one enemy champion during the travel, the attacks are further enhanced to deal increased bonus magic damage and additionally grant Shen 50% bonus attack speed.

- **Increased Bonus Damage:** 10 / 16 / 22 / 28 / 34 / 40 (based on level) (+ 5 / 5.5 / 6 / 6.5 / 7% (+ 2% per 100 AP) of target's maximum health) → at max rank, level 20: depends on level (+ 7% targetMaxHealth [2% per100:AP])

- **Maximum Monster Damage:** 100 / 125 / 150 / 175 / 200 → at max rank, level 20: 200

Notes:

- The Spirit Blade will track Shen as it is traveling to him, following his movements if he changes locations while the blade is in-flight.
- The Spirit Blade will stop in place upon colliding with projectile-intercepting effects. If this occurs, Shen will not gain the empowered attacks as the Spirit Blade failed to complete its travel to him.
- When it is recalled, the Spirit Blade also checks for units whose gameplay radius it overlaps with (equal to a 0 radius check) before flying off, allowing it to hit enemies whose center is behind the missile origin.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against wards.

- **Minimum Shield Strength:** 120 / 220 / 320 (+ 135% AP) (+ 15% of his bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 320 (+ 135% AP) (+ 15% bonusHealth)

- **Mechanics:** channel (3 s); shield (5 s)

Upon completing the channel, Shen and his Spirit Blade blink to the target ally's location, placed between them and the nearest enemy champion. Shen also destroys any projectiles targeting him at the time of completion.

- **Mechanics:** blink

Notes:

- Due to several character stats needing to be recalculated each time a character spawns, casting Stand United immediately upon respawning will cause its cooldown to be unaffected by ability haste, and the shielding will not be increased by Shen's ability power, but will be increased by his bonus maximum health.
- Stand United can target allies via the mini map or on their portraits or by using the F2 - F5 keys (none of this can be done if Shen is affected by nearsight or his target is untargetable).
- Shen will turn to face towards his target ally at the start of the channel.
    - If the target is stealthed, enemies could, in theory, use this to estimate their location.
- Shen will channel so long as his target is alive (even if the granted shield is broken). If his target dies, the channel is canceled and Stand United goes on full cooldown.
    - Interrupting Stand United's channel will not remove the shield prematurely.
- The target's screen will glow purple to telegraph that Shen has targeted them with Stand United.
    - It will change back when the channel ends.
- Shen finds the nearest visible enemy champion within 2000 range from the ally or the nearest invisible champion within 600 range to place himself 175 units from his ally towards that enemy.
- Stand United has a forgiveness radius of 100 units.
- Experimental Hexplate Overdrive, Fiendhunter Bolts Opening Barrage, and Zeke's Convergence Frostfire Tempest will activate at the end of Stand United's channel.
