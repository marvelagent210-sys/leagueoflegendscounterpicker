## R: UPGRADE!!!

Source: [Template:Data Heimerdinger/UPGRADE!!!](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Heimerdinger/UPGRADE!!!?oldid=4006857) (revision 4006857). Ranks: 3.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Cooldown | 100 / 85 / 70 |
| Static cooldown | 3 |
| Cost | 100 mana |
| Cast time | none |
| Cooldown starts | starts after using an empowered ability |

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Toggle: Heimerdinger empowers his next basic ability for an additional effect, consuming the empowerment upon cast and placing UPGRADE!!! on full cooldown. These empowered abilities have the same cooldowns as their basic forms, deal modified damage, and do not have a mana cost.

UPGRADE!!! incurs its static cooldown on-cast, and can be toggled off to remove the empowerment and place the ability on its static cooldown.

UPGRADE!!! can be used while affected by cast-inhibiting crowd control.

Notes:

- Toggled abilities do not count as ability activations for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
    - Consuming the buff for an empowered basic ability does.
- No compensations for mana are made if UPGRADE!!! is toggled off; Heimerdinger will lose 100 mana each time he toggles the ability on and off.
