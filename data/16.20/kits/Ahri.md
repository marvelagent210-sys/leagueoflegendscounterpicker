Active: Ahri sends her orb in the target direction that deals magic damage to enemies it passes through. Upon reaching maximum range, it returns to her to deal the same amount in true damage to enemies it passes through.

- **Damage Per Pass:** 35 / 60 / 85 / 110 / 135 (+ 50% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 135 (+ 50% AP)
- **Total Mixed Damage:** 70 / 120 / 170 / 220 / 270 (+ 100% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 270 (+ 100% AP)

Enemies can be hit only once per pass.

Notes:

- Orb of Deception will hit additional units around the return point in a small circle when the orb turns around.
    - This applies both to the initial and return missile's respective areas of effect (though the initial missile only registers a hit around the return point if the unit was not hit by it already, since it can only register once per pass).
- If Ahri dies while the orb is out, the orb will visually disappear but continue to deal damage and return to Ahri.
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.

## R: Spirit Rush

Source: [Template:Data Ahri/Spirit Rush](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Ahri/Spirit_Rush?oldid=4074438) (revision 4074438). Ranks: 3.

| Field | Value |

Targeting: Location · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: true · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Active: Ahri dashes to the target location and then fires essence bolts to up to 3 nearby visible enemies, each dealing magic damage. Spirit Rush can be recast twice more within 15 seconds of the activation at no additional cost, with a 1-second static cooldown between casts. Consuming a champion's essence with Essence Theft while Spirit Rush is active extends the recast duration by and up to 10 seconds, and grants an additional recast, storing up to 3 recasts at a time.

- **Magic Damage:** 75 / 125 / 175 (+ 35% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 175 (+ 35% AP)
- **Mechanics:** dash; reveal

Recast: Ahri mimics the first cast's effects.

Spirit Rush's recast duration will persist even after using all recasts. Fox-Fire can be cast during the dash.

Notes:

- This ability always employs quick cast.
- Each cast counts as an ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- There exists a slight delay before a champion gains vision of the fog of war once inside it. Because of this, if Ahri dashes into it, it is possible that Spirit Rush will not target any enemy in range.
- The bolts do not fire if Ahri dies while dashing.
    - The bolts will fire if the dash is interrupted by other means, including by entering resurrection.
