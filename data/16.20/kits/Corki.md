## Innate: Hextech Munitions

Source: [Template:Data Corki/Hextech Munitions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Corki/Hextech_Munitions?oldid=4050625) (revision 4050625). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: Physical True · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Corki's basic attacks deal bonus true damage equal to 20% AD. This damage is affected by critical strike modifiers.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury bolts and all Spellblade effects are special cased to benefit from Hextech Munitions, causing them to deal bonus true damage equal to 20% of their pre-mitigation damage.

Notes:

- The mixed damage is dealt in two simultaneous instances of damage, but will pretend to be a single instance for most effects (such as Conqueror stacking).
    - The true damage is dealt in an instance before the physical damage.
    - Both instances deal basic damage and thus natively apply life steal.
    - The attack applies on-hit effects only once, right at the start.
    - Even if the target dies from the true damage, the physical damage will still be applied to it.
      - If the target dies from on-hit damage, the true damage portion will be skipped and only the physical damage applied.

    - Issuing an Attack Move order at a location will not cause Corki to instantly turn.
