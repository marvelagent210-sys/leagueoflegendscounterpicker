## Innate: Blood Well

Source: [Template:Data Aatrox/Blood Well](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Aatrox/Blood_Well?oldid=3288754) (revision 3288754). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Aatrox fills his Blood Well through his abilities, depleting by (20% maximum) per second while out of combat. When the Blood Well is filled, Aatrox becomes Hellbent for 4 seconds as the Blood Well drains. While Hellbent, dealing or taking damage against enemy champions or neutral monsters will completely fill the Blood Well and refresh Hellbent's duration.

Hellbent: Aatrox gains 25% increased base AD and 30% / 40% / 50% / 60% (based on level) bonus attack speed. Upon taking fatal damage Aatrox will revive over 3 seconds, recovering 30% of maximum health over the duration. Upon reviving, Aatrox instantly recovers 100% of Blood Well. Aatrox's revive is then put on a cooldown.

Notes:

- Aatrox's resource bar colors red when Hellbent is active (light red when the revive is off cooldown, dark red when on cooldown)
- Chronoshift and Guardian Angel passive will take precedence over Blood Well's revive.
- Blood Well's stasis causes Aatrox to become untargetable, which will reset turret aggro.
- Tethers from abilities (Soul Shackles) will remain active while Aatrox is reviving.
- Blood Well's healing component interacts with Spirit Visage passive.

## Q: The Darkin Blade

Source: [Template:Data Aatrox/The Darkin Blade](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Aatrox/The_Darkin_Blade?oldid=4074361) (revision 4074361). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Direction · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Aatrox can activate The Darkin Blade three times before the ability goes on cooldown, with a 1-second static cooldown between casts. If Aatrox does not recast the ability within 4 seconds of the previous cast, it goes on cooldown.

Active: Aatrox performs a strike with his greatsword for each of the three casts, dealing physical damage to enemies hit within an area. Enemies hit within a Sweetspot of the area take 75% bonus damage and are also knocked up for 0.25 seconds. Each subsequent cast gains 25% more damage.

- **Mechanics:** airborne (0.25 s)

First Cast: Aatrox's first strike affects a 625 × 180-unit rectangular area in the target direction, with him centered on the back line and the Sweetspot at the farthest edge.

- **First Cast Damage:** 10 / 25 / 40 / 55 / 70 (+ 60 / 67.5 / 75 / 82.5 / 90% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 70 (+ 90% AD)
- **First Sweetspot Damage:** 17.5 / 43.75 / 70 / 96.25 / 122.5 (+ 105 / 118.125 / 131.25 / 144.375 / 157.5% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 122.5 (+ 157.5% AD)

Second Cast: Aatrox's second strike affects a trapezoidal area in the target direction, with the Sweetspot at the farthest edge. The hitbox begins 100-units behind Aatrox and extends 475-units in front of him, measuring between 300 and 500-units wide from behind to in front.

- **Second Cast Damage:** 12.5 / 31.25 / 50 / 68.75 / 87.5 (+ 75 / 84.375 / 93.75 / 103.125 / 112.5% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 87.5 (+ 112.5% AD)
- **Second Sweetspot Damage:** 21.875 / 54.6875 / 87.5 / 120.313 / 153.125 (+ 131.25 / 147.656 / 164.063 / 180.469 / 196.875% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 153.125 (+ 196.875% AD)

Third Cast: Aatrox's third strike affects a 300-radius circular area centered on a target location that is 200 units in front of him, with a 180-radius Sweetspot within.

- **Third Cast Damage:** 15 / 37.5 / 60 / 82.5 / 105 (+ 90 / 101.25 / 112.5 / 123.75 / 135% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 105 (+ 135% AD)
- **Third Sweetspot Damage:** 26.25 / 65.625 / 105 / 144.375 / 183.75 (+ 157.5 / 177.188 / 196.875 / 216.563 / 236.25% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 183.75 (+ 236.25% AD)

The Darkin Blade deals 55 / 60 / 65 / 70 (based on level)% damage against minions. Against monsters, the first cast deals 25 bonus physical damage, which is affected by the subsequent cast and Sweetspot damage modifiers; and the knock up duration from hitting the Sweetspot against them is doubled to 0.5 seconds.

- **Maximum Non-Minion Non-Sweetspot Damage:** 37.5 / 93.75 / 150 / 206.25 / 262.5 (+ 225 / 253.125 / 281.25 / 309.375 / 337.5% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 262.5 (+ 337.5% AD)
- **Maximum Non-Minion Sweetspot Damage:** 65.625 / 164.063 / 262.5 / 360.938 / 459.375 (+ 393.75 / 442.969 / 492.188 / 541.406 / 590.625% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 459.375 (+ 590.625% AD)
- **Mechanics:** airborne

Notes:

- The first and second casts function like any point-blank AOE (ground/area effect centered at or offset to the caster). An enemy is considered to be hit by them based on edge range - i.e. the hit registers if any part of the enemy's gameplay radius is within the hitbox.
- The third cast, as well as the Sweetspot of the first and second cast, register their hit based on center range. The enemy's center must be within the hitbox to apply the effects.
- As implied by the previous point, the Sweetspot for the first and second cast is implemented as separate areas of effect to the main component of the ability. Enemies must be within both areas to trigger the bonus damage, knock up and Deathbringer Stance cooldown reduction.
    - The 'target hit successfully' SFX plays whenever an enemy is within the Sweetspot - meaning it is possible to trigger the sound effect without affecting an enemy.
- Each cast counts as an ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- The Darkin Blade's angle of cast is fixed during the cast time, i.e. it maintains its direction in relation to Aatrox and does not respect any changes in his facing direction.
- If Aatrox changes his position during The Darkin Blade's cast, his real facing direction for effects such as Petrifying Gaze is the direction he is moving and not the direction the model is facing.
- Aatrox cannot declare basic attacks shortly after casting The Darkin Blade.
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.

    - If Blood Thirst/Price is blocked, the effect will still apply, but the base damage will not.
