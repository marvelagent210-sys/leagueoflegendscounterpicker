## Innate: A Harmless Scarecrow

Source: [Template:Data Fiddlesticks/A Harmless Scarecrow](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Fiddlesticks/A_Harmless_Scarecrow?oldid=4058532) (revision 4058532). Ranks: 1.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Effect radius | 900 |

Targeting: Vector · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate - Scarecrow Effigy: Fiddlesticks begins the game with an exclusive Scarecrow Effigy, which permanently occupies the trinket slot. Fiddlesticks can pretend to be an Effigy by standing still and not acting nor being acted upon for 2 seconds, during which it hides its health bar, adjusts its body into a scarecrow and extends its arm out to expose its lantern.

Innate - A Harmless Scarecrow: From level 6 onward, placing an Effigy also summons a Sweeper Drone at the location for 6 seconds.

Notes:

- The 2-second duration stated in the ability description is not accurate. The actual time required is slightly longer and appears to be inconsistent. It usually takes approximately 2.2 seconds, but may take up to 4.4 seconds after casting a spell for unknown reasons.
- Attempting to impersonate an Effigy, or maintaining the impersonation, will be interrupted if Fiddlesticks:
    - Moves.
      - There is a brief grace period after moving where Fiddlesticks is still considered an effigy.
    - Is affected by a crowd control effect.
    - Is hit by an ability.
    - Takes damage.
      - Fiddlesticks can still enter the effigy state while taking damage from non-champion units due to the preparation time being uninterrupted by non-champion damage.
      - Ocean Drake and Hextech Drake are exceptions, as their attacks apply crowd control effects which interfere with the impersonation.
    - Declares an attack.
    - Casts an ability or summoner spell.
    - Uses an item's active effect, potion, or consumable, including Support Quest items and Control Ward.
      - Using Scarecrow Effigy does not interrupt the impersonation.
    - Begins channeling.
- While pretending to be an effigy, when standing within a 225 radius of a faelight, Fiddlesticks imitates superward visual effects for 39 seconds or until interrupted.
    - While within 75-225 radius of a faelight, Fiddlesticks will blink into a 75 radius of the faelight's location.
- The trinket slot can still be temporarily replaced by Eye of the Herald.
- - Surprise Party Fiddlesticks has a special icon for this ability.

## Q: Terrify

Source: [Template:Data Fiddlesticks/Terrify](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Fiddlesticks/Terrify?oldid=4058043) (revision 4058043). Ranks: 5.

| Field | Value |

| Cooldown | 15 / 14.5 / 14 / 13.5 / 13 |

| Cost | 65 mana |

| Target range | 575 |

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: true · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: While Fiddlesticks has been out of combat for at least 2.25 seconds and is not visible to the enemy team, or is impersonating an Effigy, its damaging ability will additionally fear targets hit for a duration.

- **Fear Duration:** 1.2 / 1.4 / 1.6 / 1.8 / 2 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 2 s
- **Mechanics:** fear (2 s)

Active: Fiddlesticks launches a crow at the target enemy which arrives after 0.35 seconds, dealing magic damage and fearing them for a duration. Terrify has a minimum damage threshold and is capped at 400 against monsters.

- **Magic Damage:** 4 / 4.5 / 5 / 5.5 / 6% (+ 3% per 100 AP) of target's current health → at max rank, level 20: 6% of targetCurrentHealth (+ 3% per100:AP)
- **Minimum Damage:** 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 / 120 → at max rank, level 20: 120
- **Fear Duration:** 1.2 / 1.4 / 1.6 / 1.8 / 2 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 2 s
- **Mechanics:** fear (2 s)

Targets feared by Fiddlesticks by any means cannot be affected by it again for a duration equal to Terrify's cooldown. Against these targets, Terrify's damage and minimum threshold are doubled.

- **Increased Magic Damage:** 8 / 9 / 10 / 11 / 12% (+ 6% per 100 AP) of target's current health → at max rank, level 20: 12% of targetCurrentHealth (+ 6% per100:AP)
- **Increased Minimum Damage:** 80 / 120 / 160 / 200 / 240 → at max rank, level 20: 240

Terrify slows affected targets by 90%.

- **Mechanics:** slow (90%)

Notes:

- The fear-immunity timer is not refreshed by subsequent attempts to fear the target.
    - Epic monsters are immune to the crowd control effect of fear, but are still affected by the fear-immunity timer. Terrify will therefore deal doubled damage to them while the timer is active.
- The fear-immunity timer is applied even if the target is crowd control immune. It is not applied if the effect is blocked by a spell shield.
- The fear from Terrify is applied at the end of the cast time, regardless of the target's distance.
    - Since V14.14, the missile can be destroyed by projectile-interception effects.
- Terrify will still cast and affect the target even if Fiddlesticks dies or loses sight of them during the cast time.
- The meaning of "out of combat" in Terrify's description is somewhat imprecise. For the purposes of its passive, Fiddlesticks becomes eligible to fear approximately 2.25 seconds after it has stopped dealing damage, not taken damage from an enemy champion, and not been affected by crowd control.
    - Dealing damage to any unit counts for this purpose, including champions, minions, monsters, turrets, inhibitors, jungle plants, wards, and summoned units.
      - Damage dealt to monsters by Jungle Companions also does not reset the timer.
    - Using Exhaust on an enemy champion also breaks this state despite dealing no damage. Other non-damaging summoner spells do not.
    - Damage from non-champion units does not break this state. However, crowd control from non-champion units still does, which is especially relevant when fighting Ocean Drake and Hextech Drake.
- Enemy sight from any source can prevent the unseen condition from being satisfied.
    - impersonating an effigy allows Bountiful Harvest and Reap to fear enemies even when seen.
- The passive portion does not communicate any information to Fiddlesticks, its allies, nor its enemies, of whether Fiddlesticks is eligible to apply fear.
    - While Fiddlesticks is channeling Crowstorm, a visual indicator will circle around enemy champions if the unseen requirement was satisfied when the channel began. This indicator is visible only to Fiddlesticks, but does not guarantee that Crowstorm will apply fear, as enemy gaining sight or taking champion damage during the channel can invalidate the passive before Crowstorm deals damage.
    - The indicator briefly appears when Crowstorm is cast even if Terrify has not been learned, but disappears almost immediately.
- There is a very brief grace period after Fiddlesticks becomes visible or enters combat during which Terrify's passive can remain active.
    - This allows Fiddlesticks to move slightly out of vision-blocking terrain, such as brush, or immediately retaliate after being revealed and still apply fear.
    - This grace period is shorter than the cast times of Fiddlesticks's basic abilities, so the fear-applying effect can not affect multiple basic abilities.
    - Crowstorm is an exception. After Fiddlesticks blinks from an unseen location into enemy vision using Crowstorm, an ability immediately cast after arriving will still apply fear before the grace period expires. This allows interactions such as Crowstorm into Reap, as well as the Crowstorm–Bountiful Harvest–Flash interaction.
- Flashing into enemy vision immediately removes Terrify's passive fear-applying eligibility and removes the normal grace period.
    - However, if an ability that is already eligible to apply fear is cast during the grace period before using Flash, that cast retains its fear effect after the Flash. This allows for the Crowstorm–Bountiful Harvest–Flash interaction.
- The interaction between Terrify's passive and monsters differs from its interaction with enemy champions.
    - If Fiddlesticks is out of combat and not visible to a particular monster, Bountiful Harvest, Reap, and Crowstorm will always apply fear to that monster even if Fiddlesticks is visible to the enemy team.
      - Visibility is checked individually for each monster. Fiddlesticks may be visible to other monsters, including monsters from the same camp, without preventing fear against a monster whose sight of it is obstructed.
    - If Fiddlesticks is out of combat and is visible to a particular monster, Bountiful Harvest will not apply fear to that monster.
    - If Fiddlesticks is out of combat, is visible to a particular monster as well as being visible to the enemy team, Reap and Crowstorm will not apply fear to that monster.
      - Crowstorm behaves differently against monsters than against champions. Even if Crowstorm's initial channel location is visible to a particular monster and to the enemy team, if the first damaging instance of Crowstorm occurs in a location where Fiddlesticks is not visible to that monster, that monster will be feared.
        - Subsequent damage instances of Crowstorm cannot independently satisfy the out-of-combat condition because the first instance has already caused Fiddlesticks to enter combat.
- Minions use the same fear conditions as enemy champions.
- The player's screen will have a visual effect when successfully feared by Terrify.
- - Surprise Party Fiddlesticks has a special icon for this ability.
