## Innate: Blaze

Source: [Template:Data Brand/Blaze](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Brand/Blaze?oldid=4052621) (revision 4052621). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Brand's abilities apply a stack of Ablaze to enemies hit for 4 seconds, stacking up to 3 times and refreshing the duration of all stacks with each application. Subsequent abilities used against a target with Ablaze are empowered. Killing an enemy afflicted with Ablaze or any enemy with an ability restores 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 (based on level) mana.

Ablaze: While afflicted with Ablaze, the target is dealt magic damage equal to 2% of their maximum health over 4 seconds. Each stack of Ablaze deals damage to the target individually and applies their damage ticks dependent on the time they were applied, even if the duration of all the stacks are refreshed. Ablaze deals 270% damage per tick to monsters; this damage is capped at 12.5 / 25 / 37.5 (based on Ablaze stacks) per tick against non-epic monsters and 20 / 40 / 60 (based on Ablaze stacks) per tick against epic monsters.

Upon applying 3 stacks of Ablaze to a large monster or enemy champion, the fire becomes unstable, causing it to form a fiery ring around the target that grants sight within its radius and, after 2 seconds, consumes their stacks to explode. All enemies within the detonation are applied a stack of Ablaze and dealt magic damage equal to 6 – 12 (based on level)% (+ 2% per 100 AP) of their maximum health, capped at 270 / 355 / 440 / 525 (based on level) against monsters.

- **Mechanics:** reveal

Brand cannot stack Ablaze more than once on enemies that have had a ring explode from them in the last 4 seconds.

The ring will form even if the target dies to the ability applying the third stack, and will still trigger the explosion if the target dies before it.

Notes:

- The burn deals persistent damage, and the explosion deals area damage; both are additionally tagged as indirect damage.
- Ablaze is a debuff that persists through death.
- Ablaze's damage cap against a monster is applied after the increased monster damage.
- The explosion is not considered a tether effect and will not affect untargetable targets, even if the explosion originated from them.
- Given the behavior of the stacking mechanic, the damage over time will deal varying amounts of damage based on the time the stacks were applied:
    - At sub-optimal applied times, 2 stacks will deal a minimum of 6% maximum health damage over 4 seconds, but will deal 7% maximum health at optimal applied times.
    - Against small-medium units 3 stacks will deal a minimum of 9% maximum health at sub-optimal times, but 11% maximum health at optimal times. If these stacks are further refreshed at optimal times they can deal up to 12% maximum health over 4 seconds.

### Assumptions

- Blaze's committed source specifies 2% maximum-health damage over 4 seconds but no tick cadence; the model uses sixteen equal 0.25-second ticks per stack. Existing stacks preserve their independent pulse clocks when refreshed.
- Blaze follows the description's explicit 2% per-stack formula. The same revision's illustrative notes claim 6–7% over 4 seconds for two stacks and 9–11% for three, which this equal per-stack model does not reproduce; this source inconsistency remains unresolved.
- Each Pyroclasm leg restarts at the sourced 750 units/second and accelerates by 500 units/second each second, capped at the sourced 3000. The source does not explicitly state whether bounce legs retain velocity; this model resets it at each leg.
- Pyroclasm bounces only between the sole enemy champion and Brand, allowing three enemy hits when each intervening bounce has a target within 600 units. Extra minion/pet bounce targets and Conflagration's splash onto them are not modeled.
- Blaze's stack clocks and detonation are tracked on the enemy champion; burn stacks on enemy pets and the explosion's splash damage to those pets are not modeled.
- Blaze grants no pre-fight stacks. The duel ends on champion death, so a ring persisting through death cannot change its recorded result.
- The stat-budget reference estimates sustained Ablaze uptime and detonation frequency from spell cooldowns; fight damage instead uses the actual landed hits, stack clocks, and bounce paths.
