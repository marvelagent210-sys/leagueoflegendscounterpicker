## Innate: Reign of Anger

Source: [Template:Data Renekton/Reign of Anger](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Renekton/Reign_of_Anger?oldid=3961774) (revision 3961774). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: unknown · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Renekton's basic attacks generate 5 Fury on-hit. After 12 seconds of being out of combat and not basic attacking, he loses 1 Fury every 0.25 seconds. Entering combat or using an attack will stop the Fury loss and refresh the timer. Additionally, while Dominus is active, the timer is continuously refreshed.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

While Renekton has at least 50 Fury, his next basic ability, excluding Slice, consumes 50 Fury to become empowered with an additional effect. Empowered abilities do not generate Fury.

Renekton generates 50% bonus Fury from all sources while below 50% of his maximum health.

Notes:

- Basic attacks against any enemy that is not a structure will generate the Fury.
    - Renekton can generate Fury by collecting Honeyfruit and Health Relics (interacts with Reign of Anger's bonus generation).
- Fury generation with parrying effects (blind, dodge, block)

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Reign of Anger Bonus: Renekton instead strikes 3 times, completely destroying damage-mitigating shields on the target upon the first strike if they are not a monster, as well as increasing the stun duration to 1.5 seconds.

- **Total Physical Damage:** 15 / 60 / 105 / 150 / 195 (+ 225% AD) → at max rank, level 20: 195 (+ 225% AD)
- **Mechanics:** shield; stun

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- Each strike of Ruthless Predator deals basic damage, but the first strike also triggers spell effects by also being tagged as spell damage.
- If the target becomes untargetable or dies during the empowered attack's cast time, it is cancelled but not consumed.
- Each strike of Ruthless Predator counts as a separate cast instance for effects such as Electrocute and Eclipse Ever Rising Moon.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against structures.
- Even if empowered Ruthless Predator is blocked by a spell shield, it will still destroy any existing damage shields on the target.
- Ruthless Predator does not cause Renekton to attack twice/thrice. Effects that trigger on-attack or interact with the attack windup will only trigger once and thus do not interact with Ruthless Predator. (e.g. Lethal Tempo, Hail of Blades).
