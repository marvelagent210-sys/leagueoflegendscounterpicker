## Innate: Lightslinger

Source: [Template:Data Lucian/Lightslinger](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Lucian/Lightslinger?oldid=4064854) (revision 4064854). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: After casting an ability, Lucian's next basic attack within 3.5 seconds fires an additional shot on-attack after 0.25 seconds, which deals 50 / 55 / 60 (based on level)% AD physical damage, increased to 100% AD against minions. He will shoot the same target, else another target within 50 units beyond his basic attack range automatically.

- **Mechanics:** on-attack

The second shot applies life steal and on-hit effects, triggers on-attack effects, and is affected by critical strike modifiers.

- **Mechanics:** life steal; life steal; on-hit; on-attack; critical strike

Innate - Vigilance: Whenever Lucian is healed or shielded by an ally, or an enemy champion within 1000 units is immobilized, his next two shots within 6 seconds are empowered to deal 5 (+ 15% AD) bonus magic damage on-hit. He can store up to 4 empowered shots at a time.

- **Mechanics:** heal; shield; on-hit

Notes:

- Lightslinger is triggered and consumed by the first attack, even if no target can be found upon firing the second attack.
- The second attack, if the initial target was killed, will prioritize enemy champions regardless of having sight of them or not and enemy minions with low health.
- Lucian can perform actions freely while firing Lightslinger's second attack.
    - The second attack has special movement animations depending on the direction Lucian is moving while firing.
- The second attack counts as a separate hit for effects such as Electrocute, Muramana Shock, and Eclipse Ever Rising Moon.
- The second attack separately rolls a critical strike.
- The second attack does not trigger Sundered Sky Lightshield Strike.
- The empowered shots will apply against structures.

Passive: Relentless Pursuit's current cooldown is reduced by 1 second for each Lightslinger shot hit, doubled to 2 seconds against enemy champions.

Active: Lucian dashes in the target direction.

- **Mechanics:** dash

Relentless Pursuit resets Lucian's basic attack timer. Lucian can cast any of his abilities during the dash. Relentless Pursuit can be cast during W cast time and during R channel.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- Additional on-hits during Lightslinger are not considered for the cooldown reduction (i.e Runaan's Hurricane, Guinsoo's Rageblade).
- Unlike some dashes, Lucian's dash speed does not scale with his movement speed.
- Relentless Pursuit's dash can go through terrain, but will not be extended further if the target location is within terrain, unlike other dashes, like Ekko's Phase Dive.
- Casting Relentless Pursuit directly after attacking with Lightslinger will reduce Relentless Pursuit's cooldown when the projectiles hit the target.
- Relentless Pursuit can be cast during Recall cast time.
    - If Relentless Pursuit is cast just as the cast time ends, Lucian will not perform his Recall animation, instead staying idle. This allows Lucian to use his emotes during his Recall.
