## Innate: Crimson Pact

Source: [Template:Data Vladimir/Crimson Pact](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Vladimir/Crimson_Pact?oldid=4073468) (revision 4073468). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Vladimir gains (3.333% bonus health) as ability power and (160% AP) as bonus health. These two bonuses do not stack with each other.

Notes:

- Crimson Pact only affects Vladimir's maximum health and will not increase Vladimir's current health to prevent Vladimir from restoring health whenever his ability power fluctuates.
    - The only exception is purchasing applicable items while inside the fountain, which will restore health equal to the bonus gained.
    - Vladimir's current health can decrease to match his maximum health if Crimson Pact's bonus health is lost.
- Crimson Pact's bonus ability power stacks multiplicatively with other sources of % ability power but its bonus health stacks additively with other sources of bonus health. With Rabadon's Deathcap Magical Opus, the total is (30% AP + 4.3329% bonus health) bonus ability power and (208% AP + 1.59984% bonus health) bonus health.
- Crimson Pact and Riftmaker Void Infusion create a recursive stat loop.

Active: Vladimir drains blood from the target enemy, dealing magic damage and healing himself. He then generates 1 point of Crimson Rush over the cooldown.

- **Magic Damage:** 80 / 100 / 120 / 140 / 160 (+ 60% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 160 (+ 60% AP)
- **Heal:** 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 (+ 35% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 40 (+ 35% AP)
- **Mechanics:** heal

At 2 points of Crimson Rush, Vladimir surges, gaining 10% / 20% / 30% / 40% (based on level) bonus movement speed decaying over 0.5 seconds while the Crimson Rush depletes over 2.5 seconds. Crimson Rush depletes 75% slower during Sanguine Pool, Tides of Blood, or stasis.

- **Mechanics:** stasis

Casting Transfusion during the surge consumes all Crimson Rush to deal 85% increased damage and heal Vladimir for an additional 30 – 200 (based on level) (+ 5% (+ 4% per 100 AP) of his missing health). The additional healing is reduced to 35% against minions.

- **Increased Damage:** 148 / 185 / 222 / 259 / 296 (+ 111% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 296 (+ 111% AP)
- **Mechanics:** heal

Notes:

- Even though the healing effect is visualized as a projectile, the healing itself triggers as soon as the cast time completes.
- Vladimir's resource bar indicates his current Crimson Rush and changes colors depending on the charge-up stage of his surge.
- # White while generating the first stack and while at 1 stack (there is no time-out period).
- # Orange while generating the second stack.
- # Red while he is surging (Crimson Rush will deplete over 2.5 seconds once triggered).
    - Each stack generates over-time (Transfusion's cooldown).
- Vladimir can cast Sanguine Pool and Hemoplague during Transfusion's cast time.
- The Crimson Rush depletes normally while under resurrection effects.
- Transfusion's cast is special cased to not be cancelled if the target becomes untargetable, dies, or is too far away or no longer in sight during the cast time.
    - If the target is untargetable by the time the cast completes, however, Tranfusion will not deal them damage but still grant Vladimir the healing.

## W: Sanguine Pool

Source: [Template:Data Vladimir/Sanguine Pool](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Vladimir/Sanguine_Pool?oldid=4034923) (revision 4034923). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Vladimir sinks into a pool of blood, becoming untargetable and ghosted for 2 seconds. He also gains 37.5% bonus movement speed that decays exponentially over 1 second.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable (2 s); ghosted (2 s)

Enemies within the pool are dealt magic damage every 0.5 seconds over the duration and are slowed by 40%. Vladimir heals himself for 30% of the pre-mitigation damage dealt, reduced to 18% against minions.

- **Magic Damage Per Tick:** 20 / 33.75 / 47.5 / 61.25 / 75 (+ 3.75% bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 75 (+ 3.75% bonusHealth)
- **Total Magic Damage:** 80 / 135 / 190 / 245 / 300 (+ 15% bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 300 (+ 15% bonusHealth)
- **Mechanics:** slow (40%); heal

Vladimir cannot use basic attacks nor abilities during Sanguine Pool, but he can still move. If Tides of Blood is charging at the time of Sanguine Pool's activation, that ability may still be recast.

Notes:

- The first tick damages immediately so the final one occurs 0.5 seconds before Vladimir becomes targetable again.
- There is an extra damage tick for 0 damage when Vladimir becomes targetable again, triggering the same spell effects as the normal ticks (except those which require damage greater than 0 being dealt).
    - This will trigger turret aggro onto Vladimir if an enemy champion is still within the effect radius when Sanguine Pool ends.
- The slow ends immediately once affected enemies get out of range.
- Vladimir can still use summoner spells and item actives during Sanguine Pool.
- While unable to attack, Vladimir can still input attack commands - causing him to follow his attack target.
    - Vladimir's attack range is reduced to 0 while pooled - causing him to attempt to move right up to his attack target.

### Assumptions

- Crimson Rush starts empty. Ordinary Transfusion generates one point over its cooldown; the empowered cast consumes both points and does not generate another, giving one empowered Q per three casts. This reset policy reconciles the base spell's generation wording with the empowered spell's consume-all wording. Empowered entitlement is captured at declaration. Ordinary Q healing survives spell shields/parries as a separate completion heal; the source explicitly confirms only the untargetability exception.
- Crimson Rush's 0.5-second movement-speed bonus decays linearly. Pool's unpinned exponential decay uses exp(-5*t) over its sourced one-second duration, then zero.
- Tides of Blood damage interpolates linearly through its first second. The missing channel-interaction table is represented by ordinary non-movement channel interrupts; W overlays the charge and a pre-existing E can release while pooled.
- The default policy releases E at one second when the champion is in reach, otherwise at maximum charge. Its charge telegraph reports the maximum 1.5-second release deadline to enemy reactions; the earlier policy recast is visible as its resulting missile.
- The fifteen-bolt nova is represented by its target-facing bolt on the duel axis. Enemy pet interception and lateral bolt coverage are not represented; the enemy champion can receive only one packet.
- Pool affects the sole enemy champion within 350 center units. Its zero-damage ending proc, minion healing, pets and item/summoner actions are outside the modeled duel.
- Hemoplague is blocked by a spell shield at infection, but a shield acquired after infection does not block its burst. Untargetability/parry still guard the burst; an infected living champion grants its delayed heal even if its burst is guarded. A champion already dead at detonation grants no heal.
- Hemoplague uses separate physical/magic amplification, preserving true damage. Its named additive exception with Alistar's Unbreakable Will requires that future kit's explicit implementation; item and monster sources are absent.
- The pinned Hemoplague text does not establish owner-death persistence. A detonation whose clock has not elapsed when Vladimir dies does not prolong this duel; no posthumous burst or heal is granted.
- The budget assumes one empowered Q every three casts, a half-health reference for its additional heal, four Pool contacts, full-charge E with eight-percent max-health self-cost, and one Hemoplague champion; it does not price Pool untargetability or repeated minion healing.
