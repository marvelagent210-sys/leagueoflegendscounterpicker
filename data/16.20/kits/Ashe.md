## Q: Ranger's Focus

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: true · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: While Ranger's Focus is inactive, Ashe's basic attacks on-attack generate a stack of Focus for 4 seconds, refreshing on subsequent attacks and stacking up to 4 times. Stacks expire one by one every second when the duration ends.

- **Mechanics:** on-attack

Active: For 6 seconds, Ashe gains bonus attack speed and empowers her basic attacks to fire a flurry of five arrows that deal modified physical damage. The flurries apply life steal and Frost Shot per arrow, but apply on-hit effects only once.

- **Bonus Attack Speed:** 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60% → at max rank, level 20: 60%
- **Physical Damage Per Arrow:** 22 / 23 / 24 / 25 / 26% AD → at max rank, level 20: 26% of AD
- **Total Damage Per Flurry:** 110 / 115 / 120 / 125 / 130% AD → at max rank, level 20: 130% of AD
- **Mechanics:** life steal; life steal; on-hit

Ranger's Focus resets Ashe's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- The flurries deal 1 instance of basic damage followed by 4 instances of a non-reactive type of damage (either default or proc).
    - The flurries are classified as a basic attack on a script level, but are considered an ability for other effects (e.g. Sap Magic).
- The first flurry of a Ranger's Focus cast fires one additional arrow, dealing 20% increased total damage.
- Ranger's Focus works against structures.
- Flat damage reductions (e.g. Tantrum, Nimble Fighter, or Eclipse) apply per arrow, resulting in extremely increased effectiveness against Ranger's Focus.
    - The sole exception is Warden's Mail Rock Solid, as that applies per cast instance rather than per damage instance.
- A flurry can apply 5 stacks of Black Cleaver Carve to a single target as it deals 5 instances of physical damage.
- Ranger's Focus also empowers Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury bolts to become a flurry of 5 bolts each as well.
    - The empowered bolts use Ashe's attack projectile, including her missile speed (2500 compared to their default 2000).
    - The bolts are evenly distributed among the targets, but a single target cannot be hit by more than 5 bolts.
    - These flurries also apply on-hit effects only once to each target hit.
- Because Focus stacks are generated on-attack, Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury bolts will not generate any.

### Assumptions

- Frost critical slow is averaged by the attack's declaration-time critical chance; its extra strength decays linearly over the documented first second. A new Frost hit refreshes the ordinary slow without weakening a stronger critical slow still decaying.
- Ranger's Focus resolves its five arrows (six on the first flurry) at the same contact tick because the pinned source gives no inter-arrow interval. Each arrow is a separate damage packet; attached on-hit effects and the attack-hit hook occur once. Follow-up packets use the engine's attached-damage classification.
- Enchanted Crystal Arrow's stun is interpolated between the pinned distance bands, using travel from the cast-start position to the enemy's hitbox at contact. The one-dimensional model cannot distinguish transverse contact positions.
- Volley models the aimed arrow in its cone; an enemy receives its damage only once. The source's 75-unit forward missile spawn offset is included in its reach, while travel starts at Ashe's center.
- Hawkshot has its documented charges, recharge, cast time and no mana cost. The duel has no fog of war or brush, and ordinary sight cannot reveal invisibility, so Hawkshot provides no combat effect and the automatic behavior does not cast it.
- Enchanted Crystal Arrow: speed has no value past level 4; its level-4 value is used at 20.
- The stat-budget hint estimates continuous attacks: four ordinary attacks to build Focus, six seconds of flurries, and one extra arrow per activation. Actual fights retain stack expiry, mana, cast decisions, defenses and interruptions.
- Ashe's base critical-damage modifier is normalized to one for the engine's averaged damage calculation: this represents Frost Shot's unconditional, continuous critical-chance bonus rather than ordinary attacks dealing extra damage only when they critically strike.
