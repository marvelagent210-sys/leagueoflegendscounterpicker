## Innate: Whisper

Source: [Template:Data Jhin/Whisper](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Jhin/Whisper?oldid=4075448) (revision 4075448). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate - Death In 4 Acts: Jhin's basic attacks consume ammunition within 4 rounds. He will reload over 2.5 seconds immediately after expending all rounds or withholding leftover rounds after 10 seconds of being out of combat and not being affected by crowd control. The latter reload can be interrupted by declaring an attack or casting an ability. Jhin is unable to declare basic attacks during the reload time.

- **Mechanics:** lockout

Jhin's final round attack has an uncancellable windup, always critically strikes, including against structures, and deals bonus physical damage equal to 15 / 20 / 25 (based on level)% of the target's missing health. The damage based on the target's health is capped at 800 against monsters.

- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; critical strike

Innate - Every Moment Matters: Jhin gains bonus attack damage equal to 4 – 44 (based on level)% (+ 0.35% per 1% critical strike chance) (+ 0.3% per 1% bonus attack speed) AD.

Additionally, critical strikes against enemies grant Jhin 14% (+ 0.44% per 1% bonus attack speed) bonus movement speed for 2 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Notes:

- The missing health damage benefits from life steal.
- The bonus damage based on the target's missing health does not affect structures.
- The 4th attack versus a structure is classified as a critical strike.
- The attack damage multiplier stacks additively with Dragon Slayer's.
- Every Moment Matters' bonus AD will benefit from any amount of bonus attack speed, even in excess of an amount that would usually reach the attacks per second cap (3).
    - The maximum modifier without attack speed is 79%.
      - 44% base modifier at level 18.
      - 35% from critical strike chance on account of the 100% cap.
      - Sources of cripple will not affect Jhin's bonus attack damage from his passive and will ignore his attack speed as it is fixed.
        - However, Realm of Death will reduce Jhin's bonus attack damage.
- Attack damage reductions apply before the multiplier causing them to be stronger against Jhin than advertised or versus other champions.

### Assumptions

- Whisper starts with four ordinary rounds and no free fourth shot. Ammunition is spent when the windup launches, so a canceled windup costs no round. The fourth shot is an uncancellable guaranteed 150% AD critical attack plus 25% of the target's missing health at impact, folded into its single life-stealing physical packet. R shots neither spend nor reload basic ammunition.
- The wiki's AD table explicitly caps at level 18 (44%); the exact 16.20 game calculation continues its +4 percentage points per level beyond 18, giving 52% at this simulator's level 20. The model uses that versioned continuation, with six-decimal rounding of float32 coefficients, plus 35% of critical chance and 30% of all bonus attack speed. The 10-second idle reload and one-second trap arming retain the explicit wiki mechanics despite client constants of eight seconds and 0.75 seconds without corroborating server script.
- Ordinary critical outcomes are averaged by the engine: Jhin's first three rounds use a probability-weighted missile speed from 2600 to 3000, and the two-second critical movement buff is multiplied by their declaration-time crit chance. The fourth uses 3000 and the full movement buff. Fixed basic attack speed includes growth only and ignores cripples, while all external bonus attack speed still increases AD and movement speed.
- Dancing Grenade begins on the enemy champion, then bounces at most three times to the closest unvisited enemy champion or eligible non-ward pet within 450 units, with stable identity ties. A previously struck target's death before the next contact adds 35% of the original packet; it cannot hit the same target twice. Spell shields do not stop bouncing. There is no minion wave outside the actual summoned pets.
- Deadly Flourish retains its start position but aims toward the enemy at completion. It is an instantaneous line intercepted by projectile walls and blockers despite having no travel speed. Enemy pets before its first champion collision take the pinned 25% minion reduction as area damage; a mark acquired from that same W hit cannot retroactively root. The source's estimated rear 20-unit extension is excluded from aimed forward duels.
- Lotus Traps are placed after their cast and stop at an intervening projectile blocker. No validated throw speed is pinned, so placement has no additional invented flight. Their three-hit ward body is targetable for the first second and then untargetable; its unpinned body radius is represented by a point. Enemy champions or eligible enemy pets can bloom a trap. The slow is refreshed while actually inside the fixed two-second bloom, with normal CC immunity; each shield-guarded explosion is independent. Trap and Curtain Call reveals grant ordinary sight, so they never expose invisibility in the no-fog duel.
- Curtain Call's one-second activation opens a ten-second stationary channel, with four separately cast 0.25-second shots and one-second intervals unaffected by haste. Its original facing fixes the cone on the duel axis. Shots pierce enemy pets, stop on the first champion even through a spell shield, evaluate missing health at contact and double the fourth packet. Versioned recast flags say its windup is uncancellable and does not break the parent channel; an announced shot therefore completes when ordinary CC interrupts the retained channel, an interpretation without server-script confirmation.
- The budget values sustained basic rounds plus reload time, one full-health trap explosion per recharge, and all four base Curtain Call packets over the parent cooldown plus actual activation/recast duration. Manual shots are excluded from independent per-second ability estimates; conditional grenade kills and R missing-health escalation are not guaranteed. The combat policy establishes marks with Q/basic damage, prefers marked W, and channels R outside basic attack reach in fight/kite modes.
