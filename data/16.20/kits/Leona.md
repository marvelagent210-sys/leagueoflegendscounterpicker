Active: Leona illuminates her shield, empowering her next basic attack within 6 seconds to have an uncancellable windup, gain 50 bonus range, deal bonus magic damage and stun the target for 1 second.

- **Bonus Magic Damage:** 10 / 35 / 60 / 85 / 110 (+ 30% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 110 (+ 30% AP)
- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; stun (1 s)

Shield of Daybreak resets Leona's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- The enhanced attack will apply other on-hit effects and can critically strike as normal.
    - Critical strikes do not interact with Shield of Daybreak's bonus damage.
- : Shield of Daybreak's interactions with dodging, blocking.
    - Shield of Daybreak's attack can miss, nullifying its effects entirely.
- Shield of Daybreak's attack does not put Leona's basic attack on cooldown.
    - This effectively performs a second attack reset.
    - This is unlike other empowered attacks (including those which, similar to Shield of Daybreak, use a more unconventional spell cast rather than an attack override).
    - Leona will wait out Shield of Daybreak's wind-down before automatically attacking, anyway. The player must issue the next attack themselves to gain the full benefit of the second reset.
    - Shield of Daybreak shares this feature with Thundering Smash and Emberstrike.
- The empowered attack can trigger against turrets exclusively.

Targeting: Direction · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: true · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Active: Leona projects her sword in the target direction that deals magic damage to enemies hit.

- **Magic Damage:** 50 / 90 / 130 / 170 / 210 (+ 40% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 210 (+ 40% AP)

After reaching its maximum range, if the sword hit an enemy champion during its flight, Leona dashes to 225 units beyond the location of the last champion struck and roots them for 0.5 seconds.

- **Mechanics:** dash; root (0.5 s)

Leona is unable to move or attack while Zenith Blade is in flight. She can cast any of her abilities during the dash.

- **Mechanics:** lockout

Notes:

- Leona will fail to dash to and root the last champion struck if they are not within 3000 units of her by the time the sword missile fizzles from reaching maximum range.
- Leona will not track the target if they change locations during her dash.
    - She will only dash to their current location at the time the sword fizzled.
- The root application on the last champion struck is applied at an inconsistent timing.
- Leona will be ordered to basic attack the target she dashed to, after her dash completes.
- Spell shield will block the damage, and prevent Leona from dashing as the last champion struck by her sword.
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.

### Assumptions

- Sunlight is applied and refreshed by Leona's damaging abilities, but never consumed in a solo duel: Leona cannot trigger her own allied-champion passive.
- Eclipse continuously recalculates its resistance bonuses from the current bonus resistances of other sources, excluding its own granted resistance buff to avoid self-scaling feedback.
- Zenith Blade roots when the sword reaches maximum range, since the pinned source says the root timing is inconsistent. Its dash uses the documented 2000-unit missile speed as an estimate; the source gives no dash speed. The landing point is fixed 225 units beyond the target's position at sword expiry.
- Solar Flare aims at the enemy's location within 1200 units, with a 325-unit outer radius and the documented 175-unit epicenter. Epicenter membership is determined by the target's center on the one-dimensional line.
