Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: True · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Upon taking fatal damage, Kog'Maw enters a zombie state for 4 seconds, becoming ghosted and gaining 10% bonus movement speed that increases up to 50% over the duration. At the end of the duration, he explodes to deal 140 – 650 (based on level) true damage to nearby enemies.

- **Mechanics:** revive (4 s); ghosted (4 s)

While under this state, Kog'Maw becomes invulnerable, untargetable, and immune to crowd control, but is also rendered unable to declare basic attacks, cast abilities, use summoner spells, and activate items.

- **Mechanics:** invulnerable; untargetable; cc-immune

Notes:

- Icathian Surprise's bonus movement speed increases by 2.5% every 0.264 over the duration, up to the maximum of 50%. However, as a result of the ticks being rounded up from 0.25 seconds, the movement speed only reaches up to 45%.
- Icathian Surprise's untargetability does not destroy in-flight projectiles.
- Icathian Surprise will still trigger if Kog'Maw is killed by the Nexus Obelisk.
- If Kog'Maw has the missile from a basic attack in-flight at the same time that he's moving while he dies, as long as the missile hits the target after Icathian Surprise has started, Kog'Maw will be able to use a basic attack once during Icathian Surprise.
- Kog'Maw's usable summoner spells will be placed on a 5 second cooldown upon becoming a zombie.
- During Icathian Surprise, Kog'Maw's attack range is reduced to 0 , making him automatically path near enemies he is trying to target.
