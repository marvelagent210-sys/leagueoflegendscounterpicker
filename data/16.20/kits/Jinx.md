## Innate: Get Excited!

Source: [Template:Data Jinx/Get Excited!](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Jinx/Get_Excited!?oldid=3973632) (revision 3973632). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Whenever Jinx scores a takedown against an enemy champion, epic monster, turret, or inhibitor within 3 seconds of damaging them, she gains 175% bonus movement speed decaying over 6 seconds.

Additionally, she is allowed to exceed the attack speed cap and gains a stack of Get Excited! for the same duration, stacking up to 5 times. Only takedowns against enemy champions can grant stacks beyond the first.

Get Excited!: For each stack, Jinx gains 25% total attack speed, up to a maximum of 125%.

Notes:

- Jinx's attack speed cap is increased to 90.0 for the duration, however this value is already beyond the technical limit for attack speed.
- Jinx will still trigger Get Excited from killing a summoned Rift Herald.
- Jinx will fail to trigger Get Excited in the specific circumstance of killing Baron Nashor while more than ~2500 distance away.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Toggle: Jinx switches between Pow-Pow, her minigun, and Fishbones, her rocket launcher.

Pow-Pow: Basic attacks with Pow-Pow generate a stack of Rev'd up for 2.5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent attacks with Pow-Pow and stacking up to 3 times. Each stack of Rev'd up grants bonus attack speed, with all stacks beyond the first one being 50% effective. Stacks expire one by one when the duration ends.

- **Bonus Attack Speed:** 15 / 27.5 / 40 / 52.5 / 65% → at max rank, level 20: 65%
- **Attack Speed per Subsequent Stack:** 7.5 / 13.75 / 20 / 26.25 / 32.5% → at max rank, level 20: 32.5%
- **Maximum Attack Speed:** 30 / 55 / 80 / 105 / 130% → at max rank, level 20: 130%

Fishbones: Basic attacks with Fishbones cost mana on-attack to deal 110% AD modified physical damage to the target and nearby enemies. The damage is affected by critical strike modifiers. While Fishbones is equipped, Jinx gains bonus range but loses 10% of her bonus attack speed.

- **Bonus Range:** 100 / 125 / 150 / 175 / 200 → at max rank, level 20: 200
- **Mechanics:** on-attack; critical strike

Only the first attack after switching to Fishbones will benefit from Rev'd up.

Notes:

- Toggle abilities do not count as ability activations for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- Despite Jinx starting the game using Pow-Pow she doesn't receive any bonus attack speed until Switcheroo! has been learned.
- Fishbones splash damage affects structures (minions will target Jinx) but does not affect targets if Jinx's attacks are blocked, dodged, or missed.
- Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury bolts are special-cased to generate one Pow-Pow stack per enemy hit.
- Each of Runaan's Hurricane additional bolts apply Fishbones' splash damage, which stacks with each other.
- Both weapons deal basic damage to their primary target (this includes the increased damage of Fishbones), which applies life steal based on the damage dealt (post-mitigation) as usual.
- The splash damage of Fishbones is based on the pre-mitigation damage done to the primary target (which includes critical strikes). It deals proc damage and thus doesn't apply life steal or on-hit effects.
    - The splash damage of Fishbones will not account for damage modifiers applied to the primary target (e.g. Hexoptics C44 Magnification).
- Fishbones damage calculations:
    - Basic attack damage: 110% AD
    - Critical strike damage: 220% AD
    - Critical strike damage with Infinity Edge: 253% AD
    - Runaan's Hurricane bolt damage: 71.5% AD
    - Bolt critical strike damage: 143% AD
    - Bolt critical strike damage with Infinity Edge: 164.45% AD
    - Maximum potential damage with Runaan's Hurricane: 253% AD
    - Maximum potential critical strike damage with Runaan's Hurricane: 506% AD
    - Maximum potential critical strike damage with Runaan's Hurricane and Infinity Edge: 581.9% AD

### Assumptions

- Weapon policy: 0 switches for range, 1 prefers minigun, 2 prefers rockets: 0.
- Assumed seconds between later Rev'd up stack expiries: 1.
- Jinx starts with Pow-Pow, no Rev'd up stacks and no Get Excited! stacks. Weapon policy switches to Fishbones only outside minigun range by default; explicit policies can prefer either weapon. Rocket mana is paid on attack declaration, even if the windup is cancelled; insufficient mana returns to Pow-Pow before the next attack. Toggle declarations retain their sourced static 0.9-second cooldown and are not spell activations for other kits.
- Pow-Pow gains Rev'd up on launched attacks, including blinded launches, with a shared sourced 2.5-second timer. The pinned text says stacks expire one by one but does not give later intervals; revDecayInterval exposes the assumed one-second interval. Fishbones retains stacks for its first declared attack and consumes them when that attack launches. The 10% penalty applies to bonus attack speed, before total-attack-speed multipliers. Fishbones windup uses the pinned 0.27/1.6 cast/total fraction, relative to the minigun's compiled fraction.
- Get Excited! is earned only from counted enemy champion deaths in this two-champion arena; ordinary summons, natural expiry and resurrection eggs do not qualify. Epic monsters, structures and summoned Rift Herald are absent. Its 175% movement burst uses an explicit linear six-second decay because the pinned text gives no decay curve. The 25% total attack-speed stacks are additive with one another, refresh together and raise the cap to the sourced 90.
- Zap!'s sourced cast-time table is linearly interpolated by current bonus attack speed and clamps at 0.6/0.4 seconds. Its reveal is ordinary sight, recorded as a timed mark without exposing invisibility; map fog is absent.
- The three Chompers are projected as one central 115-radius trigger and one 225-radius explosion per cast, respecting the sourced one-chomper-per-champion limit. Their transverse arrangement is absent, so side-chomper placement and separate pet explosions are not fabricated. The toss takes 0.4 seconds, is stopped by projectile walls, then arms after 0.5 seconds and expires five seconds after landing. A champion trigger explodes immediately, independently checks shielded snare/knockdown, and consumes the whole represented cluster. Natural expiry causes damage without snare. Summons receive explosion damage but never trigger champion-only traps. Sight does not grant true sight.
- Super Mega Death Rocket uses the pinned finite 25000-unit global range, moves at 1700 then switches to 2200 after 1350 traveled units, and linearly interpolates the sourced distance damage table up to 1500. Only its flat/bonus-AD component ramps; each actual target supplies its own missing health at impact. It collides only with champions; shielded primary contacts still explode onto nearby summons for 80%. No epic-monster missing-health cap is applied to ordinary minion summons. Its launch position and direction are fixed at cast declaration; map sight and lateral geometry are absent.
- The stat budget values fully stacked minigun attack speed, its sourced normal cap and one actual Chomper explosion per E cooldown, without preloading combat stacks. Death Rocket uses its actual distance callback in the reference state (zero distance), without an invented maximum-range hit. It does not pre-award champion takedown buffs or assume collateral targets. Actual weapon choice, mana, stack timing and destruction remain combat outcomes.
