## Innate: Martial Poise

Targeting: Direction · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Innate: When Kalista inputs a movement command during her basic attack windup or shortly after it completes, or while in cast time of Pierce, she will dash in that direction. Kalista cannot dash while immobilized or grounded, and she will be knocked down by any immobilizing or polymorphing crowd control during the dash.

- **Mechanics:** dash

Martial Poises dash range and speed details: * Dash range of basic attacks is modified by the tier of Kalista's Boots and the angle of the dash. ** At a 0° angle towards the target, the minimum dash range is 140 / 153 / 166 / 175 (based on Boots Tier) units. ** At a 90° angle, the maximum dash range is 240 / 263 / 280 / 300 (based on Boots Tier) units. * Dash range of Pierce is 300 units when dashing anywhere in the direction of the point of cast. ** If the dash is inputted to a location that is away from Pierce point of cast, the dash range is reduced based on the angle, with a minimum distance of 150 units if the inputted location is directly opposite. * Dash speed is increased based on the tier of Kalista's Boots, and is affected by her bonus attack speed as well as movement speed modifiers she is under the effect of (including slows).

- **Mechanics:** slow

Innate - Oathsworn Bond: Kalista begins the game with an exclusive Black Spear that she can activate on an allied champion to deem them Oathsworn, forming a tether together. While she and the ally bound as the Oathsworn are within tether range of each other, they can both interact with Soul-Mark and Fate's Call.

- **Mechanics:** tether

Kalista can purchase the Black Spear from the shop at no cost to obtain it again and, before 3 minutes of game time, may activate the item again on a different target to designate a new Oathsworn. See Black Spear for more details.

- **Maximum Target Range:** 240 / 263 / 280 / 300 (based on Boots Tier)

- **Dash Speed:** 1025 / 1100 / 1160 (based on Boots Tier)

Notes:

- Basic attacks and Pierce cannot be cast while dashing, unless animation cancelled with a movement command.
- Kalista must wait for her auto attack windup or cast animation to finish, before she dashes. However, Kalista will dash immediately if the movement command to dash is input after her auto attack windup finishes.
- Martial Poise also "remembers" your auto attack target after dashing, letting you dash repeatedly by only clicking the ground.
- Martial Poise cannot keep up with attack speed values greater than 2.1, due to the combination of her auto attack windup taking too long, and her dashes being too slow. Movement speed bonuses make it less of an issue.
    - Sufficiently high attack speeds can also cause Martial Poise to interpret movement inputs as dash inputs, during the dash of a previous auto attack (such as jumping towards attack-move inputs).
- Move commands inputted during an attack windup or the cast time of Pierce while Kalista is immobilized or grounded will fail to cast and thus prevent the dash from triggering.
- If multiple move commands are inputted during the attack windup, the most recent one is used for the dash's targeting.
    - If the most recent input is not a move command, the dash will not trigger.
      - In this case, the buffer for the previous move command was cancelled by a new non-move command such as an input for an attack or ability cast.
    - The move command will still be buffered to the end of the immobilization or the end of the attack windup or cast time of Pierce while grounded if it has not been overridden by other inputs.
- Kalista's facing direction will change immediately on move command even among multiple inputs, but will not visually update until the dash.
- The facing direction when dashing is considered to be in the direction of the dash, not her apparent facing direction based on her model's animation state.
- Dash distance decreases as the dash direction becomes closer to the minimum direction. This defines a forward distance for basic attacks and a backwards distance for Pierce.
- Empirical testing has given the following values below.
    - Basic attack dash range given the angle θ:
      - \begin{cases}(175 + (125 \times \sin(\theta))) \times T \end{cases}
        - Where T = 0.800, 0.875, 0.950, 1.000 depending on the tier of Boots (T0, T1, T2, T3)
      - Minimum dash range of 225 units when dashing backwards
    - Pierce's dash range given the angle θ:
      - \begin{cases} 150 + (150 \times \sin(\theta)) & \mathrm{Backwards} \\ 300 & \mathrm{Otherwise} \end{cases}
- There is a very brief grace period after completing a basic attack windup wherein Kalista can still input a movement command to trigger the dash.
- The dash speed is unaffected by additive movement speed modifiers.
- There are a total of 24 different dash ranges based on direction with Boots and Pierce being cast.
- Kalista will automatically be issued an attack command on her attack target at the end of the dash, as long as they remain in her attack range by then.
- The attack move (default A + MB1) click feature checks for targets in brief intervals only if Kalista is not dashing.
- Sleep does not count for knocking the dash down.

Active: Kalista invokes her tether to retrieve and hold her Oathsworn for 4 seconds. Kalista cleanses the Oathsworn from all crowd control and renders them invulnerable and untargetable for the duration, and pulls them to her over 1 second.

- **Mechanics:** cleanse (1 s); dash

While held, the Oathsworn is vanished and may select a target location to dash to with displacement immunity, ending Fate's Call's invocation and reappearing at the location. If the invocation ends without the Oathsworn selecting a target location, they will automatically do so at maximum range from Kalista's facing direction.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable; dash; displacement-immune

The Oathsworn's dash stops upon colliding with an enemy champion. Upon the dash ending by collision or arriving to the targeted location, the Oathsworn knocks back all nearby enemies and keeps them airborne for a set duration, while simultaneously landing to their base attack range from the closest target hit.

- **Airborne Duration:** 1 / 1.5 / 2 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 2 s
- **Mechanics:** airborne (2 s); airborne (2 s); dash

The Oathsworn must be within tether range to cast this ability, and is also silenced and unable to perform movement or attack commands while Fate's Call is in effect.

- **Mechanics:** silence

Notes:

- The Oathsworn's landing location of the lunge is precisely to their base attack range from the closest target they hit and relative to where this target would be displaced to from the knock back.
    - In other words, the Oathsworn will land to a location where they are within their exact base attack range of the target by the end of the target's displacement.
    - The calculated landing spot does not evaluate if the target was successfully displaced or not, meaning that the Oathsworn will end up closer than their base attack range if the closest target hit was not knocked back.
- The airborne effect is attributed to Fate's Call's initial caster.
- If the Oathsworn does not hit any targets with the knock up effect, the dash will simply end and they will reappear at the target location.
- Kalista is briefly unable to start basic attacks or cast basic abilities when the Oathsworn starts their flight.
- The following will defer Fate's Call's effects at the time of cast to the end of their duration if the Oathsworn is:
    - In a cast time.
    - Channeling.
      - Fate's Call will not defer by the following channels: Recall, Defiant Dance, Realm Warp, and The Culling.
      - Fate's Call cannot be cast during the following channels: Teleport, Gate, Grand Starfall, Hero's Entrance and Stand United.
    - Using an ability that preloads UnstoppableForceMarker.
      - If the Oathsworn is still occupied after 6 seconds of being deferred from this time, Fate's Call will cancel.
- Fate's Call will not pull the Oathsworn if they are dashing but they will be retrieved after 1 second regardless.
- Fate's Call can be cast even if the Oathsworn is untargetable.
- Fate's Call is disabled if the Oathsworn is resurrecting or is being affected by another Fate's Call.
- Fate's Call can be used even if the Oathsworn is in a zombie state.
- Fate's Call does not render the target vanished while being held if they are creating E aura.
- The Oathsworn being held is represented by a light over Kalista's head.
- Kalista's death does not cancel Fate's Call.

### Assumptions

- Use barefeet Martial Poise hops (0 disables the approximate dash policy): 1.
- The current pinned basic attack deals ordinary 100% AD physical damage and ordinary critical strikes; historical 90% AD and sight-loss penalties are absent from the current source. Its 35.9999998659% base windup uses the pinned 0.75 modifier, is uncancellable by movement, and may be cancelled by Rend. Automatic retargeting during an already-declared windup is not optimized.
- Martial Poise defaults to bare-feet forward 140/backward 225-unit attack hops and forward 300/backward 150-unit Pierce hops on the duel's axis. The normal policy buffers a retreat while kiting/fleeing or while a backward hop retains attack range, otherwise an advance. It uses the sourced nominal 1025 speed, scaled by the engine's multiplicative movement-speed/slows while excluding additive movement-speed buffs. The complete attack-speed velocity formula is not present in the pinned wiki/client evidence; positive AS velocity scaling remains unresolved and is deliberately omitted. This can understate high-AS Kalista kiting. Set martialPoise=0 to inspect stationary combat. No unproved full-speed formula or boot item is invented. Terrain uses the shared clear-endpoint jump rules, and ordinary immobilization/grounding prevents launch; the source's sleep knockdown bug is not specially emulated.
- Rend lives on each actual champion/summon, starts empty, refreshes 4 seconds, and caps 254 stacks. Accepted basics and unshielded Pierce apply stacks; blind/dodge/block/parry do not. E consumes all eligible in-range marks and resolves immediately, spending mana and starting cooldown before its 0.25-second cast lock. Its reference castTime remains 0.25 while the instantaneous delivery uses castTimeFor=0 plus a local lock. Existing Pierce casts and hops retain their absolute clocks. Untargetable/stasis targets keep their marks and cannot supply an activation target. The pinned page gives 1100 effect radius but only says cast range is lower; this model openly uses 1100 for activation because the smaller exact distance is unavailable.
- Pierce flies at 2400, collides with the first real spell target, and continues only if that contacted target dies. It carries only the last killed target's pre-contact Rend stacks, following the pinned multi-kill bug rather than accumulating all corpses. An unshielded surviving target receives its carried stacks plus its own new spear. E empowers already-flying basic/Pierce missiles to rend immediately on impact; an E during an unfinished Q also empowers its future missile and models the documented additional first-spear repeat on the previously rended target at impact. These finite launched packets can finish after Kalista's death. Monster-only 50% damage reduction is absent because the duel has no epic jungle monsters.
- Rend kill resets and 30 mana refunds require an actual counted E kill; resurrection-protected targets grant none. A targetable Sion already in Glory in Death grants the documented reset even when it survives. The offensive policy keeps accumulating spears while basic attacks remain in range and rends when leaving attack reach or when its damage after resistance, reductions and shields can finish the target; it is an explicit policy rather than an exhaustive timing search. The budget amortizes a sustained E cycle containing ordinary attacks and one Q application per Q cycle, without pre-awarding kill refunds.
- There is no Oathsworn allied champion in the duel. Soul-Mark contributes no fabricated 18%-max-health magic damage, and Fate's Call cannot be cast to grant Kalista her ally's invulnerability, retrieval or two-second knockup. W can be cast manually as a two-charge, 50-second-recharge scout with 0.5-second cast and 30-second between-cast cooldown; the damage policy does not waste casts on a scout when ordinary global duel visibility already suffices.
- A Sentinel is a two-basic-hit, non-attacking, CC-immune pet invalid to abilities. It patrols the selected 1400–5000-unit line for three round trips at 280 speed, half at one health, detects visible champions within 450, then chases/reports ordinary sight for 4 seconds and disappears. Its raw 65 radius is an upstream assumed default. The two-dimensional forward cone, obstacle routing, and unquantified rapid decay of its initial +200 speed are not asserted. The shared pet lifecycle removes scouts when their owner dies.
