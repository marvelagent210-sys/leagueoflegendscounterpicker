### Assumptions

- Sap Magic starts ready with zero stored stacks. The source's level-20 series gives 14.1% maximum-health healing and an 18.8235294117647-second static cooldown. Readiness is captured at basic-attack declaration; its sourced 0.25-second delay is its attack-speed-scaled windup, and a connected blocked attack can heal. Wards, structures and unclassified pet targets do not qualify. Large versus epic monsters cannot be distinguished by the arena's monster class, so large-monster refunds and epic-monster Sapling refunds are omitted rather than assigned to every monster.
- Bramble Smash deals one damage packet per body across its nearby circle and traveling shockwave. Its nearby control applies in every direction, independently of projectile interception. The source specifies up to 300 knockback by proximity without a curve: the model linearly reduces 300 to zero over the sourced 325 center-radius. Champion and actual-pet pushes travel independently at the binary's 950 speed. Primary 600 range and 300 knockback override binary 650/400; a separate 0.075-second action lock follows the primary 0.3 cast.
- Twisted Advance tracks its actual target at 1300 speed while untargetable, updating its endpoint each tick. The primary instant cast overrides the binary's 0.25 cast. A relocation over the source's estimated 2000 limit releases the chase toward the previous position without damage/root. Knockdown, displacement, death and resurrection release its untargetability; an untargetable arrival receives no packet.
- Sapling Toss begins without pre-duel saplings. A real finite projectile flies for the exact binary 0.8500000238418579 seconds, then creates an untargetable invulnerable minion pet, with 0.5-second GrowthDuration arming. The arena brush flag describes actual brush availability at landing, not Maokai's buffs or starting position; when true every legal placement in this abstract lane is in brush. The arena has no individual bush coordinates. Saplings use a point body because no pet collision radius or HP is provided; HP 1 is an invulnerable bookkeeping value. Same-point placements shift by one detection radius toward the owner, a disclosed spacing rule because the source gives no exact offset. The normal owner-death pet cleanup remains; these sources do not grant a persistence exception.
- Primary E detection radius 250 and empowered duration 1.5 seconds override binary SaplingDetectionRadius 550 and EmpoweredDoTDuration 2. The full raw primary explicitly uses 50*2/3 and corresponding ratios for each empowered packet; the pet article's 'double damage over 1.5 seconds' specifies the same total without its three-packet distribution. The parsed 66.7% formula is retained unchanged: one impact and two attached packets on non-minions, normal impact only on minions. Per-body attached slots cap at two and refresh their common expiry without replacing pending ticks; the next missing slot is refilled. The 300/600 non-champion caps apply before area resistance. Existing attached packets persist through untargetability but ordinary death/resurrection clears them. Burn packets retain the explosion's owner-stat snapshot.
- Nature's Grasp represents the central one of five lateral brambles, with its four sourced missiles spaced 100 apart and accelerating continuously from 100 at 300 units/s² to 750. Four lateral brambles are omitted: source-bound lateral offsets are unavailable, so they are not superimposed into five damage hits. The central branch hits each actual body once; its first actual champion-class contact destroys its remaining missiles even when a spell shield or parry blocks effects. Projectile destruction removes only the intercepted missile, retaining the sourced invisible-trailer counterplay. Root duration uses that missile's actual traveled distance, interpolated on the pinned distance series. Successful champion contact grants a linearly decaying two-second movement buff; already-released missiles continue after owner death, while cast interruption prevents unreleased packets.
