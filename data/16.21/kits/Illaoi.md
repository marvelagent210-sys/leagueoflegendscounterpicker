## Innate: Prophet of an Elder God

Source: [Template:Data Illaoi/Prophet of an Elder God](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Illaoi/Prophet_of_an_Elder_God?oldid=4070338) (revision 4070338). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: false · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Periodically, Illaoi spawns a Tentacle on the nearest terrain, if no other Tentacle is around it. Tentacles fully spawn after a 2 second delay and are untargetable in the meantime.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable

Tentacles are commanded to attack by Illaoi's abilities, dealing 9 – 180 (based on level) (+ 110% AD) (+ 40% AP) physical damage to all enemies struck, increased by 0 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 (based on Tentacle Smash's rank)%. Each enemy individually reduces the slam damage by 50% for every slam they were hit by in the last 0.66 seconds, capped at 75% less damage.

Each Tentacle also heals Illaoi for 5% of her missing health if it hits at least one enemy champion.

- **Mechanics:** heal

See Pets for more details about Tentacles.

Notes:

- Illaoi cannot spawn Tentacles on ally invulnerable structures [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8r_7zpD3tY] , in addition to neither her nor the Vessel being able to spawn them in Nexus Obelisk's attack range.
    - When the inhibitor is down, Nexus turrets will only have a single hardcoded valid space on each of their external sides to spawn Tentacles on. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xJ4Jwm7jhZ8]
- Tentacles are prevented from spawning on base gates.
- If multiple Tentacles hit simultaneously, the received healing is calculated iteratively based on the new amount of missing health, as per this formula: (maximum health−current health) × (1−0.95n), with 'n' the number of Tentacle hits.
    - Because the healing is calculated iteratively, Grievous Wounds ends up slightly less effective than its nominal value, since the reduced healing leaves more missing health on each subsequent Tentacle hit.
- The healing applies after omnivamp.

## W: Harsh Lesson

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: special · Usable while knocked down: special

Active: Illaoi empowers her next basic attack within 6 seconds to have an uncancellable windup, gain 225 bonus range and cause her to leap to the target's location if they are beyond her normal basic attack range.

- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; dash

The attack deals additional physical damage, with a minimum threshold, and capped at 300 against non-champions, and commands all Tentacles in range to attack the target. The minimum additional damage can also be applied against turrets.

- **Additional Physical Damage:** 3 / 3.5 / 4 / 4.5 / 5% (+ 3.5% per 100 AD) of target's maximum health → at max rank, level 20: 5% of targetMaxHealth (+ 3.5% per100:AD)
- **Minimum Physical Damage:** 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 → at max rank, level 20: 60

Harsh Lesson resets Illaoi's basic attack timer. Harsh Lesson can be activated during Illaoi's other abilities. If the leap is interrupted, the ability is cancelled and put on cooldown.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- The additional damage doesn't apply life steal but it does apply spell vamp and Omnivamp.
- Harsh Lesson cannot be cast while pulling a Spirit.
- Harsh Lesson is made of two different abilities which are decided by Illaoi's range to the target, being either melee or within dash range, causing interactions to act differently.
- The melee attack will still hit if the target becomes untargetable [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUlojKtZGF8], while the dash attack will not. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JOcA7oSdlqM]
- Abilities cannot be buffered during the melee windup but they can be buffered during the dash windup.
- The Tentacle attacking because of Harsh Lesson is considered to be of the same cast instance as the basic attack, and for example will not grant additional stacks of Conqueror and Electrocute.

## E: Test of Spirit

Targeting: Location · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: Physical, Magic, True, (Echoed damage) · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: special · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Illaoi launches a tendril in the target direction that stops at the first enemy hit, pulling their Spirit out in front of Illaoi over 0.75 seconds if they are a champion. The target is tethered to the Spirit for 7 seconds, revealing them in addition to granting sight of the area around them while the tether remains.

- **Mechanics:** tether (7 s); true sight; reveal

The Spirit spawns with the target's current health, armor and magic resist, and echoes a portion of the pre-mitigation damage received to the tethered champion, with the last hits before it dies potentially echoing reduced damage.

- **Damage Transmission:** 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 / 45% (+ 8% per 100 AD) → at max rank, level 20: 45% (+ 8% per100:AD)

When the Spirit is killed or the target leaves tether range, the tether is severed, marking the target as a Vessel for 4 seconds and slowing them by 80% for 1.5 seconds once the tether returns back to them. Each Tentacle autonomously attacks the closest Vessel or Spirit, prioritizing the latter, once every 4.5 / 4 / 3.5 (based on level) seconds.

- **Mechanics:** slow (1.5 s, 80%)

Enemies made a Vessel continuously spawn a Tentacle near themselves, when possible. Tentacles can spawn 300 units closer to each other than when spawned by Illaoi.

Illaoi is unable to act while firing the projectile and while the target's Spirit is being pulled. She also reveals herself while firing the projectile if there is an enemy champion nearby. Vessels can dispel their mark by scoring a takedown against Illaoi. Test of Spirit cannot be cast again until the Spirit dies.

- **Mechanics:** lockout; reveal

Notes:

- The self-reveal has not been fully tested for complete confirmation. Is the ability invisible when passing nearby minions and there is no enemy champion nearby? Is the tip of the ability enough to reveal her? Whether it can collide with a minion or not? Just if she's around the exit of the fog of war, and even if she points towards the inside of the fog of war? [https://youtu.be/Fq2KuW55oVs]
- This ability will cast from wherever the caster is at the end of the cast time.
    - And will fire towards the same pointed location.
- The tendril is destroyed if Illaoi dies before it hits a target. However, if it has already hit a target, then the Spirit pull will continue even if Illaoi has already died.
- The tendril can be blocked by projectile-destroying effects (e.g. W) and spell shields.
    - It will only be destroyed and not replicated against Rebuttal.
- Sion Spirit cannot be spawned during Glory in Death. Spirits created before Sion's zombie state will remain active.
- Spirits are classified as clones:
    - The Spirit spawns with the tethered champion's default model and size. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWkLcM6Xs9I]
    - Spirits can only be fully targeted by Illaoi's team and neutral monsters. The Spirit's allies cannot manually select it as a valid target for spells and abilities, but they can still do so with automatically targeted effects.
    - Effects, like runes, may consider the Spirit as a champion for their stat-tracking.
    - Spirits cannot be healed nor shielded (Summon Aery excluded).
    - Like other clones, killing the Spirit awards 25, but unlike other clones it doesn't award Experience.
- Post V12.8, does grabbing the Spirit of an Oathsworn make the Spirit invisible? [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cApbCTz_lIQ]
- The Spirit is immune to stasis (i.e. Tempered Fate).
    - Illaoi needs 937.5 / 875 / 812.5 / 750 / 687.5 total AD to reach 100% echoed damage. As per above, the damage is still dependent on the Spirit's and Vessel's damage modifiers and may not always be able to deal 100% of the target's current health as total echoed damage.
- Transferred damage will apply on-damage effects, such as damage amplifiers (e.g Last Stand), Black Cleaver, Morellonomicon, Executioner's Calling, Elixir of Sorcery, Sudden Impact, Shadowflame, The Collector, First Strike, Aspect of the Dragon burn damage, and The Darkin Scythe.
    - Echoed damage always counts as single target for the purpose of Omnivamp.
    - Echoed damage always counts as proc damage and thus will not doubly apply on-hit or on-spell effects versus the target (e.g. Liandry's Torment). These effects are only applied versus the Spirit.
    - Some effects like Sudden Impact are consumed by the target first, before the Spirit. Other effects like Hextech Alternator are consumed by the Spirit first, before the target.
    - Attacking the Vessel and its Spirit simultaneously will only apply one stack of Black Cleaver to the Vessel.
- When the tether is broken (whether the Spirit's lifetime ran out or the target moved out of range), the Spirit will vanish and move to Illaoi's location.
    - An uncancellable windup attack may still kill the Spirit after it vanished to grant the gold, but without making the target a Vessel and without echoing damage. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ph8_kGqAlOQ&t=670s]
- If the target that had its Spirit pulled from them is taken into the Realm of Death, the tether will break, but they will not be made into a Vessel.
- Riposte can be used to prevent becoming a Vessel if used before the Spirit is killed [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2jcsdMpvZRc], or while leaving the tether's range. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yI121hbf3jA]
- Spell shields can also prevent the slow upon becoming a Vessel. Spell shields will not prevent becoming a Vessel or Tentacles from attacking.
- The application of the slowing missile cannot be blocked by projectile- destroying effects (e.g. W).
    - Effects that destroy homing missiles (i.e certain untargetabilities) will prevent the application of the slowing missile.
    - The slowing missile will not be fired if the target is already untargetable when the Spirit dies.
- The Vessel status effect is a debuff that doesn't persist through death.
- If the Vessel debuff expires on the same server tick as the Tentacle becomes ready to slam, whether the slam occurs depends on which of the two spawned first. This is expected to be the Vessel, so no slam occurs.
    - The slam will occur on clones created after the Tentacle already spawned.

Notes:

    - 2+ champions: one Tentacle at 60 degrees.
    - 3+ champions: one Tentacle at 300 degrees.
    - 4+ champions: one Tentacle at 120 degrees.
    - 5+ champions: one Tentacle at 240 degrees.
    - 6+ champions: one Tentacle at 180 degrees.

### Assumptions

- A Tentacle is 65 units in radius: the wiki gives none, and the game files give it no gameplay radius, which leaves the game's default (the wiki's 65 for Gangplank's Powder Keg, which has none in the game files either).
- Tentacles rise where Illaoi stands: the terrain at the lane's side is next to her.
- With the lane set up, one awake Tentacle stands beside Illaoi when the fight starts, and the innate's timer is ready; without it, the timer starts its cooldown.
- A commanded slam lands 0.75 s after it starts, as Tentacle Smash's does (the wiki gives no time for the others), and a Tentacle's queued slams follow one another without a pause outside Leap of Faith.
- The enemy can step out of a slam sideways, as from any skillshot. On the line, a slam always hits a Spirit standing in its stretch, and one aimed at the Spirit can catch the champion beyond it; a slam that hits a Spirit heals Illaoi as if it hit a champion (the wiki counts Spirits as clones).
- Harsh Lesson's leap takes Illaoi to the target as the attack starts (the game files' leap, at her movement speed plus 600, covers its 225 units in under 0.25 s).
- Tentacles slam a Spirit or a Vessel within 950 units on their own every 3.5 s (the wiki at level 13 and up; the game files say 3 s, plus a delay of up to 0.5 s), the first as soon as they can.
- The Spirit stands 100 units in front of Illaoi with its champion's size; Illaoi attacks it only when she can't attack the champion. The tether breaks when the champion gets 1500 units from it (edge to edge); a Vessel is slowed as the Spirit dies or the tether breaks.
- Test of Spirit splits the fight on whether it lands; when the fight can't split further, a hit more likely than not pulls the Spirit.
- Leap of Faith's Tentacle rises awake at once, 700 units from Illaoi toward the enemy.
- The enemy attacks a Tentacle before Illaoi when it can reach one awake and the Tentacle could slam it.
- Illaoi casts Harsh Lesson to leap at an enemy just out of her reach, and between attacks otherwise.
