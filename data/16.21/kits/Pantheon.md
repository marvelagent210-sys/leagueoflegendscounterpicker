## Innate: Mortal Will

Source: [Template:Data Pantheon/Mortal Will](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Pantheon/Mortal_Will?oldid=3963570) (revision 3963570). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Pantheon generates a stack of Mortal Will whenever he lands a basic attack on-hit or casts an ability, stacking up to 5 times. At 5 stacks, Pantheon's next basic ability consumes the stacks to become empowered with an additional effect.

- **Mechanics:** on-hit

Pantheon gains maximum stacks of Mortal Will upon starting the game, completing a Recall channel, and respawning.

Notes:

- The current number of Mortal Will stacks is represented by a counter under Pantheon's health bar, visible to all players. It will light up when he reaches maximum stacks and the empowered effect is ready.

- **Mechanics:** channel (4 s); slow (4 s, 10%)

## W: Shield Vault

Source: [Template:Data Pantheon/Shield Vault](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Pantheon/Shield_Vault?oldid=4026707) (revision 4026707). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: physical · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: true

Active: Pantheon leaps to the front of the target enemy's location. If the target is within 800 units upon arrival, he deals physical damage and stuns them for 1 second. Against minions and monsters, Shield Vault has a minimum damage of 60 and is capped at 150.

- **Physical Damage:** 6 / 6.5 / 7 / 7.5 / 8% of target's maximum health (+ 1.5% per 100 AP) (+ 0.4% per 100 Pantheon's bonus health) → at max rank, level 20: 8% of targetMaxHealth (+ 1.5% per100:AP) (+ 0.4% per100:other:pantheon's bonus health)
- **Mechanics:** dash; stun (1 s)

Mortal Will: Pantheon empowers his next basic attack within 4 seconds to have a 0.375-second cast time and strike the target 3 times over a brief period, dealing 40 – 55 (based on level)% AD physical damage per hit, up to 120 – 165 (based on level)% AD. Each hit is affected by critical strike modifiers and applies on-hit effects.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike; on-hit

Empowered Shield Vault resets Pantheon's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- Applies spell damage on the initial vault. Deals basic damage on all the attacks from the empowered ability.
- Pantheon will be ordered to basic attack the target at the end of the leap.
- The empowered attack rolls a critical strike once for all strikes; either all will critically strike, or none will. If Sundered Sky Lightshield Strike is ready, all three strikes will critically strike.
- Despite the multi-hit attack only having a hidden cast time, Pantheon will remain unable to act until the last strike has been dealt.
    - The empowered attack's total time is affected by Pantheon's attack speed.
    - The attack's total time is the cast time plus Pantheon's attack windup time.
- The 3 strikes that occur after Empowered Shield Vault each count as a separate cast instance for effects such as Electrocute and Eclipse Ever Rising Moon.
- The 3 strikes that occur after Empowered Shield Vault each apply a stack of Mortal Will, even if the attacks are dodged, blocked, or missed while Pantheon is blinded.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against structures.
- The empowered attack does not cause Pantheon to attack thrice. Effects that trigger on-attack or interact with the attack windup will only trigger once and thus do not interact with Shield Vault. (e.g. Lethal Tempo, Hail of Blades).

- **Mechanics:** channel (2 s); untargetable (2 s); cc-immune (2 s); channel (2.25 s); reveal

After 0.8 seconds into the channel, Pantheon hurls his spear to the target location over 0.2 seconds that deals 40 / 70 / 100 / 130 / 160 / 190 (based on Comet Spear's rank) (+ 115% bonus AD) (+ 50% AP) physical damage to enemies near the impact and slows them by 50% for 2 seconds.

- **Magic Damage:** 300 / 500 / 700 (+ 100% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 700 (+ 100% AP)

Grand Starfall is placed on a 30-second cooldown if the first channel is canceled.

Notes:

- The spear's damage is not empowered by Mortal Will, nor increased against enemies below 20% of their maximum health.
- During the second channel, Pantheon gains a minimum health threshold of 1.

- Pinging the ability will inform allies in chat which visible enemy champions are in range of Grand Starfall.
- Pantheon is considered to be in his original casting position even after leaping, but he will blink to the target location the moment the second channel completes.
- Using Grand Starfall will inform allies with a ping.
- If Pantheon leaves the Death Realm during Grand Starfall's shockwave, its hitbox will become invisible but it will still deal damage.
- Experimental Hexplate Overdrive, Fiendhunter Bolts Opening Barrage, and Zeke's Convergence Frostfire Tempest activate at the end of Grand Starfall's second channel, or at the end of the initial channel if it was interrupted.

### Assumptions

- Pantheon starts every fight with full Mortal Will stacks, as after a recall.
- An ability cast that consumes Mortal Will doesn't also give the next stack.
- Comet Spear's hurl is held for the shortest time (0.35 s) before release.
- Shield Vault's leap is quick (2,000 units a second); the wiki gives no speed.
- Aegis Assault's 11 strikes (one every 4 ticks) share its 100% AD total; the slam follows the full channel.
- Aegis Assault's invulnerability covers every hit: in one dimension the enemy stays in front of Pantheon.
- Grand Starfall is used only to get away: after its interruptible 2-second channel Pantheon vanishes and lands far away.
