### Assumptions

- Tailwind never grants Janna her own directional allied movement aura. Actual living Champion-class allied pets can receive the six-percent aura only after their own real displacement or basic-attack declaration establishes a heading toward her. Unknown stationary orientation is not fabricated; a stationary body retains its last observed heading. The proc is one thirty-percent bonus-movement-speed on-hit, distinct from Zephyr's already included same ratio. No allied champion, turret or minion wave body is invented.
- Howling Gale anchors its origin and firing direction at the initial instant cast; Janna can walk, attack and cast while it charges, and charging causes no contacts. Manual release is a second activation, automatic release is not. Range and speed interpolate the pinned field endpoints over three seconds and retain a 1.25-second full flight. Damage interpolates its primary minimum/maximum by actual charge duration. Primary knockup series caps at 1.25 seconds after 1.5 charged seconds; the binary instead encodes 0.5+0.25 per second up to 3 seconds. The primary series controls this documented conflict. Width 240 is the sidestep width; no unsourced longitudinal endpoint radius is added. A real missile traverses actual hostile bodies, with area pet scaling, projectile destruction and one CC refund per launched storm. The anchored charge and released flight may finish after caster death; neither rearms on resurrection. Full hit/miss branching applies to decisive control.
- Zephyr's ghosting and sourced ten-percent plus AP movement bonus persist through its active cooldown: the pinned primary does not say they are disabled on cast. Its 550 edge range, 0.245-second cast, 1600 homing flight, dynamic AP slow capped 99%, target-loss cancellation, spell shield and parry use shared rules. Primary damage already includes Tailwind's thirty-percent bonus-movement-speed ratio, so it is not added twice. No unsupported unit-targeted pet command is invented for W.
- Eye of the Storm defaults to self; an explicit aimed cast can select an actual living Champion-class ally within 800. The sourced 175 forgiveness is a pending-target allowance, not extra declaration range; the instantaneous cast has no pending target-loss window. Shields refresh their own source/ability layer and its bonus AD is active only while that exact positive shield holds, including synchronous shield breaking and owner death. Primary shield lasts 4 seconds; binary DecayGracePeriod is also 4 seconds, so no unquoted earlier decay curve is invented despite the blurb mentioning decay. Turrets and their kill-credit transfer require an actual turret entity and are outside this arena. Accepted slow/airborne against an actual enemy Champion-class recipient refunds 20% of remaining E cooldown once per spell instance; damage-only contact and denied control give no refund.
- Monsoon instantly contacts real hostile bodies within 700 center range, pushing each outward toward 875 center separation over 0.5 seconds with terrain collision and real control defenses; no guessed lateral movement is added. Its stationary interruptible 3-second channel emits exactly twelve quarter-second heals using absolute tick deadlines, to self and actual nearby targetable Champion-class allies. Each pulse uses current stats and recipient healing rules. Moving, attacking, ordinary casting, disabling control, death and resurrection stop future healing. Fight may use R against visible threats after significant missing health; hold/trade/flee reserve it. Completed trades retreat before optional actions.
- Reference allocation uses self-only E shield and AD uptime, the primary minimum-charge Q per cooldown, one Zephyr and its included Tailwind ratio per cooldown, basic Tailwind procs and a sustained Monsoon healing rate including channel time. It does not price imagined allies, E cooldown-refund chains, guaranteed charged hits or turret damage. Actual scenarios still require mana, real contacts, active shields, missing health and mode policy.
