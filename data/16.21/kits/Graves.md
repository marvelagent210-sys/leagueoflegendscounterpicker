## Innate: New Destiny

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: true · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate - Double Barrel: Graves basic attacks fire his shotgun to consume ammunition within 2 shells. He will reload over a few seconds immediately after expending all shells, or after withholding a leftover shell for a while. The reload time is reduced by Graves level and bonus attack speed, and is cancelled if Graves reloads a shell with Quickdraw or expends a leftover shell. Graves is unable to declare basic attacks during the reload time if he has expended all shells.

- **Mechanics:** lockout

Innate - 12-Gauge: Graves basic attack sprays 4 pellets in a cone, each colliding with the first enemy unit in their path, dealing 70.0002 – 100.005 (based on level)% AD physical damage plus 23.3115 – 33.3037 (based on level)% AD additional physical damage for each subsequent pellet hitting the same target, up to 139.935 – 199.916 (based on level)% AD total physical damage against a single target. Pellets deal 25% reduced damage against structures.

Critical strikes spray 6 pellets over a 25% wider cone, with each pellet dealing 50% increased damage, resulting in 199.976% (+ Infinity Edge *0.5*(1+5*0.33302)/(1+3*0.33302)%) total damage if all pellets hit a single target.

- **Mechanics:** critical strike

Pellets apply life steal. Only the first pellet to hit each enemy applies on-hit effects.

- **Mechanics:** life steal; life steal; on-hit

Innate - Buckshot: Non-champion units hit by more than one pellet are knocked back, though not through terrain.

- **Mechanics:** airborne

Notes:

- Graves takes 2.08 seconds to reload 2 shells (when the clip is empty) and 1.3 seconds to reload 1 shell. He must idle with 1 shell for 2.08 seconds before he starts reloading. These values can be improved with bonus attack speed.
    - Values of bonus attack speed between 150% and 200% have a negative effect on Graves' reload speed.
    - Attack speed growth will always have a positive effect on Graves' reload speed and is not counted toward the 150% and 200% attack speed thresholds. For example, Graves gains 58.995% bonus attack speed from attack speed growth at level 20; meaning the thresholds at level 20 are at 208.995% and 258.995% respectively.
    - Maximum reload speed is 0.65114 – 0.89119 (based on level), which equates to a reload timer of 1.5357680376 – 1.12209517611 (based on level) seconds, which is the shortest reload time at the 150% threshold.
      - Precise formula is unknown, but it is confirmed to be confusing. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXmXsCdqAyw&t=520s]
- The in-game HUD for the champion's stat panel incorrectly displays Graves "reload speed" for reloading 2 shells as his attack speed, instead of displaying his actual attack speed which behaves normally like any other champion. This displayed value more accurately represents his reloads per second, and not attacks per second as the attack speed tooltip in the HUD would suggest.
- Graves can hit multiple individual enemies with a single shot from his basic attack. The first pellet hitting an enemy:
    - Deals the highest damage out of all other pellets.
    - Applies and triggers on-hit effects.
      - "Single-use" on-hit effects such as Spellblade items will be applied to the very first enemy hit by a pellet (closest one in this case).
    - Applies basic damage, while all additional pellets deal default damage.
    - Is reduced by Warden's Mail Rock Solid.
- Graves attack windup is based on such a low value that it will always take only 1 game tick, even at the lowest possible attack speed of 0.2.
- When Graves has his attack range increased by 35% to 573.75 via Rapid Firecannon Sharpshooter, it has special effects on the cone spread of his basic attack:
    - The reach of the individual missiles is also increased by 35% to 843.75 / 783.75.
    - The spread of the individual missiles is decreased by 35% (angle × 1/1.35) to 17.7° / 22.2°.
- Pellets will not hit wards nor jungle plants.
    - When Graves performs a basic attack against these units, he will fire a standard ranged homing missile at the target to successfully hit it.
      - This same missile is also used for when his basic attack is empowered by an Energized effect.
- Pellets can be dodged and blocked, but not missed while Graves is blinded.
    - While Graves is blinded, the pellets are special cased to fire in a random direction that is always away from the primary target of his basic attack.
      - Enemies within range of the pellets in their randomly fired direction will be hit as the pellets cannot be missed.
- Basic attack resets other than Quickdraw do not affect Graves basic attack timer.
- Structures can be hit by multiple pellets, as with other valid targets.
    - If Graves' basic attack critically strikes against an enemy behind their structure, and this structure is hit by all pellets, the damage dealt to the structure is increased by 33.3%.
- On-attack effects such as Help, Pix! and Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury will be triggered as usual from the completion of Graves' basic attack windup. They will obey their standard target-acquisition rules in spite of Graves' special basic attack behavior.
    - Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury can still fire its bolts at secondary targets even if they were already hit by the pellets of Graves' basic attack.
- Black Cleaver Carve will be applied for the physical damage dealt by each pellet, meaning that each of Graves' basic attacks can apply up to 4 stacks of the effect, increased to the maximum of 5 with a critical strike.
- Sundered Sky Lightshield Strike will function normally with Graves' basic attacks, causing the pellets to critically strike if the primary target was also a target of the item's effect.
    - If none of the pellets hit the target of Lightshield Strike's effect, however, the item's effect will not be consumed on the target thus not triggering the heal nor applying the on-target-cooldown, but still causing Graves' basic attack to critically strike as if the effect was successfully consumed.
      - His basic attacks against the marked target will be guaranteed to critically strike until the item's effect has been consumed by at least one pellet hitting them.
    - Pellets will not consume the item's effect on secondary targets that are also targets of the item's effect; they will not consume the mark of enemies near the primary target that are marked by Lightshield Strike.
- Randuin's Omen Resilience will reduce the critical damage dealt by every pellet, resulting in each pellet dealing less damage than if they were to not critically strike.
- Kraken Slayer Bring It Down VFX for its missile appears to fire at the primary target, but will always collide with the target hit by the first pellet.

### Assumptions

- New Destiny starts with two shells. Spending occurs at launch, empty reload prevents attacks, one-shell idle reload begins after 2.08 seconds and E cancels either reload. The one-tick windup uses the primary note. The published reload formula is explicitly unknown: this model interpolates measured 2.08s toward the level-dependent published minimum as external bonus AS rises to 150%, scales one-shell reload by 1.3/2.08, and caps there. Growth affects the minimum via level; the documented anomalous 150–200% slowdown curve is unmodeled. This is a sourced-endpoint approximation, not an exact server formula.
- Normal four-pellet 24 degree and critical six-pellet 30-degree fans use real circular hitboxes on the duel axis and closest actual target interception independently for each ray. Critical geometry is weighted by critical chance; critical pellets gain 50% of bonus critical damage per the primary footnote (150% at ordinary 200% critical damage). Only each target's first pellet deals basic damage/onhits; extras deal default damage with basic defenses and life steal. The wiki additional-pellet table uses 0.33302 while the exact client calculation says 0.3330000043: the pinned primary table wins. The one-dimensional arena has no lateral movement, and blind deterministically fires away from the declared target rather than inventing a lateral random lane. Nonchampion knockback distance/duration are not published and are omitted rather than guessed.
- Q has a real 3000 speed piercing outward missile, a two-second powder clock measured from launch, a terrain-triggered detonation at the versioned 0.2-second delay, then a 1600 speed reverse wave. The parser's 0.231 is a rutngt rendering artifact; exact source raw says 0.2 rounded up to a game tick. Its endpoint rectangle occupies the sourced total 900 versus 800 forward reach, and each target can take the explosion/return only once. Projectile walls erase the trail. Actual raised terrain is checked; the environment walls boolean supplies no invented position. Unbreakable contact triggers the same sourced terrain fuse at the actual shield collision; its collision damage and delayed second effect remain independently guarded.
- Smoke Screen launches at cast start at 1500 speed, then deals 260 + 60% AP magic and 50% slow for 0.5 seconds in 200 radius. Its independent four-second field uses 0.25-second pulses. Anchored 250-radius sight reduction (itself estimated in the primary tooltip) persists through CC immunity/cleanse and untargetability; the separately guarded nearsight cannot reapply after resistance/removal until exit/reentry. Leaving smoke immediately clears its sight limit. No allied shared vision is invented in this duel.
- Quickdraw reloads one shell and resets the attack timer, grants one True Grit stack plus a second only when a visible targetable champion is ahead within 1300 at some actual dash tick, caps 8 and refreshes 4 seconds on damage to explicit non-minions. An unclassified summon is not assumed a non-minion. Each stack grants 19 armor and 9.5 MR. The dash uses pinned 750 speed and 275–375 distance with clear-endpoint terrain crossing. Only E begun during an actual reload enables Q/W/R overlays for that dash; ordinary dashes do not permit them.
- R pierces actual pets with a single-target spell packet, explodes on champion contact even when spellshielded or at 1100 range, and applies the separate reduced area packet only to additional targets in its 740 reach cone anchored 150 behind contact. The cone intersects the duel axis, so transverse 60-degree coverage is not separately modeled. Recoil moves 400 immediately along terrain-limited space as an explicit timing approximation; no movement speed or duration is published. Root, ground and airborne prevent it without preventing the shot. Its finite dash duration and anti-dash interaction are unmodeled. The stale GravesAutoAttackRecoil object contains Riven spell fields and is not treated as recoil-speed evidence. Launched projectiles and smoke survive Graves's death.
- The reference budget replaces ordinary attacks with a two-shell/reload cycle using the same sourced-endpoint approximation and assumes the centerline target receives the full fan; E's defensive stacks and conditional reload acceleration are not free starting power. Q budgets both passes once per hasted cast cycle, W/R use their direct damage formulas.
