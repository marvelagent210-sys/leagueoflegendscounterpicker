Innate - Shard of the Monolith: Malphite gains increased percentage size equal to 8% of his total armor, capped at 35% increased size at 437.5 armor.

Innate - Granite Shield: Malphite grants himself a shield equal to 10% of his maximum health. The shield lasts until it is broken, and replenishes to full strength after a few seconds of not taking damage.

- **Mechanics:** shield

Notes:

- Malphite's increased size is affected by his base armor and growth, but also reduced by Black Cleaver. His increased size does not consider Thunderclap bonus armor.
    - The bonus will become a size reduction if Malphite is dropped to negative armor values.
- Granite Shield does not refresh its cooldown from damage that is mitigated by shields.

## Q: Seismic Shard

Source: [Template:Data Malphite/Seismic Shard](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Malphite/Seismic_Shard?oldid=4003493) (revision 4003493). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Unit · Affects: Enemies · Damage type: magic · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: true · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Malphite sends a shard to the target enemy that deals magic damage and slows them for 3 seconds upon impact.

- **Magic Damage:** 70 / 120 / 170 / 220 / 270 (+ 60% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 270 (+ 60% AP)
- **Slow:** 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% → at max rank, level 20: 40%
- **Mechanics:** slow (3 s, 40%)

Malphite also gains bonus movement speed equal to the raw amount of movement speed the target lost from the slow for the duration.

Notes:

- The rock spawns 100 units in front of Malphite.
- Malphite will gain the bonus movement speed for the full duration as long as Seismic Shard impacts the target.
    - Both a buff shown in the hotbar and duration bar above the ability icon will indicate the remaining duration.
- Seismic Shard's movement speed gain is based off the target's movement speed raw total movement speed lost at the time of impact.
    - For example, targets that were already slowed by another effect will grant Malphite diminished movement speed, while targets that are slowed by another effect after being impacted by Seismic Shard will not.
    - Slow-resistant and slow-immune targets will reduce or nullify Malphite's gain.
- If the target becomes untargetable, dies, or is too far away or no longer in sight during the cast time, this ability will cancel but does not go on cooldown nor pay its cost (if applicable).

## W: Thunderclap

Source: [Template:Data Malphite/Thunderclap](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Malphite/Thunderclap?oldid=4091409) (revision 4091409). Ranks: 5.

| Cooldown starts | on-cast |

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: physical · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Malphite gains bonus armor, tripled while Granite Shield is active.

- **Bonus Armor:** 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30% armor → at max rank, level 20: 30% of armor
- **Increased Bonus Armor:** 30 / 45 / 60 / 75 / 90% armor → at max rank, level 20: 90% of armor

Active: Malphite empowers his next basic attack within 6 seconds to have an uncancellable windup, gain 50 bonus range, and deal additional physical damage on-hit.

- **Additional Physical Damage:** 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 / 70 (+ 20% AP) (+ 15% armor) → at max rank, level 20: 70 (+ 20% AP) (+ 15% armor)
- **Mechanics:** uncancellable windup; on-hit

Additionally, Malphite's basic attacks on-hit for the next 5 seconds are empowered to trigger a cone in the direction of the target that deals physical damage to enemies hit.

- **Physical Damage:** 15 / 25 / 35 / 45 / 55 (+ 30% AP) (+ 15% armor) → at max rank, level 20: 55 (+ 30% AP) (+ 15% armor)
- **Mechanics:** on-hit

Thunderclap deals 180% damage against monsters.

Thunderclap resets Malphite's basic attack timer.

- **Mechanics:** attack reset

Notes:

- The main target is dealt basic damage, though without applying life steal, while the cone deals area non-proc damage without applying spell effects.
    - The cone will trigger E damage reduction, Cheap Shot, Conqueror, Dark Harvest, Electrocute, Summon Aery, Eclipse, World Atlas enemy champion proc, and Ocean Dragon Soul.
      - It is also special cased to trigger Tear of the Goddess.
    - The cone does not benefit from Spear of Shojin damage amp.
- The passive will amplify negative armor, too, if Malphite's armor is dropped that low.
- The passive's bonus armor stacks multiplicatively with Jak'Sho, The Protean Voidborn Resilience and Mountainous Vigor and stacks recursively with Conditioning.
- The empowered attack:
    - Benefits from Thunderclap's cone effect.
    - Prevents Malphite from casting Seismic Shard and Ground Slam during its windup.
    - Cannot have its windup canceled by new movement or attack commands, or casting Unstoppable Force.
    - Can have its windup canceled by casting Hextech Rocketbelt.
- The cone starts from Malphite's location.
    - Unlike similar spells, it does not deal its damage separately to the primary target of the attack(s); if the target is outside the area of effect when struck, it will not take the additional damage.

- **Magic Damage:** 200 / 300 / 400 (+ 90% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 400 (+ 90% AP)
- **Mechanics:** dash; displacement-immune; airborne (1.5 s)

Notes:

- Unstoppable Force has no minimum dash range.

    - If the dash is interrupted without Malphite dying, for instance when he enters resurrection during Unstoppable Force, he halts and the ability instead affects enemies at the stopped location.
