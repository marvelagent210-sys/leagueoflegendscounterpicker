## Innate: The Darkin Scythe

Source: [Template:Data Kayn/The Darkin Scythe](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Kayn/The_Darkin_Scythe?oldid=4053172) (revision 4053172). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Kayn has a secondary experience bar that tracks progress toward his Darkin Slayer and Shadow Assassin forms. He gathers orbs from champion takedowns and each instance of damage (excluding damage over time) dealt against champions. Once he has earned enough orbs, he unlocks a form based on whether he earned more from melee or ranged champions (for Darkin Slayer and Shadow Assassin, respectively), with the other form unlocked later.

While near his team's summoning platform, Kayn can choose a form to enter transformation over 6 seconds, becoming invulnerable, untargetable, and unable to act for the duration. At the end of the transformation he is restored to full health and mana. The transformation will grant a passive bonus and permanently empower some of Kayn's abilities based on the form chosen. He gains a one-time Homeguard once the transformation is complete. Kayn cannot activate the transformation while he is affected by cast-inhibiting crowd control or a lockout that disables abilities.

- **Mechanics:** invulnerable; untargetable; lockout

Shadow Assassin Bonus: Upon entering combat with enemy champions, Kayn becomes empowered for 3 seconds to deal 20 – 40 (based on level)% of post-mitigation physical and magic damage dealt against enemy champions as bonus magic damage. This effect cannot trigger again until Kayn has been out of combat with champions for over 8 seconds, with the timer being reset if he recasts Umbral Trespass.

Darkin Slayer Bonus: Rhaast heals for 25% (+ 0.5% per 100 bonus health) of the post-mitigation physical damage that he deals to enemy champions with his abilities.

- **Mechanics:** heal

Notes:

- The Shadow Assassin Bonus will not be triggered nor have its out-of-combat timer be refreshed if Kayn deals true damage.
- If the Shadow Assassin Bonus is granted by Kayn dealing damage, the triggering instance of damage will benefit from the bonus.
- Unlike normal damage modifiers, the Shadow Assassin Bonus does not inherit damage tags from the source.
- Entering transformation will not destroy in-flight projectiles.
- ; The Orb System
- The experience is depicted as orbs that drop upon the ground. Melee champions drop red orbs that grant progress towards Darkin Slayer, while ranged champions drop blue orbs that grant progress for Shadow Assassin. Orbs will remain on the ground until automatically collected by Kayn regardless of range once he leaves combat. The orbs will be collected even if Kayn dies.
- The experience bar above his portrait gauges his current progression, the color reflects the form with greater progress. Kayn can transform by interacting with his portrait, and can wait 4 minutes until the other form becomes available, reduced by 5 seconds for each collected orb of their type after that.
- Melee orbs grant 2 points per hit and 6 points per takedown.
- Ranged orbs grant 3 points per hit and 7 points per takedown.
- The amount of points received per orb is multiplied by 3 – 10.39375 (based on minutes).
- The bar gets filled up completely at 500 points.
- If the two forms are within 8% of each other, the last champion range type hit will determine the winner. If both types are hit at the same time, the transformation will default to Darkin Slayer.
- Each side's bar has a bucket system, starting at 60 points.
    - Each time an orb is taken the system checks if there's an amount of points in the bucket equal to or higher than the amount received from the orb.
- If there is, Kayn is given an additional orb of the same type he received, while also subtracting that amount of points from the bucket.
    - The bucket fills by 0.8 points per second if out of champion combat for 70 seconds.
- Damage dealt by damage over time effects and specifically First Strike and Summon Aery will not grant orbs.

- Shadow Step can interact with player-generated terrain.
- If Kayn is inside terrain when the effect ends, he will be placed correspondingly to the nearest valid space.
- Sleep will not interrupt Shadow Step.

## R: Umbral Trespass

Source: [Template:Data Kayn/Umbral Trespass](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Kayn/Umbral_Trespass?oldid=4007627) (revision 4007627). Ranks: 3.

Targeting: Unit, Direction · Affects: Enemies, Self · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: special · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: true · Usable while knocked down: false

Passive: Kayn marks enemy champions he damaged in the last 3.15 seconds.

Active: Kayn vanishes and dashes to a marked enemy champion over 0.25 seconds, attaching himself to them at the end of the dash. After 0.5 seconds of his arrival, he channels for up to 2 seconds, during which the target is revealed. Umbral Trespass can be recast during the channel after 0.1 seconds have elapsed, and does so automatically after the duration or if it is interrupted.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable (0.25 s); dash; attach; channel (2 s); true sight (2 s)

Recast: After a delay of 0.5 seconds to 0.75 seconds, Kayn deals physical damage to the target and dashes out from their body in the target direction, detaching himself from them and becoming targetable again at the end of the dash.

- **Physical Damage:** 150 / 250 / 350 (+ 150% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 350 (+ 150% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** dash

Shadow Assassin Bonus: Umbral Trespass gains 200 bonus cast range and emerge range. Additionally, emerging will reset The Darkin Scythe's cooldown.

Darkin Slayer Bonus: Umbral Trespass is modified to deal physical damage equal to 15% (+ 10% per 100 bonus AD) of the target's maximum health, and also heals Rhaast for 11.25% (+ 7.5% per 100 bonus AD) of the target's maximum health after the recast's delay.

- **Mechanics:** heal

Notes:

- Umbral Trespass' recast delay lasts for an inconsistent period of time.
- The initial cast counts as an ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade.
    - The recast (both manual and automatic) does not.
    - The ability activation happens when Kayn is attached to his target and the channel starts.
      - Kassadin cannot reduce Force Pulse cooldown because Kayn is untargetable during the activation.
- The ability will recast if Kayn is ordered a movement command.
- Kayn will reappear from being vanished as soon as the recast is cast, while remaining untargetable.
- Upon recast, Kayn will always damage his target from the east, regardless of the direction he chooses to dash in.
- Kayn will arrive to the target after 0.25 seconds regardless of how far they move away from him during the dash.
- If Umbral Trespass is targeted on an enemy with a spell shield or a Fiora with her Riposte active, the ability will fail to cast entirely, being placed on full cooldown without triggering the active's effects (as well as on-cast effects).
    - Spell shield will not block the damage from the recast.
- If the target dies, enters resurrection, or is sent to the Death Realm while Kayn is attached, the channel will be cancelled and he will instantly detach and reappear on top of the target's location. This does not occur if Kayn is in the process of emerging from the target.
    - If those same conditions occur before the attachment, Kayn will reappear at the target's location at the end of his dash.
    - Umbral Trespass' cooldown will sometimes be refunded at the end of the channel if any of these conditions occur during it.
    - If the target is a clone that expires while Kayn is attached, Umbral Trespass will recast automatically.
- If Kayn dies while attached and the recast is available, Umbral Trespass' damage is dealt immediately. If the recast is not available, Umbral Trespass' damage is not dealt at all.
    - Entering resurrection while attached does not deal Umbral Trespass' damage regardless of if the recast is available or not.
      - Kayn will detach immediately in both cases.
- Umbral Trespass' interactions with crowd control:
    - All forms of cast-inhibiting crowd control will interrupt the channel.
    - Upon being cast, Kayn is cleansed of drowsiness effects.
    - During Umbral Trespass, Kayn is immune to fear.
    - Despite being a movement channel, Umbral Trespass is not interrupted by root.
    - Root will not disable Umbral Trespass' recast, despite it being a dash.
- As Rhaast, Umbral Trespass will grant the heal automatically if the target dies during the channel.
    - The heal is not granted if the target dies before the channel starts.
- Kayn will draw turret aggro after the recast's dash, despite the damage being dealt to the enemy while he was untargetable.
- Until the recast or after the duration, the ability will have had no effect on the target for the purposes of Electrocute or the 'in-combat' status.
- The damage is not blocked if the target is untargetable at the time of the recast.
    - While dismounting, Kled blocks the damage of Umbral Trespass.
- Umbral Trespass cannot be cast on Sion during Glory in Death.
- During Umbral Trespass, Kayn will not take the self-inflicted damage from Death's Dance Ignore Pain if the damage was sourced from a turret.
- Kayn will copy his target's total size for Umbral Trespass' duration, even if their size is smaller than his.
- The player's screen will direct to the target's position upon attaching.
- As Rhaast, an alternate icon is used for the initial activation of Umbral Trespass.
- The following table refers for interactions while Kayn is channeling and dashing out of the victim:
    - The dash out is not interrupted by cast-inhibiting crowd control.
