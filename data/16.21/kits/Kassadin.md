### Assumptions

- Nether Blade's unshielded active replaces its passive magic on-hit with one 150 + 80% AP packet. A spell shield blocks that active packet and leaves the ordinary 25 + 10% AP passive on-hit; the pinned replacement wording and shield note are interpreted together as this fallback.
- Nether Blade restores 30% of missing mana on a connected champion attack, including a damage block or an active-only spell shield, but not blind, dodge or parry. The source specifies damage shielding rather than suppression of its separate mana restoration. The generic champion attack path against pets currently omits on-hit packets and resource restoration; structures and minions are outside this duel.
- Force Pulse observes successful cast declarations exactly once, including recasts and instant casts, excluding classified toggles, transformations and internal deliveries. A canceled declared cast still counts. Its own activation refunds the newly-started cooldown at cast completion; no refund is stored while Force Pulse is already ready. At 30 ticks per second a 0.75-second refund rounds up to 23 ticks.
- Force Pulse's 78-degree cone is represented by its directed 600-unit interval on the one-dimensional line, using the caster's position at cast completion and the direction selected at cast declaration.
- Riftwalk uses the stack count at cast declaration for damage and mana payment, then increments and refreshes its shared 15-second timer after successful completion. Stack expiry during that cast does not change its selected tier. Its selected blink direction and distance remain fixed while the origin uses the caster's position at completion, consistent with the pinned Flash-during-cast note. Arrival damage checks the target's center within 270 units of the actual blink endpoint; the record does not specify a target-hitbox convention. Summoner Flash and terrain-specific blink relocation are outside the model.
- The stat budget conservatively uses Force Pulse's nominal 17-second cooldown and omits its qualifying-cast refunds. Its Riftwalk damage reference uses a 10-second full-mana window, reserves nominal Q/E/W cycle costs, adds natural mana regeneration and connected W mana restoration, and consumes only affordable casts of the exact stack ladder. This reference assumes contact and uninterrupted casts, rather than perpetual maximum-stack Riftwalk. Nether Blade's passive is subtracted once per active reference use so replacement damage is not added twice.
