## Innate: Inherent Glamour

Source: [Template:Data Neeko/Inherent Glamour](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Neeko/Inherent_Glamour?oldid=4049951) (revision 4049951). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: false · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Neeko has a collection of disguises to take on the appearance of allied champions, displayed on her Disguise Menu. She can use the menu to activate a disguise, switch her current disguise to a different one, or deactivate the current disguise by selecting herself, all of which place Inherent Glamour on a 0.5-second cooldown. Taking damage from a champion or turret while not disguised puts Inherent Glamour on a 2-second cooldown.

Standing by the nearest visible allied or neutral non-champion non-structure unit while not disguised will gather its Sho'Ma over 2 seconds, adding its disguise to the collection. Activating this disguise places Inherent Glamour on a 1-second cooldown. Neeko can collect only one Sho'Ma, but can discard or consume it using her Disguise Menu. She can store Sho'Ma from the following units, as long as they are targetable:

- **Mechanics:** reveal (2 s)

While disguised, Neeko can use basic attacks while keeping her form. Her disguise breaks if she:

Breaking the disguise via dying, being immobilized or taking damage from a turret puts Inherent Glamour on a 2-second cooldown, entering the enemy spawn platform does not put it on cooldown, and all other methods of breaking the disguise put Inherent Glamour on a 1-second cooldown.

Inherent Glamour's disguise is not deactivated from Pop Blossom until after 0.5 seconds of its cast. Neeko cannot activate Inherent Glamour while she is affected by cast-inhibiting crowd control or a lockout that disables casting abilities.

- **Sho'Ma Gathering Range:** 400

- **Leap Range:** 500

Notes:

- Before disguising
- Neeko's disguise collection is offered through a special Disguise Menu in the HUD. The menu holds up to 5 slots, based on the number of allied champions. The player can select a disguise by left-click clicking on their portrait or via the Champion Specific Interaction hotkeys (default: Shift+F1–F5). The Sho'Ma disguise can be discarded by clicking on the 'x' next to the disguise's icon or via the sixth Champion Specific Interaction hotkey (default: Shift+F6).
    - The slot containing her Sho'Ma disguise will be third in the HUD if there are two or more allied champions in the game, otherwise it will be the rightmost slot. The remaining slots will contain allied champion disguises in order of their appearance in the pre-game Loading Screen.

## W: Shapesplitter

Source: [Template:Data Neeko/Shapesplitter](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Neeko/Shapesplitter?oldid=4022848) (revision 4022848). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Direction, Location · Affects: Self · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: false · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Neeko's basic attacks on-hit generate a stack of Shapesplitter, which refreshes on subsequent attacks and stacks up to 2 times. At 2 stacks, her next basic attack on-attack consumes them all to become non-projectile, deal bonus magic damage and grant her bonus movement speed for 1 second. This attack deals 75 bonus magic damage against monsters.

- **Bonus Magic Damage:** 30 / 65 / 100 / 135 / 170 (+ 60% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 170 (+ 60% AP)
- **Bonus Movement Speed:** 10 / 17.5 / 25 / 32.5 / 40% → at max rank, level 20: 40%
- **Mechanics:** on-hit; on-attack; projectile

Active: Neeko creates a clone of her current form, after which they both instantly become invisible for 0.5 seconds and gain bonus movement speed for 3 seconds. Shapesplitter can be recast at any time while the clone is active.

- **Bonus Movement Speed:** 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% → at max rank, level 20: 40%
- **Mechanics:** summon; invisible (0.5 s)

The clone lasts for 3 seconds, moves a fixed distance in the target direction, and is untargetable to allies and for the first 0.5 seconds of its lifespan to enemies. If the clone takes damage, it will disappear prematurely after 0.5 seconds. Casting an ability, summoner spell, item active, or starting a channel causes the clone to stop moving. Additionally, the clone plays the casting animations for Blooming Burst and Tangle-Barbs as well as Pop Blossom visual effects whenever Neeko casts them. The clone will also play the animation of her next expression or Recall for a set duration that overrides its original duration and will become unable to move while doing so.

- **Mechanics:** untargetable (3 s); channel

Recast: Neeko sends her clone to the target location if it is not playing an expression or Recall animation.

Notes:

- The empowered attack will not trigger against wards and jungle plants.
- The damage done by the empowered attack is attributed to Inherent Glamour in the Death Recap.
- Using a basic attack breaks stealth at the start of the attack windup.
- Recasting Shapesplitter does not count as an ability activation for the purposes of on-cast effects such as Spellblade and triggering Force Pulse passive.
- Once spawned with a disguise, the clone cannot lose the disguise.
- When mimicking the animation of Blooming Burst and Tangle-Barbs while disguised, the clone will play the disguise unit's first and third ability animation respectively.
- When mimicking the animation of Blooming Burst, the clone will turn toward Blooming Burst's target location unless it was ordered to move within the last 0.5 seconds, in which case it will not change its facing direction.
- When mimicking the animation of Tangle-Barbs, the clone will always face to the east.
- If Shapesplitter is cast during Pop Blossom delay, the clone will also mimic the ability by displaying the VFX for its area of effect. The copied VFX is only visible to enemies, and only while they have sight of Neeko.
- When Neeko forces her clone to play an expression or Recall animation, she modifies its duration based on the animation played.
    - Joke - 8 seconds.
    - Dance - 8 seconds.
    - Taunt - 4.25 seconds.
    - Laugh - 3 seconds.
    - Recall - 8 seconds.
    - Empowered Recall - 4 seconds.
      - When attempting to play the Empowered Recall animation, the clone will instead play the non-empowered Recall VFX if Neeko's player account is challenger or level 5 honor.
- While playing an expression or recall animation, the clone cannot move via pathing, even if it is affected by a forced action.
- The clone is special-cased to not apply Frozen Heart Winter's Caress after the clone's base duration (3 seconds) has elapsed.
    - This effect can still apply before that, including if the clone disappears prior to the base duration elapsing.
- The clone will sometimes not disappear prematurely after 0.5 seconds of taking damage from certain sources.
- Both Neeko and the clone use the same running animation.
    - When passing near a jungle camp for the first time, Neeko will wave at the camp. The clone will likewise wave at nearby camps every time it is spawned, which can override the running animation and allow players to recognize the clone.
- The clone can still be targeted by allied auto-targeted effects (e.g. Heal, Celestial Blessing).
- The clone benefits from allied Speed Shrine, despite being untargetable to allies.
- While disguised as any non-champion other than a small or medium Krug, the clone will display the aggro icon while standing idle.

- Shapesplitter starts without preloaded attacks. Two actual connected on-hits prepare the next declared basic; the empowered attack consumes its stacks and grants movement at declaration, even if later blinded, dodged or cancelled. Its ordinary physical damage and separate magic on-hit retain normal defenses. It is non-projectile. Ward and jungle-plant attacks do not consume the empowerment.
- Blooming Burst releases its seed at declaration, before its0.25second cast lock ends, from the declaration location with its captured direction and fixed chosen location. Each actual bloom can earn the next one by contacting a real champion or explicitly large monster, or by killing a real body. Shielded contact still qualifies. At most two additional blooms occur0.75seconds apart. At the branch cap, a champion-dependent follow-up retains its earned probability weight; an independently qualifying real pet contact can earn the full follow-up. Already released seeds and earned finite blooms survive Neeko death. No invisible farm wave is supplied.
- Shapesplitter creates a real, non-attacking champion-class clone with its own identity. Its HP/resistances/radii and movement snapshot the owner at creation because the retained generic clone source does not provide a Neeko-specific stat table. This is an explicit unverified interpretation. Its first0.5seconds are untargetable/invisible, its normal life is3seconds, and positive damage schedules disappearance after the primary wiki0.5seconds; the conflicting client0.75seconds and reported inconsistent DoT behavior remain visible. Commands/casts stop its old movement; no damaging spell is duplicated. Cosmetic emote/Recall duration extensions are outside the modeled duel commands.
- Pop Blossom activation pays100mana and begins its cooldown immediately, while its1.25second windup permits ordinary actions. Its later0.6second cast begins even during stasis, knocks up bodies within590 centered units, then damages/stuns bodies within600 at landing. The two packets test spell shields separately. Death before landing cancels unreleased damage; no obsolete client shield is granted.
- Inherent Glamour starts with no allied champion or Sho'Ma form. Only actual represented, visible allied minion/non-epic monster pets can provide a stored form after2seconds within400units. Manual I selects that stored body, without cloning its HP, armor, MR or AD. This restricted form copies attack range/projectile/speed, size/pathing and movement, and caps the adopted attack rate at2. Source-specific ward/plant/trap, allied-champion menus, map structures and non-champion targeting/damage-class exceptions are not modeled; ordinary duel results have no such fabricated form.
