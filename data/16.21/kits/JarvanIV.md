### Assumptions

- Martial Cadence snapshots 8% actual target current HP before the ordinary basic, then deals its separate physical packet after that basic only if the body survived. Its 20 minimum, 400 non-champion cap and static 3-second per-target clock are source-read. Champion-class summons and explicit clones are champions; unclassified pets receive the non-champion cap. Structures and explicitly Other props do not trigger it. Blind/dodge/parry/block preserve readiness; actual damage immunity can consume readiness. Pet-ID views synchronize their current statuses before the bonus packet.
- Dragon Strike uses the primary 0.4-second cast and 65–785 lance geometry, deals 250 + 145% bonus AD before 26% armor shred for 3 seconds, then detects the furthest real own flag within 0–850 detection length (including its 65 gameplay radius) in the selected direction. The actual dash travels 1400 units/second from the exact pinned EDashSpeed array, crossing terrain only with a clear endpoint. Root/ground do not deny its pull; knockdown can stop it. Each actual body gets one guarded 0.75-second knockup attempt per dash. The random 50–100 lateral knockup scatter is omitted in one dimension. The primary 136 damage / 300 flag widths are retained; lateral width cannot be resolved on the duel line. Champion lance damage uses the shared scalar skillshot fallback from the 0.4-second telegraph, while actual pet occupancy and dash knockup contacts stay geometric; the lance does not create an independent probability branch.
- Demacian Standard grants 30% permanent AS and a separate non-stacking 30% aura within 1000 of an actual 8-second flag. Each flag is an enemy-untargetable Minion-class body with 65 gameplay/13.6 pathing radius; its infinite HP is only a representation of enemy untargetability, never a hittable health target. No flag exists before E. Primary E has no cast time and publishes no landing delay; cosmetic missileSpeed 1450 and binary spellCastTime 0.25 do not prove script delivery timing, so flag placement/impact is immediate and this timing gap is explicit. E damage is 240 + 80% AP within 200; sight 700 uses standard sight only. Owner death removes the flag under ordinary pet lifecycle; primary does not specify a death persistence exception.
- Golden Aegis applies independently guarded 35% slows for 2 seconds to actual nearby bodies within 600, counts only accepted actual champion-class/clone contacts and grants 140 + 70% bonus AD plus 1.3% max HP per counted champion for 4 seconds. CC immunity still counts an accepted spell; spell shield/parry/stasis/untargetability do not. It never assumes a second champion, ward, minion wave or imaginary summon.
- Cataclysm targets a live visible actual enemy champion or explicitly Champion-class/clone summon within 650, retains the actual recipient ID, tracks its location over the primary 0.35-second displacement-immune leap, and survives target invisibility/untargetability as an already declared leap. Landing damage remains separately guarded. Death during the leap cancels damage/terrain and starts its full cooldown. Landing creates two real impassable wall intervals at 255–445 from its center for 3.5 seconds, blocking both sides, and applies the primary 0.15-second inward/outward perimeter displacement. The two intervals project 24 circular wall units onto one dimension; individual Ornn segment removal and lateral exits are outside this arena. Primary 350 terrain radius approximates additional damage footprint because neither source labels a server damage radius; binary castRadius 210/castRadiusSecondary 340 remain exact conflicting targeting metadata. Targeted body's landing contact is always tested against that footprint. Pets receive guarded perimeter airborne but shared pet movement has no forced-displacement action. Sight 1700 is standard sight. Manual dismissal after 0.75 seconds is an actual activation; natural expiry is not. Post-effect R cooldown begins after dismissal/expiry; no preduel terrain is supplied.
- Behavior evaluates common trade completion before offensive choices, reserves ready R in trade/hold, opens with W/E/Q in range, and dismisses an existing ring for escape. The budget adds one current-health Cadence at the half-health reference point per 3 seconds plus attack interval, Q/E/R by ordinary cast/cooldown and W's guaranteed base shield only. Aura AS receives source 8-second flag uptime per E cycle. Runtime accepted contacts and actual terrain determine outcomes; no coefficients fit matchup scores.
