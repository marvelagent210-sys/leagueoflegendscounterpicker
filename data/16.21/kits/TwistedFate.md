Notes:

    - Recasting the ability does not.

    - Upon activation, an indicator visible to allies and enemies appears above Twisted Fate to show him shuffling his deck of cards while remaining in the same order, the card he can choose at every moment glows over the others. ## E: Stacked Deck

Source: [Template:Data Twisted Fate/Stacked Deck](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Twisted_Fate/Stacked_Deck?oldid=3963571) (revision 3963571). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Enemies, Self · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: false · Projectile: true · Parry: special · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Passive: Twisted Fate gains bonus attack speed. His basic attacks on-hit generate a stack of Stacked Deck, stacking up to 3 times. At 3 stacks, his next basic attack is empowered to consume them all to deal bonus magic damage, reduced to 50% against structures. Twisted Fate gains maximum stacks of Stacked Deck upon respawning.

- **Bonus Attack Speed:** 15 / 25 / 35 / 45 / 55% → at max rank, level 20: 55%
- **Bonus Magic Damage:** 65 / 90 / 115 / 140 / 165 (+ 20% bonus AD) (+ 40% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 165 (+ 20% bonusAD) (+ 40% AP)
- **Mechanics:** on-hit

Notes:

- Stacked Deck can be dodged and/or missed if Twisted Fate is blinded (the on-hit effect is not consumed in either case) but cannot be blocked (the enhanced attack is consumed and the bonus damage is still applied).
- As of patch V14.2, Twisted Fate is intended to gain maximum stacks of Stacked Deck upon learning the ability, but currently does not.
- The empowered attack will trigger against structures but not wards.

### Assumptions

- Explicit first hovered card (Blue 0/Red 1/Gold 2; random server phase is not assumed known): 0.
- Actual Stacked Deck stacks before the duel, without an assumed respawn: 0.
- Pick a Card starts at the explicit firstCard=0; the server's random starting card is not silently optimized. Its three-card Blue→Red→Gold order uses the primary note's 0.528-second interval, whose raw annotation remains pending test, rather than the contradictory 0.5-second description. The six-second cycling window and separate six-second selected-card window use absolute clocks. One free internal lock selects only the currently hovered card, works under cast-inhibiting control, resets the basic timer and does not count as another cast. W's six-second hasted cooldown starts at attack launch or selected/cycling expiry. Exact server post-effect start between launch and impact is unquantified and this launch policy is explicit.
- Selected cards use a fixed 0.25-second attack windup and 1500-speed missile. Their entire normal attack damage is replaced by the sourced magic formula and linear critical-chance multiplier, with no extra ordinary AD/critical packet. Target death, untargetability, stasis or range loss before launch cancel freely and retain the selected card within its original expiry. Cancelled windups do not create mana, splash or Stacked Deck. An already declared empowered windup or launched card retains its captured entitlement if the selection window expires, an explicit policy where that exact boundary is unsourced; expiry starts the cooldown once and a later launch cannot restart it. Blind/dodge/parry and whole-attack spell shields use ordinary defenses. A connected damage block denies base damage but retains card utility/on-hits, an explicit interpretation where the primary does not enumerate every block edge. Blue restores 150 mana on accepted basic contact, Red slows the actual primary and deals independently defended area damage to real nearby recipients only, Gold stuns for 2 seconds. Explicit ward bodies exclude both card empowerment and Stacked Deck; no ward, plant or structure is fabricated for a duel.
- Stacked Deck starts at explicit history 0, not an invented respawn. Actual connected champion/pet attacks generate one stack, and the next consumes three for 165 + 20% bonus AD + 40% AP noncritical proc magic. Damage-only blocks still trigger it; blind/dodge/parry preserve stacks. The permanent 55% bonus attack speed participates in ordinary attack clocks. Loaded Dice records expected gold 4+3×crit for actual hostile kills in memory only; pre-fight stat spending cannot be increased by future kills and no farming gold is fabricated.
- Wild Cards is a piercing 1450-range/80-width/1000-speed fan with 56 degrees between outer rays; all actual bodies lie on the central axial ray and receive at most one contact. Separate lateral rays and lateral recipients do not exist in this 1D geometry. Cast origin is completion position, direction is declaration facing. Released cards survive owner death and actual projectile defenses remain enabled.
- Destiny reveals currently targetable enemy champion bodies, including actual champion-class summons, for 10 seconds, subject to each body's spell shield and parry according to the primary spell-shield flag. Nearsight retains priority through normal visibility rules. It creates no allies or map knowledge. Gate becomes available after 0.5 seconds, is blocked and interrupted by accepted root/ground/silence, and uses an interruptible 1.5-second channel represented by a cast lock; movement/attacks cannot bypass it. Its absolute selected endpoint clamps to 5500, projects out of actual terrain, reports a real blink, and destroys hostile homing champion or pet projectiles targeting this actual champion. Projectiles targeting other actual bodies and nonhoming missiles remain. R's 110-second hasted cooldown starts when Gate is declared or the recast window expires, including interrupted Gates. Ordinary duel AI uses Gate to flee, reserves utility in other modes, waits for legal Gold/Blue card phases and honors completed trades.
- Budget values Q, permanent attack speed, one Stacked Deck proc per four basics, and a Gold Card replacement per hasted six-second cycle plus average observed phase wait and fixed windup. The replacement subtracts that attack's ordinary expected physical damage. No ally, splash victim, farming gold or teleport damage enters its price. Blue is chosen at low mana in runtime; resource clipping and actual card waiting remain combat outcomes.
