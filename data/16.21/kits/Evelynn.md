## Innate: Demon Shade

Source: [Template:Data Evelynn/Demon Shade](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Evelynn/Demon_Shade?oldid=4038334) (revision 4038334). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Evelynn shrouds herself in Demon Shade after not performing actions that break stealth for 4 seconds. Attacking or casting abilities ends Demon Shade immediately and places it on a 4-second static cooldown, reduced to 1.25 seconds from casting Last Caress. Taking damage from champions or turrets interrupts Demon Shade and places it on a 1.5-second static cooldown.

Demon Shade: While below 250 – 590 (based on level) (+ 250% AP) health, Evelynn heals herself for 15 – 150 (based on level) every second. From level 6 onward, Demon Shade also grants camouflage.

- **Mechanics:** heal; camouflage

- **Detection Radius:** 700

Notes:

- Demon Shade is also placed on a 1.5 second cooldown upon respawning.
- Demon Shade's cooldown incurs upon starting the attack windup of the basic attack and upon the cast time of the ability.
- Demon Shade activates even if Evelynn is Recalling.
- Upon Evelynn's transition into the stealth, Demon Shade will interrupt her in-progress cast commands for unit-targeted spells, excluding E and its empowered cast.
    - If Evelynn's last issued order was to cast E, her transition into the stealth will be followed by an automatic command for casting the ability (if no other orders were issued in the meantime).
- Whenever Demon Shade activates, a puff of purple smoke will briefly appear around her.
- Using a basic attack breaks the stealth at the start of the attack windup.

Active: Evelynn marks the target enemy champion or medium or large monster with a curse for 5 seconds.

Evelynn's next basic attack or ability hit against the target consumes the mark, slowing them by 45% for 0.75 seconds and refunding Allure's mana cost. If the target was marked for at least 2.5 seconds, the consumption is empowered.

- **Mechanics:** slow (0.75 s, 45%)

Empowered Bonus: The target is charmed for a few seconds and the slow duration is increased to the same period. Additional effects are also applied based on the target type: * Against champions, inflicts a magic resistance reduction for 4 seconds. * Against monsters, the triggering effect deals bonus magic damage and the duration of the charm is increased by 2.25 seconds.

- **Charm Duration:** 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2 / 2.25 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 2.25 s
- **Magic Resistance Reduction:** 35 / 37.5 / 40 / 42.5 / 45% → at max rank, level 20: 45%
- **Bonus Magic Damage:** 250 / 300 / 350 / 400 / 450 (+ 60% AP) → at max rank, level 20: 450 (+ 60% AP)
- **Monster Charm Duration:** 3.5 / 3.75 / 4 / 4.25 / 4.5 seconds → at max rank, level 20: 4.5 s
- **Mechanics:** charm (2.25 s); slow; charm (2.25 s)

Casting Allure does not break Demon Shade nor its fade into it.

Notes:

- The mark fully forming signifies the 2.5 seconds have elapsed, meaning the charm will be applied on the triggering attack or ability.
    - An enemy champion cursed by Allure is alarmed of her presence and after the mark has fully formed, an arrow will appear next to them pointing towards Evelynn's direction. This is visible to both teams.
- The magic resistance reduction will be applied to the attack or ability that triggers it.
- Allure's mark application on a target cannot be blocked by spell shield.
    - The mark's consumption will not occur if the ability is blocked by a spell shield, causing the mark to remain on the target.
- The magic resistance reduction debuff is applied independently of the Charm debuff when the matured mark is expunged, meaning a target can still have their magic resistance reduced while they are resisting applications of CC debuffs (e.g. Black Shield).
- If the target becomes untargetable, dies, or is too far away or no longer in sight during the cast time, this ability will cancel but still go on cooldown and pay its cost.
    - Allure will restart its cooldown whenever the mark debuff is removed.

### Assumptions

- The duel starts with no prepared Demon Shade, empowered Whiplash, Hate Spike mark or Allure. Demon Shade first arms after four seconds without a breaking action. Regeneration is evaluated each tick against the dynamic level-20 630 + 250% AP ceiling (the repository extrapolates the pinned level curve through level 20); health above that ceiling is never removed. Camouflage uses the actual observing champion and the pinned 700-unit detection radius.
- Hate Spike recasts use the pinned game-file decelerating missile (3000 initial speed, -4200 acceleration, 800 floor), strike all actual contacts, and consume only marks on the contacted target. The initial dart does not consume the mark it creates. The one-dimensional policy prioritizes a visible champion in recast range, then a visible damageable pet; champion-only unit-targeted spells cannot be manually retargeted to pets by the current command interface.
- Allure is applied at cast completion without a damaging projectile. The raw pin specifies the 0.25-second cast but no mark-delivery travel duration. Its mature MR reduction precedes the triggering packet independently of charm immunity. Mark removal restarts the pinned cooldown. Pets are not legal Allure targets unless their source explicitly classifies them as champions or qualifying monsters; this duel command interface targets the champion.
- Empowered Whiplash uses an interruptible target-tracking dash with earliest-tick arrival: neither the exact wiki pin nor the versioned binary quantifies dash speed. The binary's generic missileSpeed is not treated as a dash-speed claim. It updates the actual target location at arrival; a relocation of at least the source's estimated 2000 units instead ends at the prior location. Invalid targets during the cast cancel without cost/cooldown; invalid targets after commitment stop the dash without damage. Path pets receive area damage without primary-target on-hits. Normal Whiplash is dodgeable; empowered Whiplash ignores dodge; both ignore blind and respect attack blocks, parry and spell shields.
- Last Caress is a directed 500-unit interval on the one-dimensional line. It deals ordinary magic damage, with the 240% packet selected strictly below 30% health, never an execute. Its backwards blink follows the direction selected at declaration after the separate 0.5-second post-cast delay; untargetability and the movement lock end at that blink. The wiki's 500-unit radius is explicitly estimated. Terrain-specific blink relocation and lateral cone geometry are outside this model.
- The ordinary policy reserves offensive Last Caress in hold and short-trade modes, preserves completed-trade retreat, and waits stationary for Allure in hold mode. Shared reactive defenses may still answer a real incoming threat.
