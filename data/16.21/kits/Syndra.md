## Innate: Transcendent

Source: [Template:Data Syndra/Transcendent](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Syndra/Transcendent?oldid=4085617) (revision 4085617). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Syndra collects Splinters of Wrath that enhance each of her abilities based on the number of them collected, stacking up to 120 times. She gains Splinters of Wrath from the following: * Dealing two instances of ability damage to an enemy champion within 4 seconds grants 1 / 2 / 3 (based on level) Splinters. This cannot occur on the same target more than once every few seconds. * Ranking up an ability with any skill points beyond level 1 grants 5 Splinters, up to 85. * Killing a large minion grants 1 Splinter.

Collecting a Splinter of Wrath from an enemy restores 20 – 199 (based on level) mana. Splinters of Wrath are brought to Syndra from enemies over 0.6 seconds.

At 120 Splinters of Wrath, Syndra achieves Transcendence, increasing her ability power by 15%.

Syndra can collect Splinters of Wrath from enemies even if she is at maximum stacks. These Splinters do not grant any stacks, but will still restore mana for Syndra.

Notes:

- Syndra's abilities gain additional visual effects when they reach their Splinter of Wrath thresholds. Her spells will also gain a red colored tint outlining the VFX.
    - Dark Sphere will cause fractures to appear in the earth when it is used.
- The ability power increase at maximum Splinters stacks additively with other ability power multipliers.

### Assumptions

- Explicit Splinters beyond maximum-rank skill progression: 0.
- Max-ranked Q5/W5/E5/R3 grants 85 Splinters from 17 skill points beyond level 1; no large-minion or champion history is invented. additionalSplinters is an explicit 0–35 historical input. Q/W/E upgrades are therefore active at the ordinary 85 baseline; R execute needs 100 and 15% total AP needs 120. Enemy procs cap stack gain but still restore mana once per pickup. An actually killed explicitly classified large minion sends one Splinter through the same 0.6-second pickup and once-per-pickup mana restoration, rather than granting an immediate stack. Exact level 20 game ManaPerProc is 239, not three times 239 for a three-stack proc.
- Passive two-instance tracking groups all packets belonging to one actual cast into one damage instance, including W magic/true, E cone/spheres and the whole R barrage; distinct Q casts qualify separately. The pinned sources do not settle multi-orb damage-instance grouping, so this conservative cast grouping is explicit. A qualifying proc uses actual positive HP/shield damage to an actual champion body; ordinary minion pets and attacks do not supply champion procs. Splinter pickup uses 0.6 seconds, independent of distance.
- Q uses primary 210 radius and 0.6-second delayed appearance, not binary 180 radius or metadata spellCastTime 0.375. Each real sphere persists 6 seconds without a count cap. The two-charge upgrade retains static 1.25-second cast spacing and 7-second recharge with purchased haste plus 30 Q-only haste. No unsourced third charge is enabled. Sphere creation is independent of whether its damage is dodged or shielded.
- W selects actual eligible sphere/minion pets within 925 of Syndra and 500 of the aimed point. The primary description prioritizes nearest units while its notes prioritize spheres; this kit explicitly uses sphere-first, then distance and stable id. Unclassified or epic monster bodies cannot be inferred from radius/name and are not invented. Pickup is not a cast activation, cleanses a real nonsphere before stasis, retains its identity/HP/outgoing missiles and attaches its actual position to Syndra. No favorable carrying offset is guessed. Expiry, owner death or resurrection drops the same body. Sphere duration refreshes only on pickup.
- Pinned W command 1450 missile metadata conflicts with its WCast movement 20 and supplies no trusted gameplay throw flight/arc clock. This model explicitly places the real held body at the selected reachable landing point on manual recast, with no invented travel speed; separate paid pickup, manual cast, real landing, sight/slow and post-effect cooldown remain. An endpoint inside terrain is clipped to a near edge; the unknown lateral throw arc is outside scalar geometry. W uses primary 9-second rank 5 cooldown/225 radius rather than binary 8/195. Its true packet is full magic base times(12% + 2% per 100 AP), preserving nonlinear AP squared rather than the malformed parsed linear expression. Blue/Red monster mechanics need actual classified monsters.
- E uses primary 700 hostile range, 800 sphere acquisition, 950 sphere push capped 1200 from Syndra at contact and 100 sphere hit radius. The 1D axis cannot model 56/84-degree lateral cone coverage; both sourced angles remain evidence. Visible 2500 wave can damage, while 1600/1100 late waves only acquire late spheres. Every sphere has its own 2000 speed flight, wall interaction, delayed expiry and swept contacts. One E cast grants each victim damage once across cone/spheres while later sphere control remains possible. Unknown body knockback speed is represented by immediate sourced displacement constrained by terrain, omitting a temporal airborne travel duration. The upgrade slow starts after actual stun or instantaneous displacement; denied control does not invent a stun duration.
- R commits at declaration with paid mana/cooldown and schedules exactly 3 innate plus up to 4 actual eligible ground spheres within 3000; held/moving spheres are excluded. Every orb has its own accelerating projectile, spell shield, parry and target-invalidity state, then current-position landing and 6-second life. RSpell supplies initial 1, acceleration 7500 and cap 10000; release staggering is unsourced, so the model explicitly releases one orb per 30 Hz simulation tick as an approximation, including after caster death/resurrection. Target death stops unlaunched orbs, refreshing their actual ground bodies; launched orbs continue. Untargetability invalidates effects without deleting the body. Only positive R-orb damage can trigger strict below 15% execute at 100 stacks.
- Reference allocation prices sustained Q with Q-only haste, one actual sphere-supported W per cycle, one shared E damage per cycle and 3+min(4,floor(6/Q recharge)) sustain-available R spheres. It assumes no free opening W ammunition, fabricated minions/allies, guaranteed sphere-ray stuns or R executes. Scenarios still require mana, real ground spheres, contacts and legal mode policy; hold/trade reserve R and completed trades retreat.
