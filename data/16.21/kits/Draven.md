## Innate: League of Draven

Source: [Template:Data Draven/League of Draven](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Draven/League_of_Draven?oldid=4040578) (revision 4040578). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self · Damage type: – · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: – · Parry: – · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: When Draven catches a Spinning Axe, kills a non-champion, or destroys a turret, he gains a stack of Adoration.

When Draven kills an enemy champion, he consumes all of his Adoration stacks and gains 25 + (2 × stacks) bonus gold.

Draven loses 50% of his Adoration stacks upon death.

Notes:

- Upon reaching 150 Adoration stacks, and every 100 stacks thereafter, as well as when he cashes at least 150 stacks in, Draven automatically sends a chat message notifying all players.
- A chat message will display to all players whenever:
    - League of Draven grants over 300 gold.
    - League of Draven gets reduced.
- None of the parts of League of Draven require actually using a Spinning Axe for a kill to get Adoration stacks, as well as to cash Adoration stacks.
- League of Draven will trigger upon receiving an assist on an enemy executed by Death from Below.
- When receiving gold from an ally executing a minion through Shared Riches (World Atlas, Runic Compass), Draven is also awarded a Strike stack.
    - A Strike stack lasts up to 10 seconds or until Draven fails to catch an axe or a nearby minion dies without him killing it. Upon reacing 6 stacks, Draven consumes the stacks to gain 2 stacks of Adoration.
    - Other sources of gaining Strike stacks were removed in patch V25.06.

## Q: Spinning Axe

Source: [Template:Data Draven/Spinning Axe](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Draven/Spinning_Axe?oldid=3997560) (revision 3997560). Ranks: 5.

Targeting: Auto · Affects: Self · Damage type: Physical · Spell shield blocks it: – · Projectile: special · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Active: Draven starts spinning his axe, empowering his next basic attack within 5.8 seconds to deal bonus physical damage.

- **Bonus Physical Damage:** 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 / 60 (+ 75 / 85 / 95 / 105 / 115% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 60 (+ 115% bonusAD)

Upon hitting the target, the axe then ricochets off of them to land after roughly 1.4 seconds, with the location being determined by Draven's current movement. If Draven catches the axe, he gains Spinning Axe's empowered attack again.

Draven can hold up to two Spinning Axes at once.

Notes:

- The landing location is determined by Draven's movement speed and direction at the time Spinning Axe hits the target. If Draven is actually moving, it will bounce directly into his path. Otherwise, it will bounce back towards his current location, either landing directly onto him or to his sides.
- Spinning Axe will place a visual cue on the ground where it will land. This visual cue is visible to both teams, although Draven himself will see a more prominent marker.
- Axes take roughly 1.35-1.40s to go from target to landing point. This is not affected by attack speed, movement speed or level.
- Recasting Spinning Axe will refresh the buff and add another axe if possible.
    - There is no limit to how many axes can be juggled at once, just the limit of 2 of them at a time in Draven's hands.
- Draven used to be able to catch (steal) an enemy Dravens axes. This event also prompted a few unique voice lines.
- Spinning Axes may optically bypass Wind Wall and Blade Whirl but they will be destroyed upon collision. Unbreakable will not prevent the axe from bouncing off when Braum is the target.
- Unbreakable will not prevent the axe from bouncing off when Braum is not the target.
- The axe will still bounce against parrying effects.
- Runaan's Hurricane Wind's Fury does not interact with the effects of the axes.
- If a Spinning Axe is readied during a basic attack wind-up, the effects will be applied to the fired attack.
- Spinning Axe attacks deal their total increased damage in a single instance of basic damage.
- Spinning Axe may fail to properly bounce back if it hits Zaahen during or shortly after Cultivation of War revive.
- The empowered attack will not trigger against structures.
    - The duration will refresh on-attack against them.
- Spinning Axe has an indicator that appears on the ground for Draven to stand on. Some skins have a unique indicator.

### Assumptions

- Adoration stacks before the duel: 0.
- Spinning Axe starts unprimed. Q can ready up to two held axes and refreshes their shared 5.8-second lifetime; an attack consumes one at launch, so Q during a retained windup empowers that fired attack. Its 60 + 115% bonus AD is a non-critical addition inside the same ordinary physical basic packet. The separately sourced 1700 missile speed is used for an empowered axe; the game also contains older 1600-speed attack-animation wrappers.
- Each axe contact schedules an independently catchable landing 1.4 seconds later, following the pinned wiki over the conflicting 1.2-second raw return-missile field. Stationary Draven's landing is his current center; while actually moving it is one 1.4-second movement projection ahead. Catching requires his gameplay hitbox to cover that center at the landing tick. The source does not pin a catch radius or precise landing projection; this is an explicit one-dimensional approximation, and the policy does not leave a safe attack position to optimize catches. Parry still returns the axe; blindness/dodge conservatively lose it. Projectile-wall destruction creates no landing. Movement changes, immobilization and displacement can therefore make a catch fail.
- Blood Rush grants the sourced 40% attack speed for three seconds. Its 70% movement-speed bonus decays linearly to zero over 1.5 seconds and grants ghosting for that duration. The precise decay curve is not specified by the wiki; the client Temp_MSDecay is not used to assert server script timing. Successful catches reset its entire cooldown, even when the held-axe limit prevents another held empowerment.
- Adoration begins at the explicit configurable stack count, default zero, gains one per successful catch and per actual enemy-summon kill, and cashes out only on a champion kill. Bonus gold is recorded without buying stats mid-duel. Its death loss is modeled at the lethal notification. Shared Riches, lane minions, turrets and team assists do not exist in this arena.
- Stand Aside's sourced half-second sideways displacement becomes half a second of airborne interruption with zero longitudinal travel in the one-axis arena. Its two-second slow, spell shield, parry and displacement-immunity guards remain independent. No unsourced sideways distance or wall crossing is invented.
- Whirling Death travels the pinned finite 25000-unit global range, pierces health-based enemy summons, and reverses on geometric champion contact, including a spell-shielded contact, or range/recast. The axes decelerate at the game's 4500 units/s² on the original line, then accelerate at that rate up to 2000 while following Draven's current position. Thirty-Hz integration rounds this turnaround rather than reproducing sub-tick server motion. Damage drops multiplicatively by 5% per contacted unit to a 50% floor, independently resets for the return pass, and each target can be hit once per pass. Ordinary sight/minimap information has no fog-of-war effect.
- The policy primes Q before an available basic attack, refreshes Blood Rush while fighting, and preserves default defensive responses. The sustained reference values the lesser of attack speed and two continuously juggled axes per attack-flight-plus-return cycle, capped bonus attack speed at full catch-supported uptime, and two complete ultimate passes per cooldown. It assumes successful catches and target occupancy as a stat valuation, not a guarantee of the actual simulated result.
