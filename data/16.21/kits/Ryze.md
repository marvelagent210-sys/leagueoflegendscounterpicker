### Assumptions

- Arcane Mastery increases actual Mana maximum by0.1%of derived AP per AP, including AP buffs. The increase is bonus mana and feeds all three basic-spell ratios. Full converted mana initializes once, never once per tick; subsequent AP changes do not refill spent mana. Ryze has no intrinsic mana-to-AP conversion, and absent item recursion is not fabricated. Every basic spell is rank5 and Realm Warp rank3.
- Successful W/E completions reset Q and refresh one of at most two four-second Runes; free unit-target cancellation does neither. Q consumes all live Runes when its0.25second cast completes and grants 50% movement for 2 seconds only with two. Rune/reset/consumption placement inside the cast is unspecified by the primary and uses this explicit completion policy. Q retains declaration origin/facing,1000center range,110full width and1700speed; its first actual body receives single-target damage and pet collision is not duplicated. Decisive hits retain ordinary finite branching. Optional command buffering is represented by the first legal simulator decision tick, without a fabricated predeclared player input queue.
- Flux is target-owned and source-specific for four seconds, including real damageable pets. Accepted Q consumes that body's Flux, multiplies its packet by the rank3Realm Warp1.9factor and launches1500speed non-interceptable area packets only at actual nearby Fluxed bodies.
