## Innate: Dragon Practice

Source: [Template:Data Smolder/Dragon Practice](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Data_Smolder/Dragon_Practice?oldid=4078266) (revision 4078266). Ranks: 1.

Targeting: Passive · Affects: Self, Enemies · Damage type: Magic · Spell shield blocks it: true · Projectile: – · Parry: true · Disabled by grounded: – · Usable while knocked down: –

Innate: Smolder generates a stack of Dragon Practice each time he hits an enemy champion with an ability, or whenever Super Scorcher Breath kills a minion or monster. His basic abilities deal bonus magic damage based on stacks. * Q: Deals bonus magic damage equal to critical strike damage (based on critical strike chance) of Dragon Practice stacks. * W: Explosions deal bonus magic damage equal to 55% of Dragon Practice stacks. * E: Attacks each deal bonus magic damage equal to critical strike damage (based on critical strike chance) of Dragon Practice stacks.

Notes:

- Super Scorcher Breath grants a stack if the target dies while the initial missile is in flight.
    - This happens even if the target dies to Super Scorcher Breath burn, giving him a total of 2 stacks.
- Achooo! can only grant a stack of Dragon Practice with the first hit against each enemy champion.
- Flap, Flap, Flap can only grant one stack of Dragon Practice per cast.

If Super Scorcher Breath kills at least one target, Smolder restores 15 mana.

- **Physical Damage:** 60 / 70 / 80 / 90 / 100 (+ 130% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 100 (+ 130% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** on-hit; life steal; on-attack

Super Scorcher Breath becomes empowered with new effects based on the number of Dragon Practice stacks:

Tier 1 - 25 stacks: The fireball explodes upon collision to deal the same physical damage to nearby enemies. The explosion benefits from life steal at 50% effectiveness and applies on-hit effects.

- **Mechanics:** life steal; on-hit

Tier 2 - 125 stacks: The fireball sends forth 2 (+ 0.8 per 100 Dragon Practice stacks) bolts of fire in an arc upon collision that explode at maximum range, dealing 50% of the same damage to enemies hit. The bolts benefit from life steal at 50% effectiveness and apply on-hit effects.

- **Physical Damage:** 30 / 35 / 40 / 45 / 50 (+ 65% bonus AD) → at max rank, level 20: 50 (+ 65% bonusAD)
- **Mechanics:** life steal; on-hit

Tier 3 - 225 stacks: Enemies hit are set on fire for 3 seconds, causing them to take bonus true damage equal to 2.5% per 100 bonus AD (+ 0.5% per 100 Dragon Practice stacks) of their maximum health over the duration, capped at 300 against monsters. Subsequent applications of the burn stack, though not refreshing the duration of the previous burn. Enemy champions afflicted with the burn are also executed once Smolder deals damage to them that would leave them below 6.5% of their maximum health.

- **Mechanics:** execute

Notes:

- Any applied Dragon Practice damage is affected by the same damage modifiers as Super Scorcher Breath and does not benefit from the ability's life steal.
- Super Scorcher Breath's damage tags:
    - The fireball deals spell damage and procs basic damage required effects.
    - The explosion deals area damage.
    - The burn deals proc damage.
- Only Smolder's damage counts for triggering the execute.
    - If the target is below the threshold while afflicted with the burn, they will be executed on the next instance of damage dealt by Smolder, which includes the burn tick.
- If Super Scorcher Breath would damage the target to below the execution threshold, they are executed on the same damage instance.
- The number of additional bolts launched based on Dragon Practice stacks is rounded up.
    - At 125 Dragon Practice stacks, the fireball will launch 3 bolts.
- At 225 Dragon Practice stacks, the burn's damage based on the target's health ratio will increase by at least 1.125%.
- Super Scorcher Breath can only hit enemies once per cast.
- The minion and monster damage modifier applies only to the physical damage.
- Super Scorcher Breath's initial fireball, explosion and extra bolts can all be blocked and dodged.
    - The application of the burn is negated in all cases.

### Assumptions

- Dragon Practice stacks collected before the duel: 225.
- Dragon Practice defaults to 225 pre-duel stacks, an explicit changeable farming assumption that unlocks all three Q tiers. It gains one stack per real champion ability contact, with W and E limited to one per cast; ordinary attacks and generic summon damage do not grant hit stacks. Q kills on source-classified minion summons count as minion kills; champion clones and unknown classes do not. No free ambient farming occurs during the duel. The pinned wiki parser loses critical-damage template percentages in the passive text, so separately attributed game calculations supply Q magic 0.25×stacks×[1+1.4×critChance×(critDamage−1)], W magic 0.55×stacks, and E magic 0.08×stacks×[1+0.75×critChance×(critDamage−1)].
- Q uses the current rounded basic-attack windup and current edge attack range, travels as a 1800-speed targeted projectile, applies one full on-hit packet and 50% life steal only to its physical portion. Its physical critical modifier is 1+0.75×critChance×(critDamage−1); passive magic shares Q damage modifiers but grants no life steal. Block/dodge/spell shield deny the complete packet, including burn; blind does not stop this spell. Actual nearby summons receive the 285-radius tier-one explosion. Each actual target is contacted at most once per cast, even if a later bolt/explosion intersects it again. Q upgrades are fixed from the stack count at cast completion, while damage stats and passive stack damage are read at impact.
- At 125 stacks the extra arc has ceil(2+0.008×stacks) sourced bolts. The arena represents one forward 900-speed 500-unit corridor with a 150-radius terminal explosion and half physical/passive-magic damage, because it has no lateral coordinates. It neither multiplies damage by the number of bolts nor repeats damage on the primary target. Exact angular coverage, minion-primary targeted Q orders and cone-edge trajectories remain limitations. One cast refunds 15 mana at most once when it actually kills a target. If the initial target dies during flight, its pending Q gains the documented stack when that flight is discarded; extra bolts do not grant this special stack.
- At 225 stacks each unblocked Q contact creates an independent three-second true burn, ticking once per second for a total(0.00025×bonusAD+0.00005×DragonPractice)×targetMaxHP. New burns stack with separate clocks instead of refreshing old ones. The unused client Tier 3_TrueDamagePercent constant is not added to the explicit current burn calculation. A marked champion is executed below 6.5% max health only after positive damage dealt by Smolder himself, including Q's initial packet and burn ticks. Other actors cannot trigger it; shields and minimum-health floors retain their shared rules. The 300 monster cap does not apply to ordinary champion summons, which are modeled as minions.
- Achooo is a single piercing projectile, with 2000 initial speed,1600 units/s² deceleration and 400 minimum from the exact versioned movement component. This reaches the minimum after about 1200 traveled units; the wiki instead describes slowing after an estimated 1200 distance, an unresolved timing-wording difference. Champion contact creates a separately spell-shieldable 385-radius explosion even when the glob was shielded. Actual nearby summons receive the explosion; only one enemy champion exists, so no invented chain of extra 75%-falloff champion explosions is added. The initial glob has no passive magic; the explosion has it. The wiki's unspecified post-cast basic lockout is not assigned a guessed duration.
- Flight lasts 1.25 seconds, prevents ordinary attacks, grants 75% additive movement speed, ghosting and terrain-free voluntary walking, and fires 5+floor(stacks/100) homing 1800-speed spell bolts. The wiki gives 1800 speed, whereas the exact active EAttack movement component gives 2000 (its cosmetic missileSpeed field is 1800); this kit retains the wiki 1800 policy and discloses that unresolved conflict. Absolute evenly spaced shot clocks start immediately; the source does not specify their first phase. Each shot selects a currently visible champion within 700 center units, otherwise the lowest-health-percent eligible summon with deterministic nearest/id ties. Casting any other ability, any immobilization including root/stasis, or death ends flight; ground and silence alone do not. Flight ends inside generated terrain by snapping to the nearest legal one-dimensional edge. Unobstructed map vision, brush and full two-dimensional terrain choices are absent. A single spell shield blocks one bolt and flight grants only one champion-hit stack per cast.
- R retains the declaration position/direction and creates an independent wave from 600 units behind it. The client MomSpawnDelay is interpreted as one second from declaration; the source does not prove whether this delay is relative to declaration or cast completion. The 0.75-second cast is movable after an explicit first 0.4-second movement lock. The wave travels 1700 speed, heals only when it crosses Smolder's actual current position, and persists after his death. The one-axis aiming line models the 125-radius center sweetspot and its 150% damage/40% two-second slow; outer 300-radius weaker contacts cannot be represented independently by the current lateral sidestep model and remain an explicit limitation. Source-classified minion summons receive 50% damage; champion clones and unknown classes retain full damage and independent spell shields. Ordinary sight does not reveal invisible enemies.
- The policy finishes its finite flight instead of immediately canceling it with another offensive spell, otherwise follows ordinary defense and spell/attack choices. The budget prices current Q physical/passive magic, one independent burn at 225 stacks, glob plus one W explosion, complete flight at its sourced shot count and one R center wave/heal per cooldown. It uses the configured initial farm stacks, not speculative future stacking, free collateral targets or executions.
